## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Youth Summer Programs:** Safe recreation and summer park schedules for tract children.
    - **Sunnyvale Public Safety Department:** City overview of unified police and fire services protecting Lakewood Village.
    - **Mac's Hardware:** Garden supplies and exterior paints.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Editorial on Home Pride:** Encouraging ongoing yard maintenance and neighborhood attractiveness.
    - **Lakewood's Own Businesses:** Local service advertisements.
    - **Russell Congressional Campaign:** Political announcement.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Religious Directory:** Catholic catechism classes and Protestant services.
    - **Directory:** LVRA leadership contact list.
