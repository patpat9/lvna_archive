    A Time to be Thankful 
    from your President 
     
    I am so thankful for the generous 
    donations that our neighbor’s 
    have recently sent to the LVNA.  
    This will make a huge impact on 
    our events.   
     
    Our upcoming Lakewood Holiday Parade is on De-
    cember 3rd starting at 11:00am this will be our 19th year! 
    The parade route will start at Lakewood School, at the 
    corner of Lakechime and Meadowlake. The parade will go 
    down Meadowlake and turn left on Lakehaven, then contin-
    ue across Lawrence Expressway to Sandia. The route will 
    end at Fairwood Park.  
     
    So far we have had responses from Council Members, City 
    employee’s and community organiza-
    tions  
    and much more.  Come cheer on the 
    Fremont Band and the Lakewood Col-
    or Guard, see the beautiful classic cars 
    and Specialty Waste will have their 
    Antique Garbage truck! Again as in all 
    the previous years we will have FREE 
    refreshments at Fairwood Park. Bring 
    your camera, because we will have 
    Santa available for photos, while we 
    are serenaded by the Columbia Middle School Choir. 
     
     
     
    I am again asking you to come 
    forward and help out others who 
    are less fortunate than 
    ourselves.  Bring a new, 
    unwrapped toy to the parade. 
    The KLAS kids (Kids Learning 
    After School) will be collecting  
    the toys along the parade route.  
    If you cannot make the parade, 
    there will be a collection box at 
    both Fairwood School and 
    Lakewood School to drop off toys until December 
    16th. All the toys will be donated to the Sunnyvale 
    Community Center, where all the toys go to families in 
    Sunnyvale.  
     
    I have also invited the local newspaper, The 
    Sunnyvale Sun to cover this event.   We are the only 
    parade throughout Sunnyvale.  Let’s show the rest of 
    the City how it is done.  Thank you all and have a safe 
    and health holiday season! 

    The City of Sunnyvale is pleased to announce two upcoming

    Free Community Event - Las Posadas 
    Event: Las Posadas is a Christmas custom in 
    many Latin American countries which recreates 
    the procession of Mary and Joseph in search of 
    a shelter where Mary can give birth. In addition 
    to celebrating this event, there will be food and 
    music for participants. 
     
    When: Friday, December 9 
    Where: Columbia Neighborhood Center 
    Time: 6:30 pm – refreshments 
    7:30 pm – procession around the          
    neighborhood in search of shelter 
     
    Details:  Bring a warm coat, flashlight, and 
    umbrella in case it rains. 
     
    Participants are encouraged to bring some food 
    to share.

    Neighborhood Crime Corner 
     
    Lookout for This  Possible 
    Scam! 
     
     
    I received this letter from one of our neighbors: 
     
    A lady with a heavy accent and her ―daughter‖ came to 
    our door this afternoon – something about rug 
    cleaning.  They insisted they needed to come inside,  
    but no way would I let them in!  According to the 
    descriptions I have read about gypsy operations, I  
    think that this may be one.  Once they get inside, one  
    will distract you, while the other robs you.  To add insult  
    to injury, they said, ―Well, we’ll come back later when 
    your wife is home!‖  
     
    I forgot to ask them to show their Sunnyvale 
    permit.    
    The City of Sunnyvale requires permits for 
    door-to-door solicitors. The last I heard only 
    one valid permit has  
    been issued at this moment.  A permit 
    is not required  
    for youth groups, political 
    campaigners, and religious 
    groups.  The permit has the Sunnyvale 
    city seal on It.  
    (Here is an example of the logo-
    Editor) 
     
    I believe the local school guidelines  
    are that youths must be  
    accompanied by an Adult. 
     
    So beware, it sounds like a scam. They 
    didn’t hand  
    out their literature – they held tight onto 
    that.  They  
    just wanted to come in.   Beware!   
     
    They stopped at the houses of at least three 
    other neighbors, none of whom let them 
    in.  They implied to me that one of those 
    neighbors was a reference.   
    (That neighbor had shut the door on them!) 
    To keep Sunnyvale free of scams, it is 
    important to ask them  
    to show their permit and then report them to 
    Public Safety.  The non-emergency
