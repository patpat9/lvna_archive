    Las Madres Neighborhood Playgroups 
    Las Madres is a volunteer-run organization to support 
    and educate mothers of young children. I have been 
    involved with this group since we moved to Sunnyvale 
    in 2003, and it has been a wonderful experience for 
    both my daughter and me. We have made many great 
    friends and found a community at a time when we 
    didn’t know anyone here. If you are new to Sunnyvale 
    or have a small child and would like to meet other 
    mothers and caregivers and their children through park 
    days and play dates, I encourage you to visit their Web 
    site at www.lasmadres.org, or call 1-877-lasmadres.■ 
     
    Question Questionable Solicitors  
    Department of Public Safety Neighborhood Resource 
    Officer Greg Othon encourages residents to call DPS 
    (730-7100) to report solicitors who arouse suspicion. 
    Some solicitors have been associated with taking 
    advantage of the elderly and committing crimes when 
    people are away from their homes. The Sunnyvale 
    Municipal code requires some door-to-door solicitors 
    to obtain solicitation permits through the city.■ 
     
    January in Your Yard                
    and Garden 
    •Order now from seed catalogs. If you wait 
    too long, you may have to accept substitutions 
    later in the season. 
    •Order bare-root roses and fruit trees from specialty 
    nurseries. They will usually stop shipping bare-root 
    plants by the end of March. 
    •Move garden plants brought indoors for the holidays 
    back outside if they will not be subject to frost or snow. 
    •Make sure you have adequate drainage for heavy rain 
    storms. 
    •Constant dry heat from heating systems stresses indoor 
    plants. Check them for signs of yellowing or wilting; 
    they may need more water and an occasional misting. 
    Fertilize indoor flowering plants that are in bloom now. 
    •Watch out for frost. The weather is likely to be truly 
    cold this month. Be ready to cover susceptible plants or 
    to move them to a sheltered location. 
    •Prune roses, deciduous fruit trees, and ornamental 
    shrubs. Work from the inside of the plants toward the 
    outside. Remove diseased or dead branches as well as 
    branches crossing or rubbing each other. Then prune 
    the exterior for an attractive form. Prune springflowering trees and shrubs after they have bloomed. 
    •If desired, spray deciduous shrubs and fruit trees with 
    a dormant spray to kill overwintering insects. 
    •Shop for bare-root plants and plant them when the soil 
    is dry enough to dig properly. 
    •Look for colorful bedding plants such as calendulas 
    and chrysanthemums in the nursery to give an instant 
    color boost to the entryway or window boxes. 
    These tips are from the book Northern California Gardening: A Monthby-Month Guide, by Katherine Grace Endicott, published by Chronicle 
    Books, San Francisco, copyright 1996, and are used with the publisher’s 
    permission. ■

    LVNA Meetings 
    -Skip Gottschalk, LVNA Secretary 
    General Meeting 
    No General Meeting was held in December. 
    December 29 Board Meeting 
    Heidi Michaelis was introduced as the new Newsletter 
    Advertising Coordinator. Heidi grew up in Lakewood 
    Village and now has volunteered to support her 
    neighborhood. Thanks go to Heidi! 
    The Holiday Parade and Lighting Contest were 
    reviewed. Lessons learned were noted for next year’s 
    events. 
    The treasurer reported that the LVNA bank account 
    was switched from the Bank of Santa Clara to Union 
    Bank of California, because the Bank of Santa Clara 
    has started charging a $10/month fee on accounts 
    containing less than $3000. Union Bank of California 
    does not charge for checking accounts. 
    The LVNA bylaws were reviewed. Board members 
    can’t serve for longer than three years in a row, so 
    some positions will be open to new people in March. 
    Grant application paperwork was reviewed. The 
    president will handle the application. 
    Guest speakers for upcoming general meetings were 
    discussed. 
    LVNA Board elections will be held in March. Some 
    of the existing officers have reached their term limit. 
    If you are interested in serving on the LVNA Board, 
    please contact any of the current members (see back 
    page).■

    I am so angry right now I can see

    responsible and pick up after your

    the two ladies who walk their small

    But … Vandals Ruined One Display 
    The lighting display of a 2004 unit winner was 
    vandalized this year, in mid-December. Among other 
    mean-spirited actions, the perpetrators slashed inflated 
    decoration items, cut light strings, stole support 
    hooks, and knocked over holiday figures. They also 
    took seasonal items from the yard next door and threw 
    them into the ruined lighting display. 
    The homeowners filed a police report. They say that 
    after 30 years of putting up displays for all the major 
    holidays, they will no longer do so. 
    If you have any information about this vandalism or 
    other such incidents, please call the Sunnyvale 
    Department of Public Safety at 408-730-7100.■

    Toy Drive Yields Joy for Families 
    Judy Fernandez 
    Thank you to all the neighbors who participated in 
    our Holiday Parade toy drive. We collected over $175 
    worth of toys, which ranged from infant to teens. 
    They were donated to Sunnyvale Community 
    Services. This non-profit agency sets up a Christmas 
    store for needy families in Sunnyvale, who can select 
    food, clothing, and toys. It is a great service and 
    thanks to our neighborhood we made many families 
    happy this year. Sunnyvale Community Services 
    receives donations year round, so if anyone is looking 
    for a worthwhile charity, this is it―and it all stays in 
    Sunnyvale.■ 
     
     
     
     
     
    Need Help with Eating Problems? 
    Food Addicts in Recovery Anonymous (FA) is a 
    program for individuals recovering from addictive 
    eating and anorexia; it is based on the 12 steps of 
    Alcoholics Anonymous. There are no dues or fees at 
    meetings, and there are no weigh-ins. Contact the FA 
    office for more information at 781-321-9118, or visit 
    their Web site at www.foodaddicts.org. 
    The following meetings are held in this area every 
    week: 
    Monday: Cupertino, 7 p.m., St. Jude's Episcopal 
    Church, Rm. 2, 20920 McClellan Rd. across Stelling  
    Wednesday and Friday mornings: Santa Clara, 7:30 
    a.m., St. Justin's, 2655 Homestead Rd. Back Bldg. 
    Rm. 2 
    Sunday: Palo Alto, 7 p.m., St. Mark's Episcopal 
    Church, 600 Colorado St.■

    Sign-Ups

    Leagues in Sunnyvale. The league offers girls ages 516 recreational softball at various Sunnyvale parks.
