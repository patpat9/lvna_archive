    1992 Holiday Events A Huge Success
    
    As anyone who attended knows, the Lakewood Village Hol-
    iday Parade on December 19 was great fun. Residents lined the
    streets from Fairwood School to Lakewood School to cheer on
    the festive parade participants. Among those who braved the
    chilly morning air to show their Christmas colors were the Lake-
    wood Girl Scouts, Brownies, and Cub Scouts, the Fil-Am Asso-
    ciation, the Local #32 Chapter of A.B.A.T.E. (See the December
    Villager), and of course the jolly man in the red suit.
    
    After the parade, there was square dancing, music, and re-
    freshments, and a chance for the kids to visit with Santa Claus.
    The L.V.N.A. also collected $500 worth of toys to donate to
    Sunnyvale Community Services.
    
    Three days before the parade, Lakewood hosted another hol-
    iday activity—the annual lighting contest. There were five win-
    ners, and dozens of spectacular lighting displays. Prizes will be
    awarded at the January meeting to the following winners:

    Letter from the President
    
    Happy New Year! With the new year, L.V.N.A. is
    looking for new board members. The jobs of
    president, vice president, secretary, and
    treasurer will be up for election. Everyone
    should think about running for office.
    
    This year will be an internal building year
    for L.V.N.A. We need to strengthen the
    organization and carry on the new traditions
    which were established this year, such as the
    spring and fall B.B.Q.s, dumpster cleanup day,
    summer events for the kids, and the holiday
    parade and lighting contest. Also, monthly
    meetings and newsletters must continue to
    provide much needed communication to Lakewood.
    
    You may be asking yourself, "Do I have what it
    takes to lead Lakewood into another successful
    year?" Just keep in mind that the primary goal
    of the L.V.N.A. is "Enhancing the image of
    Lakewood Village in a positive manner." You
    too can do it.
    
    Another question is "Do I have the time?"
    Well, all of this year's board members have
    also worked full time. Just a little time in
    the evenings and a few lunch hour phonecalls
    plus some dedication is all it takes.

    Announcements
    
    Lakewood has a new Neighborhood Resource Officer. As of
    January 28, Tom Hoppin will be transferring, and Jeremy Wat-
    ney will take his place. Neighbor disputes and similar matters
    can then be referred to Officer Watney at 730-7146.
    
    Recently there has been a reappearance of graffiti here in Lake-
    wood. Residents are encouraged to paint over graffiti as soon as
    it appears. Also, report any leads on suspects to Public Safety at
    730-7100 or the Neighborhood Resource Officer at 730-7146.
    
    After the Holiday Parade on December 19, the parade committee
    was unable to locate 23 of the red bows which decorated the pa-
    rade route. If you found one or more of these bows, please return
    them to Vicki (730-8274), so we can use them next year.
    
    Nominations for the L.V.N.A. board will be held at the Febru-
    ary mecting. The offices of president, vice president, secretary,
    and treasurer are all opening. Start thinking of your nominees.
    
    The Lakewood Village Brownie and Girl Scout troops have al-
    ready begun their famous cookie sales. Cookies are $3 a box.
    
    If you are interested in networking with other out of work Lake-
    wood residents, contact David Jenkins at 749-1480.
    
    Master and amateur gardeners are invited to help plan for and
    plant a community garden. To get involved now, contact Char-
    line Jolly at 732-6504.
    
    There are three young (3-foot-tall) palm trees at 1125 Prescott
    which the owner wants removed. Just dig them up, and they're
    yours! Call Louise at 737-1133 for more information.
    
    Newsletter Contributions
    
    All information in the Villager is contributed by residents of
    Lakewood. If you have an article, announcement, or request for
    information which you would like published, contact newsletter
    editor Linda Cheung at 734-1661.
    
    The deadline for most months' newsletters is the first day of
    the month. Distribution is on the weekend before the third Thurs-
    day of the month. We will attempt to to print all submissions as
    space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any
    questions or concerns about the newsletter.

    Partners is over and we have also finished with the master
    planning of Fairwood Park. Future involvement with the city

    committees for Fairwood Park, a teen center, and general
    communication.
    
    Community gardens will be new this year and we will be
    working more on a community partnership with the school.
    Hopefully we will begin publishing the Villager in other
    languages and our unemployed networking group will take off.
    We also look forward to new and different recreation classes
    offered by the city in our local parks.
    
    Let's work together to build L.V.N.A. bigger and better in '93!
    Vicki Piazza, L.V.N.A. President
