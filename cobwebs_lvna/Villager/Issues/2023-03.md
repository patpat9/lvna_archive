    Dear Lakewood Village residents,
    For those who have not yet seen our newsletter, welcome! My name is Lydia Fotiades, and I am the president 
    of the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association (LVNA). LVNA is a volunteer-led association to help bring 
    the neighborhood together, inform our neighbors about happenings in our area, and make improvements to 
    our neighborhood. Our monthly Villager newsletter is one way we help keep the neighborhood informed. Our 
    newsletter is typically distributed electronically, so if you’re not on our newsletter distribution list please sign up! 
    See back page for details.
    As president, my primary reason for joining the board was to bring people together, help our community get to 
    know each other, and enjoy fun neighborhood events. Seeing new people at our events is all it takes to put a 
    smile on my face. :) We hosted several events in 2022, including:
    •	
    Memorial Day Flag Scavenger Hunt in May: We hid flags in the neighborhood for everyone to find and 
    photograph; we even shared a map with hints! The teams who found the most flags won gift cards.
    •	
    Dumpster Day in July: Approximately 20 dumpsters are placed throughout the neighborhood and residents 
    can drop off their junk for free
    •	
    Block Party: We blocked off a section of Pecos Way for people to come out and meet their neighbors. It was a 
    huge hit, and we plan to host more of these this year.
    •	
    Holiday Lights Competition in December: We hosted the holiday lights competition on Nextdoor; see the 
    January 2023 Villager for the winners
    •	
    Monthly Meetings: We have finally returned to in-person meetings! Our guest speakers this year included 
    Mayor Larry Klein, Officer Chen from the Department of Public Safety, and representatives from Helping 
    Hands of Silicon Valley and Let Me Organize It. A few of our meetings were “Meet and Greets” to meet 
    neighbors, and we ended the year with a pizza party. 
    As I mentioned, we are a volunteer-led organization. We need volunteers to join our board - elections are April 12 
    at 7:00pm in the Lakewood Park Building! We also need volunteers to help with planning and organizing events, 
    such as neighborhood picnics, National Night Out, and scavenger hunts. We also have opportunities to help 
    on an “as needed” basis, with things like being a dumpster monitor on Dumpster Day to hanging fliers for the 
    holiday social. Any level of volunteering is welcome.
    Finally, there are some major changes coming to our neighborhood soon. The City is going to be renovating 
    Lakewood Park and discussions have begun regarding upgrading the Lakewood Shopping Center into a Village 
    Center. Please help us make sure that our voices are included in these discussions, so that we get the changes we 
    want. You can find more information about these projects here:
    If you have any ideas for what you’d like to see from LVNA - whether it’s an improvement to the community, a 
    new event, or suggestions about topics for our monthly meetings (new speakers, a more social component, etc.) 
    - please email us at Board@LVNASV.org. You can also reach me directly at President@LVNASV.org. Finally, a quick 
    reminder to join the LVNA group on Nextdoor if you are not already a member (instructions to join are included in 
    this Villager). Hope to see you at our next event!
    Your President,
    Lydia Fotiades

    Sunnyvale Equity in Education: Advocating for High School Students in North Sunnyvale 
    As you may know, most Sunnyvale public high school students attend high school as part of Fremont Union High School 
    District (FUHSD). All of the FUHSD high schools are located south of El Camino Real. The only public high school in 
    Sunnyvale that was north of El Camino Real, Sunnyvale High School, closed in 1981. A group of parents joined together 
    last year to advocate for equitable school access in the FUHSD. The group, Sunnyvale Equity in Education (SEE), is 
    advocating for our students in the short-term, mid-term, and long-term. SEE’s goals are:
    •	
    Short-term: Understand the burdens faced by students and families in North Sunnyvale since the closure of Sunnyvale 
    High (survey and this listserve are starting point); advocate for transportation solutions that address those burdens 
    (Sunnyvale city council study issue on shuttle & carpool options for FHS families)
    •	
    Mid-term: Elect a representative from North Sunnyvale to the Fremont Unified High School District Board
    •	
    Long-term: Advocate for bringing high school back to North Sunnyvale
    They are advocating at City Council meetings, FUHSD meetings, and more. To raise our voice, they need your help, 
    especially from minority families and families who are not native English speakers. For more information, please see their 
    Facebook page here (LVNASV.org/tiny/k8P4) or email sunnyvaleequityineducation@gmail.com

    Support Local Restaurants 
    Looking for a place to order lunch or dinner? Early in the pandemic, Mayor Klein started an effort to dine at one new 
    Sunnyvale restaurant each day and share his experience on Facebook. It grew into his Eat Sunnyvale project, complete 
    with its own website here: www.eatsunnyvale.com. Check it out for inspiration on new restaurants to try and ways to 
    support our local restaurant community. Note, similar information is also available on Mayor Klein’s Facebook page, but 
    the www.eatsunnyvale.com is accessible without needing a Facebook account.
    Find us on Nextdoor! 
    If you have not already joined the LVNA group on Nextdoor then we encourage you to do so. Nextdoor 
    allows separate settings for group posts so it’s an excellent way to get curated Lakewood Village-
    relevant content while filtering out the rest. Board members post reminders about upcoming events and 
    moderators ensure all activity in the group adheres to the group’s rules (e.g., content relevant to Lakewood 
    Village, no soliciting, etc.). Note that only Lakewood Village residents are permitted to join. To learn more 
    or to join, visit nextdoor.com/g/alya6yxap or scan the QR code to the right.
    LVNA Villager
    This is the Villager, Lakewood Village’s neighborhood newsletter. We’re currently in our 66th year of 
    publication! A new edition is published each month, via email, and contains information about upcoming 
    events and subjects relevant to our neighborhood. Since this is our once-per-year paper delivery, we 
    encourage you to sign up for the Villager mailing list at LVNASV.org/villager. Simply submit your name 
    and email address, then check your email and click the confirmation link in your inbox. That’s it! Our 
    guarantee is that it’s only one email per month (while subscribed); nobody likes junk-mail.
    Volunteering for the LVNA Board 
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new neighbors? Would you 
    be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends, and the next generation who’s currently 
    growing up right outside your door or even in your own backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President, 
    Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in April and a new board will begin. The board meets once 
    per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to holding neighborhood general meetings and a few seasonal events 
    each year. The level of commitment is up to each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is a one-year term (April 2023 - 
    March 2024). The only requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the neighborhood, and are able to serve the full 
    12 month term. Elections are April 12 at 7:00pm in the Lakewood Park Building. For more information, please email 
    board@lvnasv.org 
    Wanted - Villager Editor
    Want to help, but not interested in a board position? We are looking for a new Villager Editor, a position open to anyone 
    who is interested (no age requirement). The role consists of at least proof-reading the content and compiling it into a single 
    document. Previous editors have also helped write content, design a new layout, take photos, and send the Villagers out to 
    the subscribers each month.
    Want to partner with a family member or friend? Great! Are you a high school student interested in writing or media design 
    and trying to increase your community service hours or build a portfolio? This is your chance! 
    Any of these roles are a great opportunity to help actively drive change for our community and make a positive impact on 
    our neighborhood. If interested, please email the board at board@lvnasv.org and let them know you’re on board (yes, pun 
    fully intended)
