    Dear Lakewood Village Residents,
    For those who have not yet seen our newsletter, welcome! My name 
    is Lydia Fotiades, and I am the president of the Lakewood Village 
    Neighborhood Association (LVNA). LVNA is a volunteer-led association 
    to help bring the neighborhood together, inform our neighbors about 
    happenings in our area, and make improvements to our neighborhood. 
    This monthly Villager newsletter is one way we help keep the 
    neighborhood informed. Our newsletter is typically distributed via email, so 
    if you’re not on our newsletter distribution list, please sign up! See back of 
    flyer for details.
    As president, my primary reason for joining the board was to bring people 
    together, help our community get to know each other, and enjoy fun 
    neighborhood events. Seeing new people at our events is all it takes to put 
    a smile on my face. :) This year, we brought back some of our typical yearly 
    events:
    •	Dumpster Day in July: Approximately 20 dumpsters are placed 
    throughout the neighborhood for residents to drop off their junk for free
    •	Pumpkin Carving in October: It was “BYOP” (“Bring Your Own 
    Pumpkin”) - LVNA provided carving supplies and patterns, prizes, snacks, 
    and a backdrop for photos
    •	Holiday Social in December: Santa, games, prizes, cookies, hot 
    chocolate and cider
    •	Monthly Meetings: Currently held virtually, these meetings feature 
    guest speakers and cover updates and/or changes to our neighborhood and the rest of Sunnyvale. Recent speakers have 
    included Omar Din (our Sunnyvale City Council representative), a speaker from Sunnyvale Urban Forest Advocates, and 
    Andrea Urton (CEO of HomeFirst, which helps support unhoused people in our community)
    As I mentioned, we are a volunteer-led organization for the entire Lakewood Village neighborhood (which encompasses both 
    the Lakewood Park and Fairwood Park sides of Lawrence Expy.), and there are many ways to get involved to support LVNA 
    and our community. We need volunteers to join our board - elections are coming up in March! There are also opportunities 
    to volunteer on a more “as needed” basis. These volunteers support many of the events listed above, and can do anything 
    from being a dumpster monitor on Dumpster Day to hanging fliers for the holiday social to organizing the neighborhood 
    picnic (which we hope to bring back this year). Any level of volunteering is welcome, even if all you can say now is “I might be 
    able to help but I’m not sure, email me when the time comes.”
    If you have any ideas for what you’d like to see from LVNA - whether it’s an improvement to the community, a new event, 
    or suggestions about topics for our monthly meetings (new speakers, a more social component, etc.) - please email us at 
    Board@LVNASV.org. You can also reach me directly at President@LVNASV.org. Finally, a quick reminder to join the LVNA 
    group on Nextdoor if you are not already a member (instructions to join are included in this Villager). Hope to see you at our 
    next event!
    Your LVNA President,
    Lydia Fotiades

    Monthly LVNA Meetings 
    LVNA meetings are held at 7:00 PM on the 2nd Wednesday of each month. These typically feature a 
    guest speaker discussing topics pertaining to Lakewood Village residents (see Lydia’s letter on page 
    1 for examples of recent speakers). Upcoming meeting dates are listed on LVNASV.org/events, with 
    instructions for how to attend virtually (and/or the event location, when we return to in-person).
    Upcoming Meeting Dates for 2022
    Find us on Nextdoor! 
    If you have not already joined the LVNA group on Nextdoor then we encourage you to do so. Nextdoor 
    allows separate settings for group posts so it’s an excellent way to get curated Lakewood Village-
    relevant content while filtering out the rest. Board members post reminders about upcoming events 
    and moderators ensure all activity in the group adheres to the group’s rules (e.g., content relevant to 
    Lakewood Village, no soliciting, etc.). Note that only Lakewood Village residents are permitted to join. To 
    learn more or to join, visit nextdoor.com/g/alya6yxap or scan the QR code to the right.
    LVNA Board Elections
    LVNA board member elections are on March 9th. Are you passionate about our neighborhood? Do you have big ideas 
    and/or skills to bring to the table? You should consider running for one of the five board positions next month. We are 
    also looking for a new Villager editor (or co-editors). Please contact any of the current board members with your notice of 
    interest. Visit LVNASV.org/contact to send us a message.
    LVNA Villager
    This is the Villager, Lakewood Village’s neighborhood newsletter. We’re currently in our 65th year of 
    publication! A new edition is published each month, via email, and contains information about upcoming 
    events and subjects relevant to our neighborhood. Since this is our once-per-year paper delivery, we 
    encourage you to sign up for the Villager mailing list at LVNASV.org/villager. Simply submit your name 
    and email address, then check your email and click the confirmation link in your inbox. That’s it! Our 
    guarantee is that it’s only one email per month (while subscribed); nobody likes junk-mail.
    Support Local Restaurants 
    Looking for a place to order lunch or dinner? Early in the pandemic, Mayor Klein started an effort to dine at one new 
    Sunnyvale restaurant each day and share his experience on Facebook. It grew into his Eat Sunnyvale project, complete 
    with its own website here: www.eatsunnyvale.com. Check it out for inspiration on new restaurants to try and ways to 
    support our local restaurant community. Note, similar information is also available on Mayor Klein’s Facebook page, but 
    the www.eatsunnyvale.com is accessible without needing a Facebook account.
