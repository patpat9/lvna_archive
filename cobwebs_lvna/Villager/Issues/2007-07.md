    President’s Column 
    -Michael Majchrowicz 
    The Dumpster Day legacy continues! Dumpster Day 
    2007, on June 30, went very smoothly, and I thank 
    everyone in the neighborhood for your cooperation in 
    dumping responsibly and for the long hours put in by our 
    many dumpster monitors. I hope everyone took the 
    opportunity to meet neighbors and spend time talking 
    with each other. 
    Once again, our neighborhood benefited from the 
    principle that one person’s trash is another person’s 
    gold. Almost $400 was raised at the parking lot sale, 
    where items still in good condition were sold. This 
    money helps LVNA put on the great events that we 
    enjoy. 
    I look forward to seeing many of you at the July 11 
    meeting, where we will hear an update by our 
    Neighborhood Resource Officer, Jim Davis. We will 
    also be discussing issues such as the use and abuse of the 
    parks and how that is affecting those who live nearby, 
    lighting on the park bike path, neighborhood trees, and 
    any other concerns you have. 
    These issues were raised at our last few meetings, and 
    for those of you who haven’t attended the meetings, try 
    to make time. Your input matters in improving this 
    neighborhood. Nothing changes unless it is addressed.

    Summer Lunch Program for Kids 
    Lakewood School is again offering their FREE lunch 
    program for children through July 27. The free lunch 
    starts at 10:45 a.m. and ends at 11:15 a.m. Any child 
    under 18 years old can get these lunches, which are 
    funded through a federal grant. 
    Lakewood School is at 750 Lakechime Drive. ■

    Villager Walkers Needed for Lakewood Side 
    Wendy Stanley is looking for walkers for two different 
    Villager delivery routes. One route involves 60 homes 
    and goes from Lawrence halfway down Lakebird. The 
    other route is a long one, 101 homes, and could be split 
    into two routes if the whole thing seems too much for 
    one person. This route involves all of Lakehaven. If you 
    want to get some exercise by delivering Villagers on 
    either route, please call Wendy at 745-1331. ■
