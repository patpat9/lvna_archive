    Mr. RogerstNeighborhoodi Mar Junge One of the best things about Lakewood Village is the stability of the neighborhood. I've lived here almost twenty years and most of my neighbors have lived here even longer. We have many friends in The Village who bought the house they grew up in and are now raising their own children here. The school's director, Lupita Montoya, has lived in Lakewood Village all her life. She began teaching at the preschool shortly after it opened in 1983. After teaching there for I I years, Lupita saved up enough money to buy the school and has been its director since 1992. Her younger sister, Carla, now a sophomore at Fremont High School, was one of the first to graduate from Fun Learning and is also a Lakewood resident. 'I still live around the corner from my preschool, and many of my students are also my neighbors. Quite a few of our graduates still live in the neighborhood too, so we often have older children visiting us in the afternoons, just to say hello," Lupita said, "Growing up and going to school in the same neighborhood gives kids a sense of security. They're more eager to learn and aren't afraid to try new activities." One activity that the preschoolers love is visiting Lakewood Elementary School to watch music shows, plays, and other assemblies.
