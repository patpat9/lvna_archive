## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Guiding Neighborhood Youth:** Mentorship, community sports, and character-building to prevent delinquency.
    - **Mac's Hardware:** Tools and lawn supplies.
    - **The Stork Club:** Birth announcements welcoming new babies to Lakewood families.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Lakewood Barber Shop:** Expanded summer operating hours.
    - **Lakewood Baptist Church Construction:** Architectural sketches and building updates for new sanctuary.
    - **Community Notices:** Classifieds and local babysitters.
