    HELP & ASSISTANCE
    Thank You to all of the neighbors and friends who have 
    stepped up during these difficult times to provide 
    their help and support to others. We’ve seen the posts 
    on Nextdoor from those reaching out to complete 
    strangers, offering assistance with getting groceries, 
    providing personal protective equipment (PPE), and 
    even just being another person to talk to. You are a big 
    part of what makes this such a great community. 
    So, THANK YOU!
    Sunnyvale Community Services (SCS) is still open to 
    the Sunnyvale community to provide support and 
    emergency assistance to those facing financial hardship, 
    homelessness, and hunger. They have emergency food, 
    financial aid, and referral services available. If you or 
    someone in the community that you know is in need of 
    assistance, please contact SCS: 
    info@svcommunityservices.org or 408-738-4321.
    If you would like to help:
    •	 Consider donating to SCS’ relief fund: https://svcommunityservices.org/donate-online-2/   
    Your contribution helps staff meet the needs of those requiring financial assistance for paying 
    rent, utilities, medical costs, food, and other essential items.
    •	 Reach out to a neighbor or loved one who may be struggling. Even in difficult times, many 
    people may still feel uncomfortable asking for assistance. Lending a hand can help ease that 
    discomfort.
    One more shout-out to the neighborhood for doing such an excellent job at following the 
    shelter-in-place order and social-distancing recommendations. Those who cross the street to 
    maintain 6+ feet between others while on neighborhood walks haven’t gone unnoticed. Keep it 
    up!

    RECIPE CORNER
    DEVILED EGGS
    Have a recipe that you love? 
    Share it with us at editor@lvnasv.org
    02     LVNA VILLAGER | APR 2020
    1. Bring a pot of water to a boil. You want enough water to 
    completely submerge the eggs in a single layer. Do not put 
    the eggs in the water yet.
    2. Once water comes into rolling bowl, place eggs gently into 
    pot. Boil for 13 minutes. 
    2. When 13 minutes are up, immediately place eggs into an ice 
    bath (bowl filled with ice and water) for at least 5 minutes. 
    3. Peel eggs and slice them in half, lengthwise. Place egg yolks 
    into a mixing bowl and egg whites onto a plate. 
    4. Mash egg yolks into a fine crumble with a fork. Add mayo, 
    mustard, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
    6. Evenly distribute yolk mixture into egg white cavities. 
    	
    TIP: Transfer egg mixture into a sandwich bag and cut off a corner. 
    Squeeze mixture out into egg whites. 
    7. Optional: Sprinkle on paprika, green onion, and/or chives.
    6 eggs
    5 TSP mayo 
    1/2 TSP yellow mustard
    salt, to taste
    pepper, to taste
    green onions or chives, optional
    paprika, optional

    LVNA MEETING ON MAR 11
    Steve Sloan, Superintendent of Libraries, discussed 
    the new Lakewood Village library, set to open late 
    2023 to early 2024.
    Development started in 2012, with Lakewood Village 
    targeted as a prime location due to the lack of any 
    other branch within a two-mile radius. Additionally, 
    US-101 is a distinct barrier for many, making those 
    other locations a challenge to get to. These are likely 
    contributing factors for why Lakewood has a very low 
    percentage of library card holders, compared with 
    the rest of Sunnyvale.
    Many agencies have been involved since the 
    beginning, including the Sunnyvale School District 
    and Parks and Recreation, to ensure this facility 
    would meet the needs of the whole community. After 
    outreach, negotiations, and reviews, the council 
    approved a single-story, 20,000 sq. ft. facility, with 
    2,400 sq. ft. designated as program space, to be built 
    in the location of the existing Lakewood swimming 
    pool.
    The city is responsible for the design of the building, 
    bidding, and construction management, and will 
    lease the site from the Sunnyvale School District for 
    50 years, with the option of extending an additional 
    25 years for $1. Construction is on track to begin in 
    2022.
