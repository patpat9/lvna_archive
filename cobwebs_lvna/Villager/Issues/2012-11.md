    By Phyllis Fowler 
     (One of neighbor’s recently asked me 
    for the Corn Casserole recipe I shared 
    two years ago. Article from the 
    November 2010 newsletter.) 
     
    Now that Thanksgiving is almost here I 
    thought I would share one of my 
    favorite recipes.  A few years Fred and 
    I had my family over for Thanksgiving.  
    With all the cooking to do I wanted a 
    simple side dish that I could put together quickly and 
    easily.  I found this southern style recipe on 
    foodnetwork.com  by Paula Deen. It was a huge hit, 
    especially with the guys!  (We don’t need to tell them it 
    was so easy, just take the praise and let them feel 
    spoiled!) I will warn you that this is rich!  I left out the 
    Cheddar cheese and reduced the butter to a third of a 
    cup.  I also use fat free sour cream, to help bring down 
    the calories.  The guys never noticed and there rarely 
    are leftovers.  However, if you do have leftovers, it heats 
    up nicely in the microwave the next day.  I hope you 
    enjoy your holiday! 
     
    Corn Casserole 
     
    Recipe courtesy Paula Deen 
      
    Prep Time: 10 minutes  
    Cook Time: 55 min 
    Level: Really Easy   
     
    Serves 6 to 8 servings 
      
    Ingredients 
     
    1 (15 1/4-ounce) can whole kernel corn, drained 
    1 (14 3/4-ounce) can cream-style corn 
    1 (8-ounce) package corn muffin mix  
     
    (Jiffy cornbread mix is recommended) 
    1 cup sour cream 
    1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted 
    1 to 1 1/2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese 
     
    Directions 
     
    Preheat the oven to 350 degrees .  
     
    In a large bowl, stir together the 2 cans of corn,  
    corn muffin mix, sour cream, and melted butter. Pour into 
    a Greased 9” by 13” casserole dish. Bake for 45 minutes, 
    or until golden brown. Remove from oven and top with 
    Cheddar cheese. Return to oven for 5 to 10 minutes, or 
    until cheese is melted. Let stand for at least 5 minutes 
    and then serve warm. (Just don’t tell anyone how easy it 
    really is!)   
     
    Green Bean Casserole 
    Recipe courtesy of Robert Irvine 
     
    Total Time: 30 min 
    Prep time: 10 min 
    Cook time: 20 min 
    Level: Easy 
     
    Yields: 16 to 20 servings  
     
    Ingredients 
    2 pounds haricots verts (French thin green beans)  
    2 large red onions, finely diced  
    8 tablespoons (1/2 cup or 1 stick) butter, unsalted  
    2 (10-ounce) cans cream of mushroom soup  
    3 tomatoes, seeded and diced  
    4 cans French-fried onions  
    Salt and freshly ground black pepper 
     
    Directions 
    Blanch the haricots verts in hot water for about 3 to 5 
    minutes then remove from heat and shock in iced water. 
    Drain and set aside to cool.  
     
    Preheat oven to 325 degrees F.  
     
    In a large skillet sauté the chopped onion in butter. Then 
    add the cooled beans and sauté for a couple of minutes. 
    Add the canned mushroom soup and mix well.  
    Transfer to a buttered casserole dish and top with the 
    diced tomato, French-fried onions, and season with salt 
    and pepper, to taste.  
     
    Bake in oven for about 10 minutes. 
