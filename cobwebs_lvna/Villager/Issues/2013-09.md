    Covered California-Medical Insurance 
     
    Covered California Information Sessions at CNC 
     
    When: Evenings on Tuesdays:  
     
    Sept 17, Oct 15, Nov 19 
    Time: 6 – 7 pm 
     
    When: Mornings on Wednesdays: 
     
    Sept 25, Oct 23, Nov 27 
    Time: 10 – 11 am  
    Where: Columbia Neighborhood Center, 785 Morse Ave 
     
    Now that getting health insurance is a new national           
    requirement, the government has created a solution that 
    makes it easier and more accessible than ever before, it’s 
    called Covered California. It’s an online marketplace where 
    Californians can compare and choose health plans from   
    established health insurance companies. Covered California 
    is the only place where the federal government will provide 
    you financial help for health insurance, based on  income 
    household size. Whether you need general checkups,     
    medical help for a pre-existing condition or protection from 
    an unforeseen event, Covered California will let you choose 
    a plan that meets your needs and budget. 
     
    Individuals and families with income between 139% and 
    400% of the Federal Poverty Level should qualify for          
    assistance for insurance premiums or out of pocket           
    expenses, depending on specific requirements and income 
    level. 
     
    The Columbia Neighborhood Center has partnered with 
    the California School Health Centers Association to    
    provide information on Covered California, Specially trained 
    CNC staff will conduct monthly information sessions starting 
    in September. Each provider must have a uniform benefits 
    package with 10 essential health benefits, making it easier 
    for people to compare coverage and decide what will work 
    best for them and their family. 
     
    Enrollment in Covered California begins on October 1, 2013. 
    Attendees to these information sessions will receive         
    information on ways to sign up, including referrals to certified 
    enrollment counselors.  
     
    IMPORTANT: Enrollment in Covered California is FREE 
    and residents should report to Public Safety any phone 
    calls, email or letters from organizations that claim to 
    help with eligibility and registration for a fee. Scammers 
    have already started taking advantage of people who are 
    not informed about this program. 

    739 Morse Ave.  
     
    Fall-related injuries are of significant concern as we 
    age. In people aged 65 and older, one in three seniors 
    will experience a fall and for those aged 80 and older,

    Lakewood at its Best! 
     
    By Leah Lane 
     
    Let me tell you about my  
    neighbors on Socorro Ave.   
    Sandy Martinez was walking around her 
    block and saw an elderly lady struggling 
    to pull the many weeds in her front yard.   
     
    When Sandy got home, she told her neighbor Sharon 
    Stowe Godinez about the lady. Sharon told her husband 
    Enrique and her two sons Tony & Rick. Then Sharon, her 
    family, Sandy and her son Jessie loaded up a truck with 
    tools and yard waste cans and drove around the block and 
    spent the rest of their Saturday working in this lady’s yard 
    on Torrance Ave. 
     
    Although this sort of thing does not happen every day, it is 
    not all unusual in Lakewood Village. We wave to our  
    neighbors, watch out for each other and help out when we 
    can. Sharon and Sandy are second generation Lakewood 
    Villagers and are teaching their kids the same values they 
    were taught. This is an example of Lakewood Village at its 
    best.  Pass it on! 

    Walk N Wag 2013 
    Benefit for Humane Society  
    Silicon Valley 
    It’s that time of year again! The South Bay’s premier 
    dog walk, Walk N Wag, will be taking place October 5th 
    at Hellyer Park in San Jose. In addition to the 5K walk, 
    there will also be gourmet food trucks, agility and        
    detection dog demos, live family entertainment  
    (Wag N Walk Cont.) and a pet costume contest. There will 
    also be a shorter 3/4 mile lakeside walk option. Corporate 
    and private teams are currently forming to raise fund on 
    behalf of the walkers. Individual walkers are also welcome. 
    All funds benefit Humane Society Silicon Valley.            
    Information on forming a team, joining a team, or making a 
    donation can be found at www.hssv.org/walk. 
