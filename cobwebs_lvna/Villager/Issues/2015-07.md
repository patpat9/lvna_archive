    Greetings neighbors!   
    It is July again, and we all know what 
    that means:  Dumpster Day!  Mark 
    your calendars for Saturday, July 18th.  That will be the 
    day when we hold our annual neighborhood cleanup.   
     
    Once again, longtime Dumpster coordinator Skip 
    Gottschalk has lined up locations for the 19 debris boxes 
    we will have.  Skip has also recruited enough volunteers to 
    staff them all and keep inappropriate things out of them.  
    Thanks to Skip for doing this important work. 
     
    Specialty Solid Waste and Recycling will deliver the 
    dumpsters starting the afternoon of the 17th.  While the 
    event doesn't technically start until the morning of the 18th, 
    some volunteers may choose to open their dumpsters on 
    Friday afternoon to accommodate people who have to 
    work over the weekend.  Please see the map we've  
    included and check with the dumpster monitor for details. 
     
    This year we will once again be hosting Goodwill trailers 
    as part of our Dumpster Day event.  Goodwill will station 
    two 28 foot trailers in the neighborhood once more to  
    accept small donated items.  One will  
    be in the parking lot of the Fairwood 
    School at the corner of Sandia and  
    Fairwood Avenue.  The other will be in 
    the Lakewood School parking lot near 
    the baseball diamond along Lakechime 
    drive.  Last year we filled up both  
    trailers for a total of eight tons of  
    donated items.  I hope we do even better 
    this year. 
     
    One difference between Dumpster Day 
    this year and the event last year will be

    The LVNA Has A New Printer 
     
    By Fred Fowler 
     
    As you read this edition of The Villager, you may 
    notice that the type face looks crisper, 
    the colors brighter, and the overall 
    quality of the printing looks much better.  
    If you are wondering why, it is because 
    the LVNA has purchased a new  
    Konica-Minolta color printer! 
     
    Back in 2010, the neighborhood 
    association got a grant from the City of Sunnyvale to 
    acquire an HP 5500dn color LaserJet. We got the 
    grant because it was costing us about $425 per 
    month to have our 6 page black and white newsletter 
    printed by a local print shop.  Getting our own printer 
    let us print a color version of the publication for 
    roughly half the prior cost.  Over the past 5 years our 
    workhorse HP has paid for itself many times over and 
    delivered almost 60 editions of The Villager.

    The LVNA Has A New Printer 
     
    (Continued from page 1) 
     
    Unfortunately, our HP color printer has 
    gotten old.  It was a used machine when 
    we bought it and it is now more than 10 
    years since it was new. The machine 
    has been cranky lately, making it a  
    challenge to get the newsletter out on schedule. The time 
    finally came to put it out to pasture and get a new model.   
     
    We found a good deal on eBay for a used Konica-Minolta 
    Bizhub C353 color printer/scanner/copier. We took  
    delivery in the last week of June, and this is the first  
    edition of The Villager we've produced with it. Phyllis and 
    I are thrilled, partly because this new machine is so much 
    faster than the old one. It took the better part of 3 days to 
    print our newsletter on the old HP. It looks like our new 
    printer will do the job in about 8 hours. In addition, the 
    toner is cheaper too, saving the LVNA even more money 
    than the HP did. 
     
    If anyone wants our used HP 5500dn, 
    please contact me at 
    fmfainc@gmail.com. There is also a used 
    black and white Konica-Minolta Bizhub 
    printer we will be getting rid of. If someone 
    from the neighborhood is interested, 
    please let me know ASAP. If no one ex-
    presses interest, they will both go to Goodwill as part of 
    our Dumpster Day donation. 
     
    Please let me know. Thanks!

    Aggravated Assault –  
    700 Blk. N. Fair Oaks 5/26 @ 9:30 pm   
    Officers responded on a report of a large group fighting in a 
    parking lot. Upon arrival, the participants had fled the scene 
    but officers were able to stop some participants in the area. 
    It appeared to be a gang related incident in which bats and 
    tire irons were used as weapons. A subject with a significant 
    head injury was located at a local hospital later in the 
    evening. Detectives are continuing the investigation. .  
     
    Residential Burglary – 800 Blk. San Junipero 5/29 
    between11:20 – 11:30 am   
    Unknown suspects entered the residence via an unlocked 
    front door and stole electronics and jewelry. The suspects 
    fled when they were observed by a neighbor.  
     
    Vehicle Extrication – Lawrence & Tasman 6/1 @ 7:45 pm  
    DPS personnel responded on a collision involving a vehicle 
    that had rolled-over. Fire personnel stabilized the vehicle 
    and cut open the roof to extricate the occupant.  
     
    Hazardous Materials – 1300 Blk. Kifer 6/4 @ 1:37 pm   
    A transport driver reported a sound coming from a bucket of 
    lithium batteries he was transporting. The DPS Hazardous 
    Materials team responded and determined a chemical 
    reaction was occurring in the bucket and the chemicals were 
    venting. The Haz Mat team determined there was no risk to 
    the public, mitigated the hazard from the transport vehicle, 
    and contained it within the bucket of origin.   
     
    Robbery – 1063 Morse 6/6 @ 2:30 am   
    As the victims were getting out of their car in the parking lot 
    of the apartment complex, they were approached by an 
    unknown suspect who pointed a gun at them and demanded 
    jewelry and money. The suspect removed jewelry from the 
    victims and left the area on foot.  
     
    Aggravated Assault – 1200 Blk. Lawrence Station Rd 
    6/6 @.1:12 pm   
    Two suspects confronted the victim regarding an altercation 
    they were involved in a couple of years ago. The suspect 
    pulled out a knife and threatened to stab the victim. The 
    victim sprayed the suspect with mace and the suspect and 
    accomplice fled on foot. Officers located the suspect and 
    accomplice in the area and they were both arrested. 
     
    Residential Burglary – 1225 Vienna 6/11 @ 
    11:20 am  
    Unknown suspect entered a residence by 
    cutting the screen to a window that had been 
    left open. The suspect was confronted by a 
    resident who had been sleeping in a bedroom. 
    The suspect fled out the front door. It did not 
    appear anything was stolen from the residence.  
