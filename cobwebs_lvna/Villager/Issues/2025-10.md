    LVNA VILLAGER - OCTOBER 2025
    Picnic Recap: A Day of Fun, Food, and Community!
    Thank you to everyone who attended our neighborhood picnic and brought tasty treats to share. We
    also want to extend a special thanks to all of our awesome volunteers who helped make the event a
    success: Bev and Robin Woidy, Dhananjay Singh and his son, Linh Ly, Evelyn Profeta, Tony and Joanne
    Bugna, Quyen Fitting, Christy and Denny Majchrowicz, Keith Huie, Erika Cramer and her dad, and Rick,
    Rena and Blake Flovin.
    We were thrilled to have special guests, including Sunnyvale Mayor Larry Klein, City Manager Tim Kirby,
    City Council Representative Eileen Le, representatives from California State Assemblymember Patrick
    Ahrens' office, and representatives from Santa Clara County Supervisor Otto Lee’s office. A big hit
    with the kids was having Sunnyvale Public Safety Fire Fighters bring over a fire engine for everyone to
    check out!
    A huge shoutout goes to our Neighborhood Walmart store at the Mercado shopping center for their
    generous donation of all the hamburgers and a large supply of water. They are currently renovating
    their store and invite everyone to their grand reopening on December 19th at 9 AM. Please support the
    store if you can, as they have been and will continue to be a fantastic supporter of our neighborhood.
    
    LVNA VILLAGER - OCTOBER 2025
    900 Pounds of Trash Removed at Calabazas Creek Cleanup
    We had 49 volunteers come out to support the statewide coastal clean up initiative in our backyard on
    the Calabazas Creek Trail on Saturday September 20 including local girl and boy scout troops. We
    collected over 900 pounds of trash and helped to beautify our neighborhood and protect our
    waterways. A big shout out to all those who came out to help!

    Neighborhood Safety in Focus: Join Us for an Important
    Meeting
    The safety of our community is a top priority, and the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association
    (LVNA) is working to keep residents informed and engaged. As part of these ongoing efforts, we are
    pleased to announce that Sunnyvale’s new Police Chief, Daniel Pistor, will be our special guest
    at the upcoming LVNA General Meeting at Lakewood Park on Wednesday, October 8, at 7:00
    PM.
    Chief Pistor will discuss neighborhood safety priorities, answer your questions, and share his vision
    for community policing in Lakewood Village. This is a vital opportunity for residents to engage
    directly with law enforcement leadership, voice concerns, and offer suggestions.
    Don’t miss this important conversation! Mark your calendar for October 8 at 7 PM. Your
    participation is crucial in shaping a safer Lakewood Village.

    Donate to LVNA: Your Support Keeps Our Events Alive!
    Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association (LVNA) is now a
    501(c)(3) non-profit organization. This milestone means your
    donations are tax-deductible, and they’re more important
    than ever!
    Community favorites like the Summer Picnic, block parties,
    Dumpster Day, the Holiday Parade and Lights Contest
    wouldn’t be possible without the support of generous
    neighbors like you. These cherished traditions depend on
    community donations to continue. Help us keep them going
    and create new opportunities for Lakewood Village!
    How to donate
    Donate online: Send your donation to
    treasurer@lvnasv.org via Zelle, or simply scan the QR
    code with your banking app.
    Mail a check: Make checks payable to "Lakewood Village
    Neighborhood Association" and mail them to: C/O Linh
    Ly, 1102 Pecos Way, Sunnyvale, CA 94089
     Every dollar counts toward building a stronger, more vibrant
    community!
    We Need Volunteers – Lakewood Village Needs YOU!
    Our neighborhood thrives because of people like you, and right now, we need your help to keep it strong!
    We’re looking for volunteers to step up in the following roles: 
    Website designer: We need your expertise to revamp our website to keep up with current events and
    news around the neighborhood.
    Photographer: Help us capture events happening and tell our story.
    Procurement team: Securing resources and partnering with local businesses.
    Lakewood School Liaison: Parents to be our liaison between the school and our neighborhood. Let us
    know what events are happening, so we can support your students!
    Block party hosts: All you need is to find a date that works, fill out a 1pg reservation with the city, make
    flyers to invite neighbors, make it a potluck, and set up a table. And you may even get to know your
    neighbors a bit more and find commonalities. The goal is to form stronger bonds, building a community
    that cares and looks out for each other. 
    Contact board@lvnasv.org today to volunteer. Lakewood Village is OUR neighborhood—let’s keep it thriving
    together!

    Help Fix Lakewood Village! Report Issues Today
    The LVNA board is working to compile a list of the most neglected areas in Lakewood Village, and we need
    your help! Our goal is to identify the Top 10 Worst Spots in the neighborhood and share them with the City of
    Sunnyvale to push for much-needed repairs and improvements.
    What Issues Should You Report?
    Look around Lakewood Village and let us know about areas that need attention, including but not limited to:
    Sidewalks that are cracked or unsafe
    Potholes that need filling
    Overgrown trees in need of trimming
    Abandoned vehicles that are taking up space
    Neglected properties that impact our community’s appearance
    How to Submit a Report
    The more reports we get, the stronger our case! You can submit issues in two easy ways:
    1. Email us with specific details (exact addresses, descriptions, and photos if possible) at board@lvnasv.org.
    2. Report directly to the City via Access Sunnyvale (https://sunnyvale.dynamics365portals.us). This officially
    enters your complaint into the system. If you do this, let us know so we can help follow up!
    3. Attend a City Hall meeting on a Tuesday to voice your concerns in person—this can make an even bigger
    impact and catch their attention. If you choose this option, also file a claim on Access Sunnyvale to
    strengthen your case. More information about the meeting: https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov/your-
    government/governance/city-council/council-meeting-information
    Together, we can make Lakewood Village a safer, cleaner, and better-maintained community. Thank you for
    taking the time to report issues and advocate for our neighborhood!
