    HURRAY ! | The Lakewood Village Aquatic
    Club is a reality. It will be built, by a private
    enterprise, on the section of land at Manzano
    and Oak Creek Drive, right in Lakewood Vill-
    age.
    
    There will be two pools for adults, heated and
    lighted and a separate pool for the children in
    their own play area. Alsothere will bea club-
    house for dancing, plus an outdoor game area
    for croquet and horseshoes. Future plans in-
    clude tennis courts and a miniature golf
    course,
    
    . Charter memberships are now available. Due
    
    to the fact that the club will have a limited
    membership, signing up will be on a first-
    come, first-served basis. This, of course, will
    insure a non-crowded area. Those who have
    already signed up need not worry, as they will
    remain at the head of the list.
    
    The club will bea great improvement to Lake-
    wood, both from a property value viewpoint,
    as well as a recreational one.
    
    Of course, the $100.00 for the first ycar and
    the $50.00 for all succeeding years will apply
    only to the first 300 charter members.
    
    As new members from the waiting list fill any
    vacancies that may occur, and as theclub fac-
    ilities are improved, the dues will! be raised
    for the new in-coming member families.
    Charter members dues will remain the same.
    So be one of the Charter members, and join
    now | Don’t delay or your name wil! be on the
    waiting list. Call RE. 6-2738 days or RE. 9-
