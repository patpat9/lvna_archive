## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Hawks Win Bayside League Championship:** Championship trophy presentation for the Lakewood Hawks Little League team.
    - **Membership Value:** Why united household membership empowers LVRA negotiations with City Hall.
    - **Lakewood Park Lecture Series:** Dr. John Smith, M.D. and Dr. Harry C. Danielson speaking at Lakewood Park.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Notes from the Board & Automotive:** Cal-Tex service guarantees; board updates.
    - **Sanitation & Health:** Fly abatement measures and proper garbage can sealing.
    - **Travel Notes:** Parnell family vacation to Missouri.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Religious Education:** Catholic catechism classes start on Sandia Avenue and Lakewood Drive.
    - **LVRA Leadership Directory:** Full officer listing and contact phone numbers.
