    Free Singer’s Clinic in Sunnyvale 
     
    The Peninsulaires a cappella 
    chorus (an non-profit music 
    education organization) is 
    sponsoring The Singer’s 
    Voice Clinic – a free vocal 
    training program specifically 
    for the male voice. 
     
    This six-week clinic begins 
    Tuesday, May 15th at 7:30 p.m. and runs weekly 
    through Tuesday, June 19th. Each 90-minute session 
    will deliver hands on training in the fundamentals of 
    singing. The location is at the First United Methodist 
    Church at 535 Old San Francisco Road, Sunnyvale.  
     
    For additional information and to register visit 
    www.freevoicelessons.net  or call John Hinkle  
    408-896-0444. 
     
    Paul Eastman, a renowned vocal coach and member of 
    the Peninsulaires, leads the weekly sessions. He has 
    competed in several quartets and choruses at the 
    international level of the Barbershop Harmony Society. 
    Paul works closely with Peninsulaires director, Steve 
    Sammonds in the chorus’ weekly practice sessions. 
     
    The Peninsulaires perform year round and their musical 
    arrangements vary from classics to jazz, patriotic to 
    sacred, and is rounded out with standards and 
    Broadway pops. For more info go to: 
    www.thepeninsulaires.org 

    Lakewood School at San Jose Giants Game 
     
     
    When: June 1st @ 7:00pm   
    Tickets are $5.00 each 
    Email jayne.ferreira@sesd.org to buy tickets 
     
    Do you love baseball? Have you ever been to 
    a San Jose Giants game? Well this is your 
    chance to get a bargain and support Lakewood 
    School at the same time!.  All the money raised 
    will be going into teacher accounts for the start 
    of next year.. So far we have sold less than 
    500 tickets. We bought 2,500 that we thought we could sell.  
     
    Mrs. Cheng will be throwing out the first pitch. Taste Turkey 
    Mike’s Barbeque, watch a ballgame and enjoy the 
    entertainment between innings. The fun includes Mr. Davis in a 
    dance-off with Gigante and the Lakewood chorus singing “Take 
    Me Out to the Ballgame.  
     
    Did you know that 149 former San Jose Giants had reached the 
    major leagues? 108 of those players debuted 
    with San Francisco. And 15 former San Jose 
    Giants debuted in the big leagues during the 
    2008 season. There were 10 former SJ 
    Giants on San Francisco's World Series roster 
    in 2010, including starting pitchers Tim 
    Lincecum, Matt Cain, Jonathan Sanchez and 
    Madison Bumgarner along with catcher 
    Buster Posey. In 1991, San Jose Giants 
    shortstop Clay Bellinger was the first former 
    SJ Giant to win a World Series ring (1999 New York Yankees). 
    Lincecum, a 2006 San Jose Giant, won the National League Cy 
    Young Award in 2008 and 2009. 
     
    Home field for the San Jose Giants is fan-friendly, historic San 
    Jose Municipal Stadium. Built in 1942 with $84,000 in WPA 
    funds, San Jose Muni provides her fans with the feeling of 
    baseball as it should be. All seats provide close up viewing 
    where not only can you see all the action, but you can hear it 
    too. Players are accessible nightly for autographs and photos. 
    Down the left field line is Turkey Mike's Baseball & BBQ. Since 
    its opening in 1988, the food has been acclaimed throughout 
    the baseball world. From a CSNBayArea.com article in 2011 by 
    Paul O'Brian, the following was written:  
     
    "...the heart of the ball park food scene at San Jose 
    Municipal, Turkey Mike's BBQ! As the sign says, it's 
    world famous! While there, you can feast on ribs, 
    chicken, tri-tip, Philly cheesesteaks, sausages, wings, 
    garlic fries and much more. Their special rub has been a 
    secret within the organization since the team became the 
    San Jose Giants in 1988. Only one person in the 
    organization has the recipe and it is kept under lock and 
    key!" 
    (Editor..Fred loves the Tri-tip and I love the hot dogs. We 
    have been going to the San Jose Giant games for years. 
    The games are very family oriented and a lot of fun! ) 

    The 27th Hands on the Arts is coming! 
     
    Where: Sunnyvale Community Center 
     
       550 E. Remington Dr. 
    When:   Saturday, May 19th  
                  10:00am – 4:00pm 
     
    Hands on the Arts 
    is Sunnyvale's 
    signature event. It is a 
    multicultural arts festival 
    especially for children 
    between the ages 3-12.  
     
    Hands on the Arts features 
    all-day family-friendly 
    entertainment on the 
    outdoor stage and more 
    than 30 workshops giving 
    children opportunities to 
    learn and try different art 
    forms. Projects this year will include: Japanese Brush 
    Painting, Indian Diyas, Wands, Wings and Things, 
    Dancing Chinese Dragons, Silly Snakes and Peruvian 
    Arpilleras. 
     
    With so many workshops from which to choose, children 
    can keep busy all day and leave with armloads of 
    projects. Most workshops allow a child to create an art 
    project to take home in about 15 minutes. 
     
     
     
     
    Performing-arts workshops teach a song or 
    dance to be presented in impromptu 
    performances throughout the day. 
     
    Admission to the festival grounds is free, but 
    participation in workshops requires purchase 
    of a wristband for $10 per child at the festival 
    information booth or in advance online.  
     
    Go to: HandsontheArts.inSunnyvale.com to 
    purchase the wristband. 
     
    Wristband allow unlimited workshop participation and 
    includes all supplies. Some workshops do have a 
    minimum age restriction. Each child must have his or her 
    own wristband and wear at all times to participate.  
     
    Parents and caregivers are invited to watch and help their 
    children complete projects, but because of limited 
    supplies, adults are not permitted to complete projects of 
    their own. 
     
    Food is available for purchase in the festival's food court 
    area, and picnics are also allowed. 
    Entrance to the festival grounds is always free and 
    includes live entertainment throughout the day. There is 
    plenty of free parking!  

    Downtown Construction Update 
    The property formerly known as The Sunnyvale Town & 
    Country was divided and sold to two 
    developers. The southern half (the parcel that 
    directly faces Washington Avenue) was 
    purchased by BRE properties and is currently 
    under construction. Their project, Solstice 
    Apartment Homes, will feature four stories 
    equaling 280 apartments as well as 30,000 
    retail space on the ground floor. To see their 
    other properties, go to: www.breproperties.com 
     
    The second parcel was purchased by Carmel Partners who 
    plan to build 130+ rental units along Olson Way (formerly 
    Capella Way) with retail facing South Frances Street and The 
    Plaza del Sol grounds. Ground breaking will not take place 
    until Summer 2012. For more information and other Carmel 
    Partner developments please visit: 
    www.carmelapartments.com 
