    Happy Holidays! 
     
    I hope you and your families had as 
    lovely a Thanksgiving as Fred and I 
     
    It’s the holiday time again and nothing  says it like our 
    neighborhood lighting up! I have always hated 
    ―daylight saving time.‖ It does not really ―save time‖ or  
    electricity. There are statistics that show there is very 
    little difference in the use of electricity by moving the 
    clock forward (in the fall). However, I always look  
    forward to seeing my neighbor’s decorations and  
    holiday lights! 
     
    Unfortunately this is also the season for 
    shopping! I now receive over 70 emails 
    a day telling me the great deals I can 
    get. (Fortunately, I am almost done with 
    my Christmas shopping)  I 
    have even received emails 
    from companies I had  
    never heard of. Be very careful of any 
    emails from someone you do not know, 
    because there may be viruses embedded 
    in them. One of the computers were I work 
    has been infected .It causes my computer 
    to slow down and I have to reboot about 
    once an hour . 
     
    This month we will have a ―Holiday Desert 
    Pot Luck.‖ It will be the December 14th,  
    the second Wednesday of the month. 
    Bring your favorite holiday treat and share 
    with your neighbors. We will have hot 
    chocolate and cider. Snickerdoodles have always  
    been a holiday tradition in my family, so I will bake 
    some up. Have a lovely holiday and see you  
    Wednesday!  

    Operation Second Chance 
     
    Starting on Thursday, December 1, 
    2016 and continuing through the end of 
    December, the Sunnyvale  
    Department of Public Safety, in  
    conjunction with the Santa Clara  
    County Sheriff's Office and other San-
    ta Clara County law enforcement agen-
    cies, will participate in a special pro-
    gram that allows individuals with NON-
    VIOLENT MISDEMEANOR CRIMINAL and  
    TRAFFIC WARRANTS to receive a citation and new 
    court date, in lieu of being booked into County jail. 
     
    Individuals can go to ANY Santa Clara County law  
    enforcement agency to receive a warrant citation, which 
    will require a new "Promise to Appear" in court date.  
    Operation Second Chance 2016 is directed towards 
    MOST misdemeanor warrants, no matter the bail 
    amount, including warrants stamped "No Cite and  
    Release" and "No Bail." 
     
    The following warrants DO NOT qualify for this program: 
    Felony warrants: 
     
    Any warrant involving violence (including All  
     
    domestic violence warrants) 
     
    Any warrant involving a firearm 
     
    Any warrant involving resisting arrest 
     
    Any warrant involving giving false information to a 
    peace officer 
     
    Individuals with these types of warrants however, are 
    urged to take this opportunity to turn themselves in so 
    they can take care of their matter in court. 
     
    The Sunnyvale Department of Public Safety will accept 
    self-surrenders 7 days a week, except between the 
    hours of 5 PM and 9 PM. If you have questions relating 
    to this program, please contact the  
    Sunnyvale Department of Public Safety Records Unit at 
    (408) 730-7101. 

    Police Blotter 
     
     
    Felony Hit & Run/Possession of Drugs  
    E. Duane & Borregas 
    Tuesday Nov 2nd @ 2:38 pm 
     
    Officers responded on a report of a vehicle traveling at a high 
    rate of speed that struck several vehicles.  The driver of one of 
    the other vehicles was transported to the hospital with non-life 
    threatening injuries.  The suspect was seen fleeing on foot and 
    was eventually located in the backyard of a residence in the 
    800 Blk. Madrone.  The suspect was in possession of cocaine 
    and methamphetamine and booked into County Jail. 
     
    Residential Burglary –  
    600 Blk. Lakehaven Ter 
    Tuesday Nov 2nd between 8:00 am – 6:00pm 
    An unknown suspect entered the residence by breaking a side 
    window and stole electronics and jewelry. 
      
    Commercial Burglary – 800 Blk. Kifer 
    Wednesday Nov 3rd @ 4:14 am 
    Officers responded on a report of an in-progress burglary at a 
    fast food restaurant.  Upon arrival, officers located a broken 
    window and heard someone inside attempting to break into a 
    safe.  The suspect was apprehended with the use of a DPS 
    K9.  The suspect was being investigated into connection with 
    other commercial burglaries in the region.  The suspect was 
    booked into County Jail. 
      
    Possession of a Stolen Vehicle  
    – 1200 Blk. Wildwood 
    Sunday Nov 6th @ 2:46am 
    Officers were patrolling in the parking lot of a hotel and located 
    a vehicle that was reported stolen out of San Jose.  The 
    vehicle was occupied by the suspect and taken into 
    custody.  It was determined that the keys to the vehicle were 
    stolen in a residential burglary.  The suspect was booked into 
    County Jail.  
     
    Auto Burglary – 1100 Blk. E. Arques 
    Tuesday Nov 8th @ 7:30 am 
    Officers responded on a report of an auto burglary that had 
    just occurred.  It was determined the victim’s purse was stolen 
    and one of her credit cards was being used at a store in Santa 
    Clara.  Officers responded to the area of the store and located 
    three suspects in a vehicle that were in possession of the 
    victim’s property, in addition to other stolen property.  The 
    suspects were also suspected in being involved in residential 
    burglaries in other jurisdictions.  All three suspects were 
    arrested and booked into County Jail.    
