    Announcements Due to overwhelming demand, the L.V.N.A. will sponsor anoth- er Dumpster Day this year. The 18 dumpsters will be placed around Lakewood on June 5. We would like to identify people who will need assistance removing large debris as well as volun- teers who can help their neighbors in need. Call Jessica Lee at 745-6534 to volunteer or request help. The L.V.N.A. is in the process of compiling a community tal- ent bank. Please contact the board if you have a talent you can share. We would like to identify people who can help, give ad- vice, or answer questions in areas such as legal or paralegal, op- crating a HAM radio, sports or music for youth, access to a copy machine, or any other talent you would like to donate. We are looking for a responsible volunteer to babysit at the monthly meetings for an hour and a half once a month, Please call Jessica at 745-6534 to volunteer. Please be aware that the Public Safety Department has been issu- ing expensive parking tickets to vehicles parked in the school personnel spaces at Lakewood Elementary. They have also been quick to tow cars which violate city parking laws. Kindergarten registration for this fall has already begun at Lakewood Elementary. If you have a child who will be 5 years old by December 2, 1993, please register him or her as soon as possible. Call the school at 745-6156 for more information. The mural art project is now in the approval stage. The artist has presented his proposal, which incorporates many symbols of Lakewood and Sunnyvale and leaves spaces for 100 neighbor- hood children to paint their own artwork. The artist and any chil- dren who volunteer will begin painting in May. The second session of Toddler Time is running from April 29 through June 10. Toddler Time is a program for preschoolers in which each parent assists for at least one class. The class is held Thursdays at 9:30 A.M. For more information, call 730-7515. The L.V.N.A. still has candy bars and T-shirts available for sale, Contact any of the board members to make a purchase and support the nieghborhood association. This month, Sunnyvale Town Center is displaying artwork from Sunnyvale childcare centers. The exhibit includes some art by the children at family daycare centers here in Lakewood. We will attempt to to print all submissions as space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any questions or concerns about the newsletter.
