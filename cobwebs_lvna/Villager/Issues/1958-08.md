## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **Inaugural LVRA Picnic A Big Hit:** Community picnic celebration at Lakewood Park with games, youth races, and family gatherings.
    - **Vandalism Prevention:** Streetlight breakage and property damage in development units; neighborhood watch coordination with Sunnyvale Police.
    - **Merchant Spotlight:** Hart Cleaners tailoring and dry cleaning on Yorkshire.
- **Page 2:**
    - **Youth Sports:** Bradley & Montgomery Little League team highlights ('The Bombastic Boys of Lakewood Village').
    - **Merchant Directory:** A-1 Superette, Mobil Service, and Travelers Insurance monthly payment plans.
- **Page 3:**
    - **Journalism Recruitment:** Invitation for neighborhood residents to write and report for *The Villager*.
    - **Local Delivery & Service Network:** Coppola Dairy farm store, Borden's Dairy home milk delivery truck, Colonial Bakery home delivery truck, Mac's Hardware on East Maude, and Pozas Trucking.
- **Page 4:**
    - **From the Executive Board:** Detailed justification for $2.00 annual LVRA household dues and community representation.
    - **Classifieds & Local Services:** Welton Shell Station, Hills Automotive Repair, Ed & Jake's Blue Bonnet Cafe, and Seiji's Bakery Depot.
