The City of Sunnyvale is holding a series of focus group meet- ings to discuss the city's parks and recreation needs. To get in- volved call 730-7522. Cub Scout Pack #403 is conducting a Thanksgiving food drive. The scouts will deliver bags throughout Lakewood on November 14, and pick up the filled bags from your doorsteps on Novem- ber 21. Please give what you can. The food will be distributed to needy families in Sunnyvale by the Second Harvest Food Bank. Christmas is coming, and so is the Lnkewood Village Holiday Parade! Be on the lookout for exciting information about our December 19 parade, which will include photos with Santa. We are in need of cars for the Holiday Parade. If you have a car, preferably a convertible, which we can use, please call Judy Fer- nandez at 747-0452. Start planning your holiday decorations now! The annual Lake- wood Holiday Lighting Contest will be judged on Wednesday, December 16. If you are interested in networking with other out of work Lake- wood residents, contact David Jenkins at 749-1480. The Lakewood Village Playgroup meets every Tuesday from noon at the Lakewood Park playground. The play- group is open to preschoolers of all ages, and allows kids to play while adults visit. Newsletter Delivery Volunteers are needed once a month to help deliver The Villager to residents of Lakewood Village. The Villager is delivered by hand to save on postage, and goes to 1600 homes every month. Newsletter Contributions All information in the Villager is contributed by residents Of Lakewood. If you have an article, announcement, or request for information which you would like published, contact newsletter editor Linda Cheung at 734-1661. We will attempt to to print all submissions as space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any questions or concerns about the newsletter. In the near future, we will also begin to offer advertising space at a resonable rate. The Villager is an excellent source of direct advertising, since the Villager has a circulation of 1800 every month, and is delivered right to the door of every resident in north Sunnyvale.
