    Letter from the President
    
    As Lakewood Village nears 40 years old, its
    best years have been those when Villagers were
    proactive in school and community affairs.
    
    | villagers have made 1993-94 a productive,
    rewarding year for all. I am proud to have led
    such a diverse, energetic, resourceful
    volunteer team and pleased to attain the
    objectives we set out to reach this last year.
    
    We abated graffiti, kept our neighborhood
    clean, and brought teen programs to Lakewood.
    
    We held forums for city council and school
    board candidates, strengthened our relations
    with the City, County, and neighboring
    communities. We voiced our appreciation and
    our concerns to the City Council, and to Santa
    Clara concerning the Mission College Mall.
    
    We painted the Lakewood mural, supported adult
    E.S.L. classes at Lakewood, donated $350 worth
    of toys, held a block party, welcomed 30 new
    neighbors, and organized the L.V.N.A. for
    increased Villager support and involvement.
    
    We published and distributed 12 issues of the
    newsletter, held 11 neighborhood meetings, and
    continued the holiday parade, the lighting
    contest, and many other traditions. This board
    started with $800 and will pass $3,700 on to
    the next board.
    
    Villagers, each and every one of you is a
    leader in making the present and shaping the
    future of our Village in your own way. Without
    you, we could not have gotten this far.
    up the good work.
    
    Keep
    
    As my presidency concludes, I want to thank
    you all for your support and trust. I look
    forward to working with the new board and
    continuing to serve Villagers as a volunteer.
    
    Jessica Wei Lee, L.V.N.A. President
