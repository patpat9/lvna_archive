    WHAT HAVE YOU 
    BEEN UP TO?
    ASSISTANCE
    We want to hear from YOU! As many of us have likely 
    been spending way more time at home than we possibly 
    could have predicted at the beginning of the year, working 
    harder than ever in an essential job to keep things running 
    as smoothly as possible, or even a mix of both, we want to 
    hear what you’ve been up to. Lakewood Village is a diverse 
    neighborhood and, as a result, has likely seen its residents 
    impacted in different ways. We are all part of LVNA, so having 
    a glimpse into the lives of our neighbors will help in planning 
    as we inch back towards a sense of normalcy, however long 
    that may take.
    Have you been catching up on TV shows? Taking care 
    of patients? Reading a book (or many)? Sticking to an 
    exercising routine? Serving customers? Learning a new skill? 
    Landscaping and/or home decorating? We want to hear it!
    Please email us at editor@lvnasv.org. We will not publish or 
    share anything you say without your consent.
    Sunnyvale Community Services (SCS) is open to the Sunnyvale community to provide support and 
    emergency assistance to those facing financial hardship, homelessness, and hunger. They have 
    emergency food, financial aid, and referral services available. If you or someone in the community that 
    you know is in need of assistance, please contact SCS: info@svcommunityservices.org or 408-738-4321. 
    If you would like to help:
    •	 Consider donating to SCS’ relief fund: https://svcommunityservices.org/donate-online/ Your 
    contribution helps staff meet the needs of those requiring financial assistance in paying for rent, 
    utilities, medical costs, food, and other essential items. 
    •	 Reach out to a neighbor or loved one who may be struggling. Even in difficult times, many people 
    may still feel uncomfortable asking for assistance. Lending a hand can help ease that discomfort.

    RECIPE CORNER
    Spring Rolls with Peanut Sauce
    02     LVNA VILLAGER | JUNE 2020
    1.	 Pour warm water into a large pan, like a baking tray. You 
    want the pan to be large enough to fully submerge your 
    spring roll wrappers.
    2.	 Dip a spring roll wrapper into the warm water for about 5 
    seconds. You want the wrapper to be slightly firm - it will 
    soften as you assemble the roll. 
    3.	 Place wrapper onto flat work surface, like a large plate or 
    cutting board.
    4.	 Add filling to the bottom center of the wrapper: few slices 
    of carrot, cucumber, bell pepper, cabbage, a small amount 
    of noodles, a thin layer of lettuce, and some shrimp on top.
    5.	 Roll everything up tightly while folding in the sides, like a 
    burrito.
    6.	 Peanut sauce: Combine all ingredients listed under peanut 
    sauce, except for water, into a bowl. Whisk in 1 TBSP water 
    at a time until you’ve reached desired consistency. 
    spring roll wrappers
    veggies, all julienned: carrot, 
    cucumber, bell pepper, purple 
    cabbage
    vermicelli noodles, cooked
    green leaf lettuce, destemmed
    medium cooked shrimp, sliced in 
    half, length-wise
    Peanut Sauce:
    1/4 CUP creamy peanut 
    1 TBSP hoisin sauce
    1 TSP soy sauce
    1-2 cloves garlic, minced
    5-6 TSP warm water
    sriracha sauce, optional
