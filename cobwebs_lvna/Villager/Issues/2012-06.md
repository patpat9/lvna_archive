    Officers Arrest Woman In Connection To String 
    Of Arsons At Sunnyvale Mobile Home Park 
     
    By Alia Wilson, Sunnyvale Sun 
     
    Investigation of a string of arsons inside the Casa de Amigos 
    Mobile Home Park has led to the arrest of a 60-year-old 
    woman on May 19th . 
     
    Genine Jobes was arraigned on June 4th  and faces 10 felony 
    charges, including four counts of first-degree burglary, four 
    counts of arson with intent to defraud, one count of arson of a 
    structure or forest land, and one count of attempted arson of 
    a structure or forest land. 
     
    All the fire reports happened within the same area of the park 
    at 1085 Tasman Drive. 
     
    The first incident occurred on April 14th  in space No. 719. 
    Fire personnel extinguished a small fire under a car. 
    The second report was on May 5th . A blanket was rolled up 
    and set on fire under a different car at the 
    same space. 
     
    On May 17th someone tried to set another 
    vehicle on fire at space No. 368. Fire 
    personnel found a small pile of materials on 
    fire leaning against the back fence of space 
    No. 365. 
     
    After that fire, the fire prevention bureau set 
    up surveillance inside the park. On May 18th 
    a garbage can was set on fire. Two hours 
    later at 1:21 a.m. on May 19, personnel 
    witnessed a female resident of the park 
    come out with bags and lit them on fire. 
     
    The series of fires resulted in one car with 
    minor damage. There were no injuries in any 
    of the incidents.

    Shakespeare at Full Circle Farm: 
      
    Presented by  
    Festival Theatre Ensemble 
      
    Produced by Festival Theatre Ensemble (FTE) and directed 
    by Bruce W. De Les Dernier and Rachel Bakker, 
    Shakespeare at Full Circle Farm (formerly Mid-Peninsula 
    Shakespeare Festival) played to overflowing audiences its 
    first year in this new location.  Full Circle Farm, a non-profit 
    sustainable community garden on over 11 acres, is an 
    outstanding venue for this all outdoor festival. 
     
    Romeo and Juliet - Shakespeare's powerful story of love, 
    hate, loyalty and blood, with breathtaking sword play and 
    unforgettable performances June 8, 9, 17, 23, 30  
     
    The Two Gentlemen of Verona – Boy meets girl, boy meets 
    another girl, boy thinks he can get away with it.  In this 
    delicious comedy, Shakespeare explores love Italian style set 
    in the Swingin’ 60s. June 2, 3, 10, 22, 29

    Bruce W. De Les Dernier. Can Alice keep her head through 
    jousting knights, dueling twins, a monster in the forest and a 
    mad, mad tea party?   June 15, 16, 21, 24, 28

    The FTE Fight Collective presents a fast and fun evening of 
    stage combat demonstrations, fight scenes from well-known 
    plays, black powder weapon demos, and more! As the 
    audience enjoys the stage fighting, their ticket dollars will help 
    raise funds for Full Circle Farm.  Suitable for all ages.  
    June 27  
     
    Friday and Saturday shows begin at 8:00pm; Thursday and 
    Sunday shows begin at 7:30  
    General seating $10 adults, $3 children, pay at the door. 
    Wheelchair seating available.   
     
    Outdoor performances – dress warmly! 
     
    The Farm is located at 1055 Dunford Way in Sunnyvale: just 
    a few blocks east of Wolfe Rd., near El Camino Real. For 
    more information about the season and Festival Theatre

    THE CANTERVILLE GHOST 
    A mansion in England, a family as American as apple pie who 
    moves in and a ghost who simply won’t let go! 
    American Hiram Otis and his family have recently moved to 
    Canterville Chase, a grand estate just outside of London, even 
    though the home was haunted. A reappearing blood stain and 
    the perpetual nocturnal visits of a rather noisy ghost with a taste 
    for the theatrical convince them. Clearly the ghost is going to be 
    driven mad by the very people he is trying to haunt! Resulting in 
    an entertainingly funny romp for the whole family. June 9, 23 and 
    July 14 
     
    THE CONTRAST 
    The first comedy written by an American citizen that was 
    professionally produced - “ a sophisticated, subversive, and dead 
    funny piece of theater.” 
    Set after the Revolutionary War, but don’t let this fool 
    you!  There is plenty of things we can relate to in 21st  century 
    America, like the theme of home-made versus foreign goods 
    and ideas on moral behaviors, economic liberties and standards 
    of education for their young country.  Enter Maria Van Rough - a 
    ―dangerously well-read‖ young lady, betrothed as a child to Billy 
    Dimple, but falling in love instead with earnest soldier Henry 
    Manly. Further complicating Maria’s life includes; two calculating 
    belles, a scheming servant, a naive bumpkin and a boorish old 
    father.  The contrasts between old and young, integrity and 
    pretense, tradition and innovation, city and country living and 
    high and low society has never been more fun! June 14, 15, 16, 
    17, 21, 23, July 7, 14, 19, 22 
     
    A THOUSAND CLOWNS 
    A heartwarming comedy “filled with laughter and warmth, 
    sweetness and inspired daffiness.” - The New York Daily News 
    A coming-of-middle-age story of eccentric bachelor Murray 
    Burns, an unemployed children’s television writer who has found 
    himself raising his sister’s son after she went out for a pack of 
    cigarettes and never returned.  When social workers come to 
    investigate whether his nephew is receiving a proper upbringing. 
    Getting an actual paying job becomes high priority, which means 
    returning to his former work writing for ―Chuckles the Chipmunk‖! 
    A Thousand Clowns speaks to us of commitment and the 
    power of love June 16, 22 July 6, 8, 12, 21

    Neighborhood Crime Corner 
     
    Compiled from Sunnyvale Department 
    of Public Safety reports.  
     
    Auto Burglary--May 16th on E. Arques 
    Avenue. An unknown person broke into a 2012 Mini Cooper 
    and stole a briefcase, miscellaneous papers and a book of 
    checks from Bank of America. 
     
    Auto burglary--May 15th on Lawrence Station Road. An 
    unknown person broke into a Lexus in the Costco parking 
    lot and stole a Sony laptop, seven USB devices, a 
    backpack, dance shoes, five DVDs and makeup. 
     
    Theft--May 14th  on Almanor Avenue. An unknown person 
    stole 200 feet of electrical wire valued at $1,000  
     
    Hit And Run--May 11th  on E. Duane Avenue. A customer 
    drove away, ripping off a gas pump hose at the Sunnyvale 
    Car Spa. 
     
    Auto Burglary – April 25th  on Wildwood. A 2002 Toyota 
    4Runner was broken into. 
     
    Theft – April 23rd on N. Fair Oaks. A storage unit was 
    broken into at Public Safety 
     
    Auto Burglary – April 23rd on Lakeside Dr. A 2010 
    Mercedes 350 parked in the visitors parking lot was broken 
    into over night. 
     
    Fraud—April 25th N. Matilda Ave. A person gave a man 
    counterfeit money for an item the man sold on Craigslist. 
     
    (Editor—Even though this was not in Lakewood Village, I 
    wanted everyone to be aware that counterfeit money is 
    being passed around in Sunnyvale. There is a pen you can 
    buy at Office Depot that helps to spot counterfeit bills) 
     
    Theft—April 20th on S. Bernardo Ave. Fuel was stolen 
    from several vehicles on the victim’s property. 
     
     (Editor—I know this one was not in our neighborhood, but I 
    wanted to bring it to your attention. With the cost of gas at 
    over $4.00 a gallon, this will happen more and more often.  
     
    Protect yourself and your vehicles, buy a locking gas cap. If 
    you see someone acting suspiciously, call 9-1-1 and report 
    it. DO NOT CONFRONT THE PERSON YOURSELF! That’s  
    what our Public Safety Officers are for, to protect our 
    safety.)
