## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **First Annual Yule Lighting Contest:** LVRA announces neighborhood holiday lighting contest rules, categories, and judging schedule.
    - **'L.V. Lights Up!':** Street-by-street Christmas illumination campaign.
    - **The First Year in Review:** Ted Glasnow recaps LVRA's foundational 1958 achievements.
- **Page 2:**
    - **Fire Station Advocacy:** Executive Board lobbying City Council for a dedicated fire station near Lakewood Village.
    - **Flood Control:** Santa Clara County Flood Control requested to dredge and clear the Calabazas Creek channel before winter rains.
    - **Membership Drive:** Faye Anderson appointed Membership Committee Chair; distribution of official 1959 membership cards.
- **Page 3:**
    - **Social Committee Report:** Sandy Cox and Shirley Pisano host; planning children's Christmas party at Fairwood School and holiday dinner-dance.
- **Page 4:**
    - **Lakewood Village Emergency Corps (Civil Defense):** Formation of local communications squad (George Urbani, Chief) and radiological defense squad (Jim Gruidl, R.A.D.E.F. Squad Leader) under federal civil defense guidelines; Irving Mandell named Public Education Director.
    - **Community Notices:** Advice to vandals; neighborhood religious class schedule.
- **Page 5:**
    - **Teen Club Happenings:** 'Name the Teen Club' contest; youth dance on Dec 6; Lakewood teens attend Ted Randall TV dance show in San Jose; teen roller skating outing.
    - **Local Business:** Alviso Building Supply ready-mix concrete and tool rentals on State Street.
- **Page 6:**
    - **Complete 1958 Roster:** Full listing of officers, committee chairs, teenage reporters, and block delivery captains across Units 1 through 5.
