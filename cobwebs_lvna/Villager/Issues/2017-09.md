    SEPT 2017 THE VILLAGER Traffic Calming for Lakewood Village Hey Neighbors, my name is Pat Muniz! As most of you know, they are using our neighborhood for the Indy 500 tryouts!! Just kidding, but we all know the excessive speeds we see on our residential streets every day puts us, our children, and pets at risk. The board and the neighbors are doing a great job of reporting speeding cars, which works for specific drivers that we can identify. However, if we want a permanent solution, we need to get some speed humps or some other traffic calming devices installed to slow down the vehicles all the time. The LVNA will be forming a Street Calming Committee to collect signatures for the petition and determined where in the Village we need speed control. The city has a specific process in place that we need to fulfill to complete this process. Please volunteer and come to the next LVNA meeting so the committee can begin the necessary steps to make our neighborhood a safer place to live. We will have a guest speaker from the City of Sunnyvale Department of Public Works Transportation and Traffic Division present to educate LVNA on the process of implementing traffic calming measures such as installing speed humps.
