    By Zedory
    
    Several Sunnyvale neighborhoods are designating
    themselves as single story only (most are Eichler
    Neighborhoods, Lakewood Village homes are modeled
    after Eichler Designs). They are doing so to protect their
    privacy and also to maintain neighborhood character.
    (Some of these neighborhoods have also adopted specific
    design guidelines for remodels/additions which can be
    found here:
    http://docplayer.net/13251099-Eichler-design-guideli

    ‘ complete an application and have a reasonable
    boundary drawn around the district.

    agree to sign petition
    * 20 lots minimum are required per application
    * Submit to hearing process
    * Upon approval, an“*S” is added to the zoning within the
