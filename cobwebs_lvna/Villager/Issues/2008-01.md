    The basics:
    Make your home look occupied, and make it difficult to

    If you will be away overnight or longer, connect some
    lamps to automatic timers to turn them on in the
    evening and off during the day. Keep your garage door
    closed and locked. Don't alldw daily deliveries of mail,
    newspapers, or flyers to build up while you are awey.
    Arrange with the U.S. Post Offrce to hold your mail, or
    arrafige for a friend or neighbor to take them in
    regularly. Arrange for your lawn to be mowed if you
    are going arvay for an extended time. Check locks on
    doors and windows; replace them with secure devices
    as necessary. Pushbutton locks on doorknobs are easy
    for burglars to open. Install deadbolt locks on all your
    outside doors. Special locks are available for sliding
    glass doors, for better security.
    Don't tempt a thief:
    Lawn mowers, barbeeues and bicycles are best stored
    out of sight. Always lock garden sheds and garages.
    Use curtains on gaf,age and basement windows. Never
