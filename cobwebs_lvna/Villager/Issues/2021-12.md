    VOLUNTEERING FOR LVNA
    Are you passionate about our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new neighbors? Would you be overjoyed 
    to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends, and the next generation who’s currently 
    growing up right outside your door or even in your own backyard?
    Perhaps you should consider running for one of the five LVNA board positions. We have 2 members who have 
    served many years on the board and will be stepping down this March. The board meets once per month to 
    discuss ongoing activities, in addition to the monthly general meeting where we invite guest speakers to discuss 
    topics of interest for the neighborhood (currently still virtual due to COVID) and the few seasonal events each 
    year. The level of commitment is up to each individual – as these are all completely volunteer roles. All of the 
    incumbent board members are happy to shuffle around, so, any of the 5 roles are available for the upcoming 
    term (March 2022 – March 2023). The only requirements are that you are 18+ and live in the neighborhood, and 
    plan to for the duration of the term.
    President, 1st VP, 2nd VP, Treasurer, and Secretary 
    Want to help, but not interested in a board position? Our Villager editors will be stepping down in March so 
    the role of Villager Editor is open to anyone interested (no age requirement). At a minimum, the role consists 
    of proof-reading the content and compiling it all into a single document. The sky is the limit though so the 
    previous editors have helped write some of that content, design a new layout, take photos for fun backgrounds, 
    and send it out to the Villager subscribers each month. The board is happy to help with these other aspects 
    though so you have as much creative freedom as you’d like.
    Want to partner up with a family member or friend? Great! Are you a high school student interested in writing or 
    media design and trying to increase your community service hours to meet a graduation or Scouts requirement? 
    This is your chance to feed a couple ducks with a single loaf of bread! (See how much fun this role can be? You 
    can make up your own idioms if you don’t like killing birds with stones.)
    Any of these roles are a great opportunity to help actively drive change for our community and make a positive 
    impact on our neighborhood. If interested, please email the board and let them know you’re on board (puns – 
    love it)!

    MEET YOUR NEIGHBOR
    LINDA CAIN
    Q: Tell me a bit about yourself and your family.
    Linda: My immediate family consists of my husband 
    Dennis, my son Damon and my two grandsons, Damon 
    and Dario, who are 19 and 18. Dennis and I celebrated 
    our 59th wedding anniversary in November. I am a retired 
    accounting manager and since retiring have spent most 
    of my time volunteering. I worked for two years at a 
    domestic violence nonprofit but had to leave when they 
    relocated to downtown San Jose. I then joined Sunnyvale 
    Community Services and have been there nine years. 
    Many volunteers were retired due to Covid in 2020.They 
    very recently moved into a new building and I hope that 
    they will soon open up to all volunteers. I really enjoy my 
    volunteer work. I was on the LVNA board for 2019-20 as 
    secretary and in 2020-21 as treasurer. This is such a great 
    association and I would love to see more neighborhood 
    people involved.
    Q: When did you move to the Lakewood/Fairwood 
    neighborhood?
    Linda: Dennis and I moved to Lakewood Village in 
    December 1969. Our son was born in March of 1969 so it 
    was a good time.
    Q: What street do you live on?
    Linda: We live on Lakeknoll Drive.
    Q: Name one thing you see out your kitchen window.
    Linda: There is not much to see out of our kitchen window, 
    just our side yard and a bit of our neighbor’s yard.
    Q: Something that is in your front yard?
    Linda: We almost always have our front door open. I am 
    light-sensitive, so drapes are kept closed. I love to see 
    walkers and their pets go by. We have a lot of walkers on 
    Lakeknoll. I also love seeing our cat colony out there. We 
    have a fairly large colony that we feed and take care of. 
    They do not live here but come to eat twice a day and 
    there are a few that will lay around our yard periodically 
    throughout the day. I particularly love the children’s 
    reaction when they see our cats. They get so excited and 
    happy.
    Q: A sound you hear frequently in the neighborhood?
    Linda: Our street is actually very quiet. I enjoy the sounds 
    of children playing but we don’t have much of that either.
    Q: What is something you like about living in Lakewood/
    Fairwood? 
    Linda: I like my neighborhood. We have almost always had 
    great neighbors. We still have four families close by that 
    have lived here even longer than us. We are also fortunate 
    to be surrounded by new young families. It is a renewal of 
    our neighborhood. It is nice to have some shopping close 
    to us. The nearby parks are also great. It is also close to 
    several freeways and expressways for quick access.
    Please email vp2@lvnasv.org if you want to recommend someone for an upcoming newsletter or if you would 
    like to interview a neighbor for the newsletter.
    Q: And something that has changed since you moved to the 
    neighborhood?
    Linda: I don’t see a big change in Lakewood Village. Maybe 
    the remodeling or new construction of homes. The library 
    that is coming will be great although I think getting rid of the 
    swimming pool was a mistake. One big minus is the Christmas 
    Parade. This was around for so many years and I would love to 
    see it make a comeback.
    Q: What are your favorite parks/things to do in Sunnyvale and/
    or the Bay Area?
    Linda: Our parks are very nice. My son spent so much time 
    there with Lakewood Youth Baseball, scouts and playing with 
    his friends. We also used the parks a lot when our grandsons 
    were growing up, they loved both parks with the water and the 
    equipment. They loved grandpa putting them in or on the tire 
    and twirling them all around. The LVNA picnic has also been 
    fun for our family.
    Q: Tell me a bit about your hobbies.
    Linda: I love to read and manage to get some in every day. I 
    also color in coloring books for adults. I have always enjoyed 
    walking but it is too dangerous for me now so I bought a leg 
    elliptical machine that I use every day.
    Q: Any great stories to share about living in the neighborhood?
    Linda: I can’t think of any specific story about Lakewood Village. 
    It is great that so many people in our neighborhood have been 
    connected to Lakewood Village on Nextdoor. There is a great 
    deal of information on that site. It allows us to stay in touch with 
    Lakewood Village and other neighbors that are close by. 
    Editor’s note: If you are unfamiliar with Nextdoor and would 
    like to join the community, go to www.nextdoor.com. Sign up 
    for an account (it’s free) to join. Once you have an account, 
    look for the group “LVNA (Lakewood Village Neighborhood 
    Association)” and request to join. This is the group specifically 
    for our Lakewood and Fairwood neighborhoods. If you 
    get stuck, please email vp2@lvnasv.org or ask your nearest 
    technology expert for help.
