    December in Your Yard  
    and Garden 
     
    ☼Be sure to get in the last of the 
    spring-blooming bulbs this month. Remember that 
    wet soil is difficult to dig properly. Be careful not to 
    plant the bulbs too deep. 
    ☼Water plants as needed.  
    ☼Watch out for frost. 
    ☼Direct water from downspouts to avoid damage to 
    the garden. 
    ☼Save wood ashes from the fireplace as a source of 
    potash for the compost pile. 
    ☼•Request seed and nursery catalogs from mail-order 
    companies. 
    ☼Set out large-sized transplants of winter-flowering 
    plants into the bedding plot, using such plants as 
    calendula, Iceland poppy, primrose, and stock. 
    ☼Apply dormant spray to deciduous flowering plants 
    and fruit trees. 
    ☼Prune pines, magnolias, junipers, and red-berried 
    plants for holiday greens. 
    ☼Cut back chrysanthemums as they finish blooming. 
     
     
     
     
    ☼Hold off fertilizing most plants in preparation for 
    winter. 
    ☼Stabilize plants that might be harmed by wind. 
    ☼Harvest winter vegetables as they ripen. 
    These tips are from the book Northern California Gardening: 
    A Month-by-Month Guide, by Katherine Grace Endicott, 
    published by Chronicle Books, San Francisco, copyright 1996, 
    and are used with the publisher’s permission.■

    Halloween Display Ruined by Vandals 
    On Halloween night vandals ruined the decorations of 
    one of our long-time Lakewood residents, even spray 
    painting a car. Please keep an eye out for suspicious 
    behavior, and if you see any vandalism happening, 
    please let the police know about it. Destroying 
    decorations keeps others from enjoying them and is a 
    blow to neighborhood morale.■ 
     
     
    Avoid Auto Burglaries 
    -Skip Gottschalk, Lakewood Village resident 
    There appears to be an increase recently in the number 
    of stolen cars and auto burglaries in Lakewood 
    Village. Remember NOT to place objects in your car 
    that can be seen from the outside, especially laptops 
    and other electronic items, sunglasses, etc. If possible, 
    park your car in a lighted area, and get a car alarm 
    system. 
    If you notice an unfamiliar car in your neighborhood, 
    ask your neighbors if they know who the owner is. If 
    no one knows, then call the Nuisance Vehicle Hotline, 
    730-7706. If the car’s windows are broken or there is 
    other evidence of burglary, call 911. 
    If you see any suspicious activities, you may call the 
    DPS non-emergency number (408-730-7100). If you 
    think it’s urgent, don’t hesitate to call 911.■

    Free Wireless Internet Service Borders 
    Lakewood Village 
    MetroFi is now providing its free wireless Internet 
    service close enough to Lakewood Village that some 
    residents may be able to use the service directly and 
    other residents may be able to use the service if they 
    install an outdoor antenna. Until recently, the closest 
    MetroFi access points were south of Evelyn Avenue. 
    The new access points provide service just south of 
    Highway 101 between Mathilda and Lawrence 
    Expressway. Residents living near Lakewood Drive 
    should get the best reception. 
    MetroFi provides free wireless Internet access and 
    municipal services that are supported by local and 
    national advertisers. Locally, MetroFi operates free 
    municipal Wi-Fi networks for residents, visitors, and 
    city workers in Concord, Cupertino, Foster City, San 
    Jose, Santa Clara, and Sunnyvale.

    Car Accident? 
    Here’s the information the insurance companies 
    don’t want you to know! 
    A free report reveals the truth about hidden injuries 
    caused by “low impact” auto accidents, or “fender 
    benders.” These hidden injuries are often overlooked
