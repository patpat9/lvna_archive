## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **1959 LVRA Elections:** Annual general meeting set for officer voting.
    - **Holiday Lighting Contest Results:** Announcement of neighborhood winners for the first annual Yule Lighting Contest.
- **Pages 2-4:**
    - **Candidate Profiles:** Statements and biographical summaries for Chairman candidates (Hugh Jess, Bill Burgess), Vice Chairman nominees (Ernie Smith, Ted Glasnow), and Treasurer candidates (Joan Glasnow, Don Kitcher).
    - **Local Sponsors:** Mac's Hardware and Housley Cleaners.
- **Pages 5-6:**
    - **Social Committee Report:** Carole Smith recaps holiday festivities and community potlucks.
    - **'Why?':** Analysis of the surge in attendance at general meetings and growing civic participation.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Civil Defense & Disaster Preparedness:** Ernie Smith outlines federal guidelines for home fallout protection.
    - **LVRA Leadership Directory:** Unit representatives and committee roster.
