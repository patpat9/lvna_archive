    Cal Trans to Present Lawrence/101 Project
    
    Due to growing demand within Lakewood Village that resi-
    dents be kept informed on matters which affect them, Cal Trans
    is hosting a local open house to share information about their
    plans for the Lawrence and Highway 101 traffic project.
    
    The open house will take place on Wednesday, December
    15 at the Lakewood Park Center on Lakechime at Silverlake.
    Come any time between 5:00 and 9:00 P.M.
    
    Maps and exhibits will be displayed, and Cal Trans staff
    will be available to answer questions. The open house format
    makes it easy for everybody to stop by for just a few minutes at
    their convenience, either before or after the Holiday Lighting
    Contest.
    
    For more information on the open house, contact David Sal-
    lady at Cal Trans at 452-7233. |
    
    Countdown to the Holiday Parade
    
    The Second Annual Holiday Parade will be here in just a
    few days. The participants have been notified and the officials
    are doing the "Lady Di Wave." The parade will be held this Sat-
    urday, December 11, beginning promptly at 10:00 A.M. The
    route from Fairwood School to Lakewood Park travels over Fair-
    wood, Sandia, Lakehaven, Meadowlake, and Lakechime.
    
    We are once again asking our neighbors to bring holiday re-
    freshments and help with the toy drive. Last year proved to be a
    great success with over $500 worth of toys donated to Sunnyvale
    Community Services. We hope to top that number this year.
    
    So bring your goodies to the park building by 9:00 A.M.
    Then take your seat along the parade route and enjoy our com-
    munity spirit and pride. Invite everyone you talk to to come out
    and have fun! a

    Neighborhood Watch
    
    Public Safety
    from Jeremy Watney, Sunnyvale Public Safety Officer
    
    This is just a brief reminder from the Sunnyvale Department
    of Public Safety to make sure you lock your doors and win-
    dows when you go out. Over half of the burglaries commit-
    ted each year in Sunnyvale require little or no force by the
    burglars to get in. The most frequent method of entry is
    through an open window.
    
    No matter how little time you plan on spending away from
    the house, please always lock your doors and windows when
    you leave. This way, your possessions will not become a
    burglar's Christmas presents. Have a safe holiday season.
    
    Announcements
    
    There is no monthly meeting in December except the Holiday
    Parade. The next evening meeting is scheduled for January 20 at
    Lakewood Park Center. The January meeting will help to set the
    priorities and budget for 1994. At this "stakeholder" meeting,
    you are invited to share your concerns, war stories, and dreams
    for our neighborhood. We want to hear your ideas.
    
    Now is the time to start thinking about nominating yourself or a
    neighbor for the 1994-1995 L.V.N.A. board. Nominations for
    board positions will come from the neighborhood at large, the
    existing board members, and the nominating committee, which
    is currently being formed. To volunteer to be on the nominating
    committee, call Paul Schaefer at 734-1680.
    
    The Sunnyvale City Council is currently seeking applicants to
    serve on the Law Enforcement Citizen Participation Commit-
    tee. The committee will serve as an advisory group to update the
    City's 5-year law enforcement policy and service document. Ap-
    plicants can represent businesses, schools, or the community at
    large. For more information, call 730-7533.
    
    The City of Sunnyvale has a program which allows residents
    who are over 60 or permanently disabled to purchase discount
    vouchers for taxi or van service. With the vouchers, local trips
    cost $2 for and extended trips cost $5 for up to 4 passengers. For
    program information and registration, call 730-7394.
    
    The L.V.N.A. would like to thank Leewards craft store for
    their donation of holiday decorations. Leewards is located on El
    Camino Real at Hollenbeck.
