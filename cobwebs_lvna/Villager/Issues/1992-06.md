    DATELINE LAKEWOOD:
    
    PARTNER’S PROGRAM GROWING
    
    Stunning reports are coming in from the field indicating a
    high level of support for the Partners Program. Since the
    extremely successful clean-up/B-BO event held on April 25th
    to kick off Partners, members of the City of Sunnyvale team
    have distributed information packets to all of the homes in
    Lakewood. These packets contain helpful information on
    various city programs and ordinances designed to assist in
    the improvement of the neighborhood
    
    Prior to the kick off event, a survey was made of all the
    homes in the Lakewood Neighborhood to get some idea of
    what conditions existed in the community. In the few weeks
    
    since the packets were distributed almost 30% of you folks
    have responded in a positive way by reviewing the
    information and taking action by cutting weeds, storing
    trash cans or removing debris from your front yards. This is a
    great beginning and we hope that more of you will “Jump On
    The Band-Wagon".
    
    The City Field Staff are now in the process of visiting
    individual residents and attempting to resolve the remaining
    property maintenance problems. Partners is designed to
    Focus on property improvement issues that are visible from
    the street and have an impact on the appearance of the
    neighborhood, The specialists will be requesting, where
    appropriate, that property owners paint their homes, repair
    fences, remove weeds and debris, also store their trash
    containers properly. Public Safety has been and will
    continue to enforce the parking regulations for inoperable
    vehicles or vehicles parked on the lawns. You may want to
    check the orange sheet on Parking Regulations in your
    information packet and make sure you do not have a
    problem with the vehicles at your house.
