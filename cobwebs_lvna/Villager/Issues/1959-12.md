## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **'L.V. Lights Up!':** Second Annual Yule Lighting Contest winners; three collaborative families take first prize.
    - **'The Village Squares':** Square dance club elections; Warren Cross elected president.
    - **Mac's Hardware:** Holiday lighting and decorative hardware.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Aquatic Club Status:** Update on construction progress at the community cabana club.
    - **President's Message:** Celebrating the formal grand opening of Dick's Lakewood Shopping Center!
    - **Letters to the Editor:** Traffic control and pedestrian courtesies around the shopping plaza.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Church Services:** Holiday services at Lakewood Methodist and Baptist churches.
    - **Classifieds & Services:** Babysitting exchanges, appliance repairs, and electrical services.
