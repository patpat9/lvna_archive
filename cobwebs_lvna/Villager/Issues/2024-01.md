    Natural Grass Versus Artificial Turf - February 4 (3:00-4:00 PM)
    Susan Hinton, Chair of the Sierra Club Loma Prieta Chapter’s Plastic Pollution Prevention Team, will be
    speaking at the Cupertino Library on Sunday, February 4 from 3:00-4:00pm. She will share an
    informative presentation comparing natural grass and artificial turf. Those who follow sports have
    heard professional athletes and others debate over whether grass or turf is safer. Arguments have
    been made over how one might save water, make for a better playing experience, be better for the
    environment, and be used for activities regardless of weather. Similar reasoning regarding
    landscaping projects has been made, especially for heat and water usage. Come learn about the health
    risks and environmental impacts of artificial turf and turf alternatives. No reservation required. The
    Cupertino Library is located at 10800 Torre Avenue. More information available at this link.

    Join the LVNA Board
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new
    neighbors? Would you be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your
    friends, and the next generation who’s currently growing up right outside your door or even in your
    own backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice
    President, Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in April and a new board will
    begin. The board meets once per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to holding quarterly
    neighborhood general meetings and a few seasonal events each year. The level of commitment is up
    to each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is a one-year term (April 2024 - March 2025). The
    only requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the neighborhood, and are able to serve the
    full 12 month term. Elections are April 10 at 7:00pm in the Lakewood Park Building. For more
    information, please email board@lvnasv.org

    SEE members have been giving feedback on the preliminary map scenarios and drawing original
    maps with the Maptitude software, available at https://www.fuhsd.org/newsroom/trustee-areas/maps.
    Preliminary map scenario 1 shows Sunnyvale split with a northern and southern trustee area. Map
    scenarios 2-4 show east and west trustee areas, a vertical split of Sunnyvale.  
    There is growing interest in calling North Sunnyvale (94085 and 94089) as a “Community of Interest”
    (COI) that should be kept together. We have a common hardship in distance to high school from our
    residences and a common interest In better transportation. Our area is where the now-closed
    Sunnyvale High School used to be. We’ve never had trustees from this part of town and after this
    districting process, we will always have dedicated representation.
    If you would like to give input about the maps and express your opinion that North Sunnyvale or
    other areas should be determined COI, please write trusteeareas@fuhsd.org

    Calkids - The First Step Toward College
    The CalKIDS program is intended to help kids in California access
    higher education. The state automatically provides a deposit for eligible
    beneficiaries, and the program requires no financial contribution by
    parents. Here are some key details about the program:
    All babies born on or after July 1, 2022 are eligible for CalKIDS, regardless of parent income. Babies
    born between July 1, 2022 and June 30, 2023 receive a $25 initial deposit. Babies born on or after July 1,
    2023 receive a $100 initial deposit. 
    Children in grades 1-12 are eligible if they were attended public school and were identified as low-
    income under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) during the 2021-2022 school year. $500 is
    available to eligible kids. 
    CalKIDS will hold a virtual information session on Tuesday, January 16 at 11:00am. To read more details
    and register for the session, go to this zoom link. 
    To access your CalKIDS account, register at this link.  
    More information can be found here at the CalKIDS website.
