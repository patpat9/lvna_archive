    MEET YOUR NEIGHBOR
    QUYEN LY
    Q: Tell us a bit about yourself and your family.
    Quyen: My name is Quyen. I live with my husband 
    and 2 daughters. We enjoy going to parks, bike 
    riding, and spending time with family and friends. 
    We love to read books and learn about animals, 
    famous people, how things work, and other topics.
    Q: When did you move to the neighborhood, and 
    what road do you live on?
    Quyen: We moved here in 2009. We live on Prescott 
    Ave.
    Q: Name one thing you see out of your kitchen 
    window.
    Quyen: Our orange tree. We have an orange tree 
    that gives delicious fruit.
    Q: Something that is in your front yard?
    Quyen: We have a persimmon tree that is in our front 
    yard. 
    Q: A sound you hear frequently in the 
    neighborhood?
    Quyen: I hear birds chirping.
    Q: What is something you like about living in 
    Lakewood/Fairwood?
    Quyen:  I like the neighbors that we have met over 
    the years. We are able to help each other out and 
    care for one another.  
    Q: And something that has changed since you 
    moved to the neighborhood?
    Quyen: At Fairwood Park, the big kids’ playground 
    changed into an obstacle course-like playground.
    Q: What are your favorite parks/things to do in 
    Sunnyvale and/or the Bay Area?
    Quyen: We like Fairwood Park and Lakewood Park 
    since they are both close to our home. We enjoy 
    playing at Baylands Park, since there is also Animal 
    Assisted Happiness Farm there. My favorite park is 
    Magical Bridge Playground in Palo Alto. I call it the 
    Disney World of playgrounds. I look forward to the 
    opening of Magical Bridge Playground on Fair Oaks 
    Ave. As a family, we enjoy going to different parks and 
    biking with friends. Also, I enjoy taking the girls to the 
    different libraries.
    Q: Tell me a bit about your hobbies.
    Quyen: I enjoy doing puzzles as a hobby. I was doing 
    one and thought it would be great to have another 
    puzzle to swap or borrow. So, I started The Little Free 
    Puzzle Library at my home. Anyone can take a puzzle, 
    return, or pass the puzzle to another person. Also, 
    during the pandemic, I started making felt bracelets 
    and stuffed dolls for my kids and other friends. It was a 
    relaxing and rewarding experience.
    Q: Any great stories to share about living in the 
    neighborhood?
    Quyen: I remember our neighbor, Al, who lived on our 
    street. He was about 98 yrs (?) and rode an electric 
    tricycle to Walmart and back. Whenever I saw him 
    riding, it encouraged me to stay fit.
