    VILLAGER HISTORY
    If you’re new to the neighborhood – Welcome!!! In case 
    you haven’t seen one of these before, this is the Villager, 
    Lakewood Village’s neighborhood newsletter. A new edition 
    is published each month and is now in the 64th year of 
    publication! Neighborhood volunteers used to hand-deliver 
    a copy to each of the 1,614 homes in the neighborhood, 
    every month, until the operation went digital in 2019. We’re 
    kicking off 2021 with a one-time paper delivery for nostalgic 
    reasons (2021 deserves a fresh start) and to spread the word 
    to those who may not already be subscribed to the digital 
    publication or follow the Nextdoor LVNA group.
    Since this is a one-time paper delivery, we encourage you to

    RECIPE CORNER
    Peppermint Mint Chip Cookies
    3/4 CUP butter, unsalted
    1/2 CUP light brown sugar
    1/8 CUP white sugar
    1 large egg, room temp
    2 TSP vanilla extract
    1 TSP peppermint extract
    2 CUPS all-purpose flour
    2 TSP cornstarch
    1 TSP baking soda
    1/4 TSP salt
    1 CUP Andes mints, chopped
    1.	 Use a hand-held mixer or stand mixer to cream together butter and 
    sugar, until light and fluffy. Add egg, vanilla extract, and peppermint 
    extract. Mix well and scrape bowl as needed.
    2.	 In a separate bowl, combine flour, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt.
    3.	 Pour dry ingredients into butter/egg mixture and mix on low until 
    fully combined. Add Andes mints and combine into cookie dough.
    4.	 Cover dough with plastic wrap and chill for at least 1 hour, or 
    overnight. 
    5.	 Before rolling out cookie dough, remove from fridge and let it sit 
    on the counter for 10 minutes. Preheat oven to 350 °F. Use large 
    ice cream scoop to make similar-sized dough blobs before rolling 
    between your hands to form a ball. 
    6.	 Add dough balls onto parchment-lined baking sheet, leaving some 
    room between each cookie.
    7.	 Bake in 350 °F oven for about 12 minutes. Let them cool and enjoy!
