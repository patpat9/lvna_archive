    *For the best selection, purchase spring bulbs as
    soon as they come on the market. Shop for native
    plants, trees, and shrubs. Do not buy or plant frost-
    tender plants such as citrus and bougainvillea (wait
    until next spring).
    frQontinue to weed. Addyoung seedhead-free

    pile. Remember to keep the pile as damp as a wnmg-

    spray from the hose to wash away aphids. Ants are
    likely to become a problem soon. Discourage them
    by picking up fallen fruit and sealing offcracks that

    winter, but do fertilize all flowering plants flrat are
    about to bloom. Fertilize roses with a low-nitrogen

    fertilize most antique roses.
    *pinctr off dead flowers. Of course, if you wish to
