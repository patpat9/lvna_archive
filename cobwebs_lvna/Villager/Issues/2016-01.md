    Greetings Neighbors!   
     
    The holidays have come and gone and 
    Lakewood Village has been blessed with 
    rainy weather.  After a long dry spell that 
    has lasted several years, it looks like Mother Nature 
    is doing something to rescue our dying lawns and 
    our thirsty roses.  I'm afraid I  prefer sunny weather 
    and I don't like this cold, dark, rainy time of year.  
    Still, one can't enjoy the warm sun as much if one 
    doesn't get to experience its absence once in a 
    while.  Luckily, it won't be long before Spring is here 
    again. 
     
    February is famous for a number of events of its 
    own.  We'll soon have Groundhog Day, Valentine’s 
    Day, President's day (celebrating both presidents 
    Lincoln and Washington who were born in February) 
    and Mardi Gras in New Orleans.  We'll also have 
    another uniquely American special event:  The Super 
    Bowl. 
     
    We'll all become very aware of 
    the Super Bowl this 
    time around, even 
    if we are not  
    necessarily fans of   
    Professional      
    Football. We won't 
    be able to avoid it 
    because it will be literally  happening 
    on our front door step. On that special 
    Sunday in February, our  area will  
    become the focus of the entire sporting 
    world as the Super Bowl is played in  
    Levi Stadium, just a short walk from 
    Lakewood Village. (Cont. on Page 2)

    Flood and Storm Safety 
     
    By Phyllis Fowler 
     
    Lakewood Village in the past has had flooding 
    issues. I have seen Lakebird and Silverlake 
    flooded several times from the storm drains 
    backing up. We have also seen flooding on the 
    Fairwood side from Calabasas creek. Until the 
    work is finished on the West side creek, we will continue to be a 
    high risk flooding area. Here is some helpful information I got 
    from the Sunnyvale.ca.gov website. 
     
    Living in the Flood Zone 
    There are many considerations to living in a flood zone.. 
    Some things you can do to protect yourself and your 
    property are: 
     
    Develop an emergency plan and acquire supplies which 
    may be needed in event of a natural disaster such as 
    flooding, earthquake, or major water outage. Know how 
    to shut-off the electricity and gas if needed.  
     
       Who Should I Call to Report Storm Hazards? 
     
     
    How Can I Prepare for Wet Weather? 
     
     Develop an emergency plan. 
     Put together an emergency supply kit. 
     Store drinking water in closed, clean containers  
      (One gallon per person per day for at least three days). 
     Know how to shut-off the electricity and gas if  
          needed. 
     Make proactive changes to protect your property 
     Raise electrical panels, installing sewer backflow   
          devices. 
     Be aware of low-elevation spots that could flood.

    What if My Home Floods During a Storm… 
     
     
    If water has entered a garage or basement, do not 
    walk through it. 
     
    If you are asked to leave your property, disconnect 
    all electrical appliances. 
     
    Tune to local radio and TV channels 
    for emergency advisories and     
    instructions. 
     
    Avoid downed power lines and   
    broken gas lines. 
     
    Do not drive through standing 
    water!

    After a Storm… 
     
     
    Avoid direct contact with floodwater. 
     
    Just 6 inches for moving water can knock you 
    down! 
     
    Follow local instructions regarding the safety 
    of drinking water. 
     
    Do not use fresh foods or canned goods that 
    have come in contact with flood-
    waters. 
     
    Flooded buildings should be 
    pumped out,  
          disinfected and dried as quickly 
    as possible to   
          prevent mold. 
     
    DO NOT turn gas back on  
           yourself.  Call PG&E at  
           800-743-5000. 
     
    Do not handle live electrical equipment in 
    wet areas!!!

    Living in the Flood Zone 
     
    Flood insurance is required for all properties within a Spe-
    cial Flood Hazard Areas (SFHA). A large portion of the 
    Lakewood side falls under the “High Risk for Flooding” 
    zone. (including my house). This means homes with feder-
    ally regulated mortgages are required to have flood insur-
    ance. However, renters, commercial property owners and 
    properties outside of the SFHA can also purchase flood in-
    surance. Remember standard property insurance does not 
    cover flood damage losses. There is a 30 day waiting period 
    before flood insurance goes into effect. If you are interested 
    in buying flood insurance, contact your insurance agent and 
    they can help you.

    Aggravated Assault – 1100 Blk. Ayala 
    Tuesday December 8th Time:  10:36 pm  
    Officers responded to a reported gang fight involving 30 
    subjects, and that bats and chains were being used in the 
    flight.  Upon arrival the victim was located with a laceration to 
    his head and was treated at the scene.  One suspect was 
    arrested for an outstanding warrant and two other suspects 
    fled and were arrested for resisting arrest. 
      
    Fraud – 600 Blk. S. Bernardo 
    Thursday December 10th Time:  9:51 am 
    Officers responded on a reported fraud in progress.  The 
    suspects had fled the scene, but officers located them driving 
    in the area.  Through an extensive investigation, officers 
    determined the suspect were in possession of close to 
    $40,000 in fraudulently obtained gift cards and 50 fraudulent 
    credit cards.  It was determined the suspects were involved in 
    transactions at various locations in Sunnyvale, San Jose, and 
    Morgan Hill.  All three suspects were arrested and the 
    investigation is continuing. 
     
    Major Injury Accident/DUI – Caribbean & Moffett Park  
    Saturday December 26th Time: 6:11 am 
    DPS personnel responded to the intersection of Caribbean & 
    Moffett Park on a report of a major injury accident.  Upon 
    arrival, officers located a single vehicle that had struck a 
    stationary object and the sole occupant suffered serious 
    injuries and was lying in the roadway.  The subject was 
    transported to the hospital and underwent emergency 
    surgery.   
     
    Fraud – 1077 E. Arques   
    Monday December 28th Time: 4:04 pm 
    Fry’s security reported a subject attempting 
    to make a large purchase with a fraudulent 
    credit card.  Officers determined the 
    suspect was in possession of a false 
    driver’s license and 30 fraudulent credit 
    cards.  The suspect was also in possession 
    of a device used to alter credit cards.  The suspect was
