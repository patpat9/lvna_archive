    The conversation that resulted was very interesting and
    I'm sure will prove to be very fruitful in the long run. Here
    are some of the highlights:
    WE NEED TO BE MORE "PARENT FRIENDLY'
    Just about everybody in the neighborhood reads the
    newsletter each month, but only a few people make it to
    the monthly meetings. Part of the reason is that the
    meetings happen in the evening on a Wednesday in the
    Lakewood park building. We've heard that there are many
    young parents in the neighborhood who can't get to the
    meetings because they coincide with getting their young
    children into bed. Others have conflicts because some
    PTA meetings happen at the same time.
    The LVNA needs to speak for everyone in Lakewood
    Village, and especially for parents of young children. They
    will be affected by the various issues we've worked on
    (closing the swimming pool, building the library, traffic
    safety, etc.). We need to find a way to give them a better
    opportunity to take part.
    , 
    ,. ., 
    :,
    One of the ideas the board has is to organize some kind
    of regular meeting at a different time of day and a different
    location that would be more convenient for parents of
    young children to attend.
    Can you think of away to give these families more of an
    opportunity to participate in the LVNA? Please let us
    know! Either send your ideas to the newsletter editor or
    send them to me. My e-mail address is
    fmfrainc@omail.com.
    THE LVNA NEEDS TO EXPAND INTO "SOCIAL MEDIA''
    Our newsletter is the largest and most widely distributed
    neighborhood newsletter in Sunnyvale. We print and
    distribute more than '1,650 six-page copies each month.
    The closest competitor is the SNAIL neighborhood
    association, which distributes about 1,200 four-page
    newsletters monthly.
    The newsletter has its drawbacks, though. Sometimes
    important events take place just after it is published, or
    else city issues come up and are decided before there is
    time to publish anything about them.
    One of the benefits of using internet tools such as
    Facebook and Twitter is that information can be distributed
    quickly.
    We know that social movements around the world use
    these tools to organize themselves. Perhaps we should
    give them a try and see what they can do for our
    neighborhood.
    One person who wants to try is our former 2nd Vice
    President, D. D. Lloyd. She has volunteered to set up an
    LVNA Facebook page. Our new 2no Vice President Pat
    Muniz wants to work on this with D. D.
    We are not sure how this will work as we are not very
    experienced with these tools. Do you have any
    suggestions? lt would be great if D. D. and Pat could
    form a kind of "task force" to work on this idea. Would
    you like to contribute? Please let me know at my e-mail
    address, fmfainc@qmail.com.
