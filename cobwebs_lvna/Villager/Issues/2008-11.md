    It is finally starting to feel a little like fall. The year's end is
    coming soon, and I hope that many of you will be able to
    attend the Holiday Parade on December 13th. We have a
    lot of great participants, and it will be a fantastic event.
    The parade will start from Lakewood Elementary at
    11:00AM and travel down Lakehaven and Sandia to Fair-
    wood Elementary. You can watch from any place in be-
    tween. Please join us at Fairwood Park afterwards for hot
    cider, cookies, and music by the Fremont School Choir.
    There will also be a toy drive in conjunction with the
    Sunnyvale Community Center. There will be people
    collecting toys along the parade route if that is more
    convenient, and there will be a collection point at Fairuvood
    Park after the parade.
    You will also have the opportunity to have your picture
    taken with Santa after the parade in Fairwood Park. Pho-
    tos will be $5 apiece.
    lwant to also invite those of you who are interested in par-
    ticipating in the neighborhood to consider the two positions
    available in LVNA; we are still looking for a first vice
    president and secretary. These positions do not require a
    Iot of time, but are important to the functionality of LVNA.
    As a special note, I want to mention something that came
    to my attention last week. We have had residents of
    Lakewood Village make endorsements of a political
    candidate using the name of Lakewood Village Neighbor-
    hood Association. Please note that LVNA does not en-
    dorse candidates or take political positions. We create a
    forum for active conversation and give any willing candi-
    date equal time to explain their positions, but do not en-
    dorse candidates.
    I think it is important for us to remember the purpose and
    goals of LVNA. We, as a neighborhood, work to enhance
    the quality of life, promote safety, and foster unity in our
    community. There are many different ways to achieve this,
    and as a neighborhood I hope that we can work toward
    these goals.
