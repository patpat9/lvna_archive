## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Executive Board Report:** Junior Chamber of Commerce (Jaycees) civic partnership.
    - **Aquatic Club Charter Memberships:** Final deadline for founding family memberships in the Lakewood Village Aquatic Club.
    - **'It's the Law':** Sunnyvale municipal weed abatement ordinance guidelines to reduce summer fire hazards.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Home Improvement:** Catalina aluminum screen doors and home maintenance.
    - **Vice-Chairman's Message:** Recognizing active community contributors and volunteers.
- **Pages 7-10:**
    - **'The Village Squares':** Charlie Lewis reports on square dancing club progress.
    - **New Minister at Lakewood Methodist:** Rev. George E. Weber appointed pastor at 1251 Sandia Avenue.
    - **Directory:** LVRA officers and unit representatives.
