## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Autumn in Lakewood Village:** Fall leaf cleanup, gardening preparations, and holiday preview.
    - **Mac's Hardware:** Autumn lawn supplies and tools.
    - **The Stork Club:** Twin girls born to Don Azzinari on Blazingwood; baby girl to Benjam Rivas on Pecos Way.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Stanley Home Products:** Alice E. Hamilton on Hiddenlake Drive; street tree preservation.
    - **Lakewood School PTA:** First general business meeting of the new school year.
    - **Local Business:** Charlie's Bike & Hobby Shop on Lawrence Station Rd; Lakewood Beauty Salon specials.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Religious Directory:** St. Mark Lutheran, Lakewood Methodist, and Lakewood Baptist.
    - **Franklin 5 & 10 Department Store:** Local shopping promotions.
