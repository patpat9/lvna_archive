    President’s Column 
     
    -Phyllis Fowler 
     
    Greetings!   
    Please come to the Lakewood Park 
    building on Wednesday, March 8th to 
    take part in the next general meeting 
    of the LVNA.  The meeting will start at 7:00pm, and it 
    is an important one! 
     
    The first order of business will be the annual election 
    of our officers.  The LVNA bylaws stipulate that the 
    organization will be governed by a committee of five 
    people serving in different capacities.  There is a  
    President, who bears overall responsibility for the  
    association.  There is a “1st Vice President”, who  
    presides if the president cannot and also is responsible 
    for overseeing publicity for the association.  There is a 
    “2nd Vice President” who presides if both the  
    president and the 1st Vice President cannot and 
    chairs something called the “Grievance committee”.  
    There is a “Secretary” who keeps the records of the 
    organization, and there is a “Treasurer” who handles 
    the money and keeps the organization's books.  Each 
    officer serves a one-year term after being elected, and 
    no one can serve as an elected officer for more than 
    three consecutive terms. 
     
    The board will also need to bill and collect from the 
    newsletter advertisers. The money collected helps to 
    pay for printing the newsletter and paying for the  
    different events we have in the neighborhood.  
     
    This year we will have a bit of a challenge.  The LVNA 
    currently has a president (me), a 2nd vice president 
    (Aubrey Tamez), and a treasurer (Fred Fowler). We 
    have had not had a 1st vice president or Secretary for 
    some time. Fred volunteered to be treasurer only so 
    that could continue as a board,.  Fred and I are  
    finishing our third term on the LVNA board and must 
    step down. Aubrey will also be stepping down. 
    Fred has been busy with his business and it had fallen 
    to Aubrey and I to run the board, meetings and events. 
    As it turns out; the LVNA board cannot function with just 
    two officers.  The Bylaws (which serve as the LVNA's 
    “constitution”) require that board meetings must have a 
    minimum of three officers present in order to conduct 
    business.  If we don't have three elected officers, we 
    can't make any decisions legally.  Without three officers, 
    we won't be able legally to print this newsletter, organize 
    Dumpster Day, apply for city grant funds, or engage in 
    just about any other activity. 
     
    I am also stepping down from being the editor of the 
    Villager. It will be up to the new board and editor if the 
    newsletter will continue.  
     
    We need some new blood to serve on the LVNA board!  
    If no one volunteers to be on the board, the LVNA 
    will cease to exist! 
     As of this date, no one has expressed an interest to 
    serve on the board.  I ask YOU to think about it!  The 
    main commitment you would have to make is to attend 
    and run the LVNA board meetings, which happen once 
    per month.  Other than that, the amount of time and  
    energy you will have to contribute is completely up to 
    you. 
     
    Over the years the LVNA has made a big difference for 
    our neighborhood.  From the graffiti wars of the 1990's 
    to the picnics, Dumpster Day and parades, the LVNA 
    has contributed a great deal toward making Lakewood 
    Village a wonderful place to call home.  The           
    neighborhood needs the LVNA.  And now, the LVNA 
    needs you. 
     
    If you are interested in running for the LVNA Board or 
    Editor, please feel free to contact me at  
    sunnyphyl@gmail.com .   
    I'll be happy to answer any questions you have, and 
    make sure your name is on the nominations list.   
    Thank you!

    Police Blotter 
     
     
    Haz Mat – 1200 Blk. Lakeside 
    Friday February 10th @ 8:37pm 
    DPS responded to an incident in which mercury was spilled 
    at a commercial location. DPS personnel contained and 
    collected the substance and there were no reported 
    exposures or injuries.  
     
    Gas Investigation – 500 Blk. E. Remington 
    Saturday February 11th @ 9:19am 
    DPS responded on a report of a gas leak at the Community 
    Center Theater. The theater was evacuated and the 
    building ventilated. PG&E responded and repaired the leak. 
    Patrons were allowed back into the building after it was 
    deemed safe to enter. 
     
    Wires Down & Residential Fires – 500 Blk. N. Eden, 
    Friday February 17th @ 9:11am 
    DPS responded on a report of wires down. Upon arrival, 
    crews located high voltage electrical lines had fallen on an 
    outside shed and the roof of the shed was 
    smoldering. While waiting for PG&E to respond to shut off 
    the power, residents across the street reported their 
    houses were filling with smoke. Crews located small fires 
    within the walls of the residences due to a power 
    surge. The fires were extinguished and did not spread 
    beyond the walls. PG&E arrived to disconnect power to 
    both sides of the street.  
     
    Monday February 20th 
    DPS (Police, Fire and Dispatch), along with Department of 
    Public Works crews were very busy throughout the day 
    dealing with the effects of the storm.  At one point, 
    personnel were dealing with 30 intersections being 
    reported as flooded, in addition to numerous downed trees 
    and power lines. 
     
    Residential Burglary – 700 Blk. San Miguel 
    Thursday February 23rd @ 3:44pm 
    A residential burglary was interrupted by the 
    homeowner.  The homeowner chased one of the suspects 
    and caught him at a nearby school.  All three suspects, one 
    adult and two juveniles, were arrested.  
     
    Vehicle Accident & Rescue – 800 Blk. E. El Camino 
    Monday February 27th @ 6:22pm 
    DPS responded on a report of a vehicle that rolled over in a 
    parking lot.  Crews stabilized the vehicle and removed the 
    occupant who sustained minor injuries.  
     
    Fire – 900 Blk. Corte Madera  
    Monday February 27th @ 6:31pm 
    DPS responded on a report of a structure fire.  Upon 
    arrival, it was determined to be a fire in the 
    chimney.  Crews extinguished the fire which was contained 
    to the chimney.
