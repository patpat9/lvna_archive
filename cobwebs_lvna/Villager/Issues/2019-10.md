    RECIPE CORNER
    PUMPKIN CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIES
    Have a recipe that you love? 
    Share it with us at editor@lvnasv.org
    1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking trays with parchment 
    paper or silicone baking mats.
    2. In a bowl or standing mixer, combine pumpkin puree, 
    sugar, oil, vanilla, and egg. Mix until well combined. 
    3. In a separate bowl, add flour, baking powder, baking 
    soda, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, and salt. Mix 
    together. 
    4. Slowly incorporate dry ingredients into wet mixture. Mix 
    well but do not over-mix.
    5. Add in chocolate chips and stir until evenly combined.
    6. Using a large cookie scoop, drop scoops of cookie dough 
    onto the lined baking trays. Be sure to leave approx 2 
    inches of space around each scoop.
    7. Bake for 14 minutes. Allow the cookies to cool. 
    2/3 CUP white sugar
    1/2 CUP vegetable oil
    1 CUP pumpkin puree
    1 large egg
    1 TSP vanilla extract
    2 CUPS flour
    yields approx 18 cookies
    2 TSP baking powder
    1 TSP baking soda
    1 TSP cinnamon
    1/2 TSP pumpkin pie spice
    1/2 TSP salt
    2/3 CUP (or more) semi-sweet 		
    chocolate chips
