    Another pair of "community oriented businesses" are two
    new advertisers, "lsabella Boutique" and "Pan African City
    Alive". Both of these businesses are located in Downtown
    Sunnyvale and are fairly far away from Lakewood Village.
    Never the less both of them want to build ties with our
    community and are reaching out to us.
    lsabella Boutique is a smallshop located in the 100 block
    of South Murphy Avenue, otherwise known as the historic
    Downtown of Sunnyvale. lt is run by a wonderful lady
    named Rosimarie Osorio. Rosimarie actually lived in
    Lakewood Village for many years and has missed it ever
    since she moved away. Her store is full of interesting
    clothing, accessories and gifts of all kinds. When we had
    our picnic last September, Rosimarie donated several of the
    door prizes we gave away. She sees our community as part
    of her own "neighborhood" and is trying to reach out to us.
    She placed her first advertisement with us this month.
    Pan African City Alive is another fascinating store and is
    located at the corner of South Sunnyvale Avenue and
    Evelyn Avenue is downtown Sunnyvale. Many people have
    heard the store even if they haven't seen it - every Saturday
    morning Pan African sponsors an outdoor concert of African
    music that Phyllis and I have enjoyed
    several times. Stepping inside the store is
    like stepping into an "adventurer's club" full
    of artifacts from the faraway "Dark
    Continent". I love African carvings and the
    store has them in abundance. They also
    have books, clothing, jewelry and even
    cosmetics from African sources, often
    produced by villagers in traditionalways.
    More interesting than the store, though, is
    the lady who runs it. She has a strong sense of community
    and is attracted by the sense of the "together" spirit of
    Lakewood Village. She donated several containers of
    authentic Shea butter as door prizes for our picnic and
    placed her first ad in The Villaqer three months ago. She
    just renewed her color ad with us for the next three months.
    She wants to build a cultural bridge and hopes that the
    Lakewood Village community might prove to be one of the
    bridge's anchors.

    Disposal of Pharmaceuticals
    ln January, we had a Public Safety officer visit our
    meeting. He came to inform us about a program called
    MedDrop. For those of you who were not able to attend
    January's meeting, here is the
    information.
    MedDrop is a pharmaceutical disposal
    program designed to encourage
    environmental ly-safe d isposal of
    unneeded, expired and unused
    prescription and over-the-cou nter
    medicines.
    The Problem
    Traditionally, many people simply flush old, unwanted
    medicines down a toilet. Unfortunately, some of these
    pharmaceuticals have found their way into the
    environment because they have not been removed during
    the waste treatment process. These pharmaceuttcals can
    have a lasting negative effect on plant and animal life.
    The Solution
    The city of Sunnyvale has created the MedDrop program,
    a program that encourages resident to drop off their
    unwanted medicines at any Sunnyvale fire station for
    environmentally-safe Disposal.
    What Kind Of Medicine ls Accepted?
    MedDrop will accept all a resident's prescription and
    over-the-counter medicines. lllegal narcotics are not
    accepted, and MedDrop cannot accept "sharps." Sharps
    are needles and lancets. However, they can be dropped
    off at the Sunnyvale Smart Station at 301 Carl Road, off
    Caribbean Drive, just look for the "red" dumpster or visit
    www.earth9l 1.com to find a nearby site.
    How Do lUs MedDrop?
    Visit any of the six Sunnyvale fire stations and give your
    medicines to an officer on duty. Please do not simply leave
    the medicines at an unattended station: medicines must be
    given to an officer.
    What Happens Then?
    The receiving officer will log in your medicines and place them
    into secure storage. The medicines willthem be turned over
    to a licensed waste disposalfirm which will destroy the
    medicines in an environmentally-safe manner.
    What lf I Have Move Questions?
