    NEW - COLUMBIA NEIGHBORHOOD SERVICE CENTER
    
    Would you like to know more about the new Columbia
    Neighborhood Service Center which is proposed by the City of
    Sunnyvale and the Sunnyvale School District? Have you
    wondered why Advanced Micro Devices, Inc, donated one
    million dollars towards this project? Well, at the June 16, 1994
    meeting of the LVNA, Chief Regan Williams (Sunnyvale
    Department of Public Safety) and Elaine Curran (Administrative
    Assistant, City of Sunnyvale, Office of the City Manager) and a
    representative of the Sunnyvale School District will be making
    a presentation on this new neighborhood service center. The
    center will be located at Columbia Middle School and will offer
    a wide variety of services to the community, including
    recreation, social services, juvenile diversion, and educational
    programs. There will be an opportunity to ask questions and to
    provide input into the planning of the new center. Do not miss
    this opportunity to learn more about your neighborhood.
    
    Mission College Project Update
    
    Matthew Castrigno 734-5442
    
    As of press time the Final Environmental Impact Report (EIR)
    has not been released for this project. It is expected that the EIR
    will be released during the second week in June. The earliest
    date for the Public hearing at the Santa Clara Planning
    Commission is June 22. The Santa Clara Planning
    Commission meetings are held in the Santa Clara City Council
    Chambers at 1500 Warburton Drive. Santa Clara Planning can
    be called for more up to date information at 984-3111.
    
    In its formal response to the Draft EIR the City of Sunnyvale
    has indicated that it wants the final EIR to include a Wildwood -
    Lawrence intersection as a mitigation option. The City of Santa
    Clara has been provided a copy of the feasibility study done on
    such an intersection. This feasibility study is the one
    commissioned by the City of Sunnyvale to study the Lawrence -
    Wildwood intersection proposals made to the City Council in
    January by Matthew Castrigno. (see February & April issues of
    LVNA Villager). By presenting this information to the City of
    Santa Clara prior to the release of the EIR the City of
    Sunnyvale has an avenue of legal recourse should the
    intersection not be included as a mitigation option.

    FAIRWOOD PARK UPDATE
    
    Matthew Castrigno 734-5442
    
    The Fairwood Park improvement project continues to move
    forward. The plans went out for bid the first week of June.
    Once this step is taken construction can be expected to start
    between two to four months from that point. The results of the
    biding process will be known by the end of June. At that point
    it will be known if the project will include improving part of
    the Hetch Hechy right of way.( see April Villager for details).
    Robert Walker, Superintendent of Parks, explained that contract
    covers a 210 day period which contains approximately four
    months of construction and three months of plant establishment.
    Mr. Walker cautioned that this schedule is optimistic as it
    depends on many factors outside of the control of the City
    including the weather. During the plant establishment phase the
    park will appear completed but will remain fenced off as the
    plants get a chance to establish themselves without the burden
    of foot traffic compacting the soil. A sign that was posted at
    the sight erroneously stated the project would be completed in
    Fall of 94. Mr. Walker indicated he has been told by reliable
    sources that the sign has been corrected. It is anticipated that the
    park will be available for use in the Spring of 95.
