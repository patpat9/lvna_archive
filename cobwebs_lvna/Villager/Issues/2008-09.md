    Flood Map Re-Zoning Question
    I fually have an answer for those of you who were won-
    dering how to be re-zoned so you don't have to pay for
    flood insurance. As I understand, if you fall into Zone B
    on the flood map, you should not need flood insurance.
    ZoneB: Areas between limits of the 100 year flood and
    500 year flood; or certain areas subjectto 100 year flood-
    ing with average depths less than one foot or where the
    contribution drainage area is less than one square mile; or
    areas protected by levees from the base flood.

    Residue from lnfected City Tree Ruining Car
    and Discoloring Street
    I have an oak tree in front of my house that is dropping a sticky
    residue on the street and on my vehicle, possibly causing damage to
    the paint and leaving a nasty wet look on the streets and sidewalk. I
    called the City of Sunnyvale Preservation Officer, Nadine, at 408-
    730-7505. The result was a note from the city indicating that the
    "sap" was actually exuded by pests called aphids. The City of
    Sunnyvale said they will no longer spray these pests due to budgetary
    reasons. They advised me to spray it myself. The city said "no" when
    I asked them to cut the tree down and replace it with a different type.
    Finally, I sprayed the affected area with water. Since then I have not
    seen as many aphid droplets on my car.
    This substance is ruining the paint on my car. The streets and side-
    walks are a mess and there is no solution in sight. Hopefully this will
    not affect my properly value. Does anyone have an alternative
    solution?
