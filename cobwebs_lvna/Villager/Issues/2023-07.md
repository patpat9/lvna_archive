    The annual LVNA Dumpster Day will take place on Saturday, July 29 from 7:00am-2:00pm.

    Dear Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association,
    I hope this message finds you all in good health and high spirits. As the President of our esteemed
    association, I am excited to bring you updates on our collective efforts to improve and beautify our
    beloved neighborhood and enhance our community.
    Quarterly Printed Newsletter: Firstly, I am delighted to announce the launch of our quarterly printed
    newsletter which you are reading, aimed at keeping our community informed about the latest news,
    initiatives, and upcoming events. This physical publication will provide an avenue for us to stay
    connected, share valuable information, and celebrate the achievements of our residents. We will
    continue to have our monthly electronic edition, however we wanted to ensure folks without access
    to the digital version could still be included. If you’d like to get our digital newsletter, I encourage all
    residents to subscribe to this newsletter on our website:
    https://lvnasv.org/villager/.

    posts so it’s an excellent way to get curated Lakewood Village-relevant
    content while filtering out the rest. Board members post reminders
    about upcoming events and moderators ensure all activity in the group
    adheres to the group’s rules (e.g., content relevant to Lakewood Village,
    no soliciting, etc.). Note that only Lakewood Village residents are

    Wanted - Help with the Villager
    LVNA is for additional support to keep the Villager going. We need a
    new Villager Editor and an additional Villager Content Creator/Writer.
    Both positions are open to anyone who is interested (no age
    requirement). With input from the LVNA Board, the Villager Editor plans
    content for each newsletter and edits it. The Villager Content
    Creator/Writer role consists of writing articles for the Villager. Articles
    can be contributed monthly or quarterly. The roles could also be
    combined into a single position if there is interest to do so.
    Any of these roles are a great opportunity to help actively drive change
    for our community and make a positive impact on our neighborhood. If
    interested, please email the board at board@lvnasv.org and let them
    know you’re on board (yes, pun fully intended).
