    Sunnyvale Schools Need Renovation Linda Kilian The Sunnyvale School District needs millions of dollars to renovate its schools. We're not talking about glamorous changes either. We're talking about new roofs, wiring, plumbing. dry rot. termite damage, bathroom tile, and the like. After a year of visiting school sites, talking with administrators, and surveying the community, the Board of Education of the Sunnyvale Elementary School District placed a bond measure on the June 4th ballot. This was a difficult decision, because passing a bond measure is not easy To win, a two-thirds majority of voters must vote in favor of the measure. One "no" vote cancels out two "yes" votes. If passed, the bond would raise $34 million. All of this money would be spent locally on our schools. None of the money could be spent on salaries. Lakewood School would receive up to $3.5 million to upgrade its facilities. Like most other schools in Sunnyvale, Lakewood is almost 40 years old. Time has taken its toll, and Lakewood needs a new roof, upgrades to the electrical system, repairs to rusted plumbing fixtures and pipes, a new fire alarm system, remodeled restrooms, new playground equipment, and more, The Sunnyvale School District has been able to renovate and reopen three schools in recent years by selling a surplus school site. However, as our student population continues to grow, selling other properties is not an option. Says Sunnyvale School Superintendent Doris Wilson, "When voters understand the condition of our schools, we feel they will see this bond measure is a good thing for the schools, for the city, and for the property values of our homes." The bond campaign organization, Friends of Sunnyvale Schools, is being co-chaired by Linda Kilian and Betty Verstegen. Area resident and Board member, Jessica Lee, will be organizing volunteers in the Lakewood area. If you have any questions about the bond measure or are interested in volunteering, call Friends of Sunnyvale Schools at (408) 450-1100. 03 It's never too late to be what you might have been.
