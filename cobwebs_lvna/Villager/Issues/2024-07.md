    Bringing Back the Lakewood Village Holiday Parade
    For anyone who has lived in Lakewood for a while, the mention of a “holiday parade” will bring back nostalgia for the
    times when Lakehaven and Sandia roads closed off to host a neighborhood parade. Santa, fire trucks, antique cars,
    cookies, elected officials, and more were included. A devoted group of Lakewood residents is trying to bring the
    parade back this year. Anyone who worked on this in the past can tell you it can be very rewarding, but takes a great
    deal of work to pull it off. Below are photos of the Fremont High School Marching Band and Lakewood Elementary
    Color Guard.
    If you want to see our holiday parade return, we need help! We need people to help with planning/staging,
    marketing, safety/security, decorations, set-up/tear-down and more. Now is the chance to sign up - we need at least
    six months of planning for this to work. 
    Let’s bring back the Lakewood Village Holiday Parade this year! Email board@lvnasv.org to help make it happen.

    The Impact of Littering in Our Neighborhood
    Littering significantly affects our neighborhood's
     cleanliness and health, creating an unsightly 
    environment and posing environmental and health risks.
    Environmental Impact
    Non-biodegradable litter, like plastic bottles and wrappers,
     takes centuries to decompose, polluting local water bodies 
    and harming wildlife, which can mistake litter for food.
    Health Hazards
    Litter can attract pests such as rats and insects,
     spreading diseases. Sharp objects like broken 
    glass can cause injuries to children and pets.
    Economic Burden
    Cleaning up litter costs local governments
     substantial taxpayer money, which could b
    e better used for community improvements 
    like parks and educational programs.
    Community Well-being
    A littered environment diminishes the
     neighborhood's aesthetic appeal, reducing 
    residents' quality of life and community spirit.
    Solutions
    Addressing littering requires community effort. Educational
     campaigns, regular clean-up events like our annua
    l dumpster day events, our Calabasas Creek clean up, mo
    re trash bins such as those that will be installed along 
    the Calabasas trail, and stricter penalties for littering can 
    help create a cleaner, healthier neighborhood. Working together, 
    we can maintain a beautiful and sustainable environment.
