    Hazardous Waste
    Did you know that hazardous waste items cannot go in your garbage bin? Hazardous waste includes anything 
    flammable, corrosive, toxic, or reactive. Common items are batteries, electronics, garden chemicals, paint, 
    household cleaners, fluorescent and LED lightbulbs, and auto fluids. Santa Clara County has a Household 
    Hazardous Waste Program for you to safely dispose of these items. There is a drop-off location in East San Jose 
    (near McKee Rd and Highway 101). Appointments are required, and we suggest making an appointment a few 
    days in advance. Visit https://www.sccgov.org/sites/rwr/hhw/Pages/hhw.aspx or call 408-299-7300 to make an 
    appointment. 
    Shoes
    Have worn out shoes to get rid of? Don’t throw them in the trash! Sports Basement in Sunnyvale (1177 Kern Ave) 
    has a bin where you can drop off old shoes for recycling. The bin is located just after you walk in the store, and 
    they take tennis shoes, flip flops, sandals, hiking shoes, etc. Call them at 408-598-4240 to confirm store hours or 
    check https://shop.sportsbasement.com/blogs/stores/sunnyvale 
    Used Eyeglasses
    Local Lions Clubs have drop boxes throughout Sunnyvale and Santa Clara to collect prescription eyeglasses 
    that are no longer used. The eyeglasses are donated to people in our community and around the world who 
    need them. Drop boxes are harder to find due to the pandemic, but we’ve confirmed one drop box location. If 
    you can’t drop it off at Kaiser, reach out to your local optometrist to see if they collect used eyeglasses.
    Kaiser Permanente Optical Services, 710 Lawrence Expressway #474, Santa Clara 408-851-4000

    CRV Recycling (Plastic, Glass, and Aluminum)
    LVNA collects CRV-eligible recyclables as a fundraising activity for the 
    neighborhood. We’ll even come pick them up! Schedule your pick-

    Suggested Tips
    Please make sure all items are clean, empty, and CRV-eligible. You should 
    see one of the following printed on the label: California Redemption 
    Value, CA Redemption Value, California Cash Refund, CA CRV, or CA Cash 
    Refund

    greatly appreciate the extra help.

    •

    RECIPE CORNER
    Roasted Cashews
    4 CUPS raw cashews
    1/2 TBSP maple syrup
    1/2 TSP ground cinnamon
    1.	 Preheat oven to 325 °F.
    2.	 Mix cashews, maple syrup, and ground cinnamon all 
    together in a bowl. 
    3.	 Place cashews on a baking tray and place in oven. Bake for 
    12 minutes. Halfway through the baking, take the tray out 
    and stir cashews around. 
    4.	 Take tray out and let cashews cool completely before 
    eating.
