    Dumpsters and Bees! 
     
    Our meeting this month is Wednesday July 
    13th at 7:00pm We will be discussing 
    Dumpster day and a special guest, Lon 
    Ong. He is a local beekeeper and would like 
    to talk to us about Honey Bees.  
     
    When I was in grade school we lived in El Sobrante, just 
    north east of Richmond.  The Sullivan’s grandfather always 
    had time to talk to the neighborhood’s kids about 
    beekeeping. Over a slice of bread spread with honey, he 
    would spin a tale about bees that fascinated us. Mr. 
    Sullivan told me when a bee starts flying around you, don’t 
    make any sudden moves or swipe at it. Just stay still or 
    move slowly away. Honeybees serve to pollinate flowers, 
    plants and fruit trees. Without them we would not have the 
    bounty of fruits and vegetables. Now, when I spread golden 
    honey on my toast; I think back to those days of watching 
    (from a distance) Mr. Sullivan take the honey laden frame 
    from the beehive.  
     
    Save our honeybees! Local beekeeper offers to relocate 
    honeybee swarms free of charge or established colonies at 
    a low cost as an alternative to pest control companies 
    which usually kill them. Come and meet Lon at the meeting 
    or contact Lon at 408-674-6580.

    Mass Apeal 
     
    by Bill C. Davis 
     
    Father Tim Farley, a lover of all good 
    things in life, is comfortable in a  
    prosperous parish. Without realizing it, 
    he has resorted to flattering his parish-
    ioners and entertaining them with sermons that skirt any  
    disturbing issues, in order to protect his Mercedes, his 
    trips abroad and the generous supply of fine wines that 
    grace his table. His well-ordered world is disrupted by the 
    arrival of Mark Dolson, an intense, hot-headed but  
    idealistic young seminarian who looks to Father Farley to 
    be his mentor, even though he challenges the older priest 
    and causes Father Farley to question his life-long choices. 
    Their confrontation, both funny and touching, examines 
    the very nature of friendship, and each man is  
    substantially changed by knowing the other

    Fire – 1000 Blk. Lakehaven 
    Wednesday June 8th Time:  2:15am 
    DPS personnel responded on a report of several large 
    trees on fire that were threatening a house and a 
    business.  Upon arrival, crews located several large juniper 
    cypress trees fully engulfed in fire.  A quick attack kept the 
    fire from spreading to the structures and was extinguished  
     
    Commercial Burglary – 1200 Blk. Oakmead 
    Friday June 10th Time:  3:57am 
    An unknown suspect broke out a window of the business 
    and attempted to pry open a safe but was unsuccessful  
     
    Gas Investigation – 100 Blk. Locksunart 
    Tuesday June 14th Time:  11:20pm 
    DPS personnel responded on a report of the smell of gas at 
    an apartment complex.  Upon arrival, crews detected high 
    concentration of sewer gas emanating from vent pipes 
    associated with four apartments.  The residents of those 
    apartments were evacuated for the evening until the hazard 
    was mitigated.  
     
    Residential Burglary – 1085 Tasman 
    Thursday June 16th Time:  8:00am – 5:30pm 
    An unknown suspect entered the residence via unknown 
    means and stole cash  
     
    Commercial Burglary – 700 Blk. Morse 
    Monday June 20th Time: 4:16am 
    Officers responded to Columbia Middle School on an alarm 
    activation.  Upon arrival, officers discovered a window 
    smash to the multi-use room.  As officers set up on the 
    school, four suspects emerged from the building and fled 
    on foot.  All four suspects were apprehended and booked 
    into County Jail. 
     
    Possession of Drugs and Burglary Tools –  
    900 Blk. W. Weddell 
    Thursday June 23th Time:  1720

    drug transaction in a hotel parking lot.  One of the subjects 
    fled on a bicycle and was ultimately arrested after jumping 
    fences into surrounding apartment complexes.  The 
    suspect was in possession of drugs and burglary tools.
