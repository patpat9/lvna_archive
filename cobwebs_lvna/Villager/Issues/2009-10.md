    20009 Picnic Photos Local Author Gets Book Published Puppy Pen Pals, a recently published book by Lake- wood Village resident Terri Silveira, is an exchange of letters between Max and Samson, two miniature schnauzers. The older puppy, Max, gives advice to the newly adopted eight-week-old puppy, Samson. In return, Samson describes first time experiences, such as getting a bath, his first visit to the vet, and getting a new collar. Max and Samson continue to write letters back and forth until Samson celebrates his first birthday where Max congratulates Samson for starting to become a well-behaved, well-loved companion to his human family. Puppy Pen Pals is an adorable book dedicated to Terri's beloved Samson. It helps children and adults understand the basic needs of a puppy.
