    Public Safety Corner. Vacation Security
    With summer in full bloom, vacation time is probably just
    around the corner for you and yor family. You shuld have a
    fun, relaxing time, not worrying about the security of your
    home. Below are some general safety suggestions to help
    make your home as safe as possible while you are away:
    
    Before leaving, secure your home by installing reliable locks
    on all doors and windows, and use them!
    
    Engrave your valuables with your dirver's license number.
    You may want to call the Department of Public Safety at
    (408) 730-7140 to schedule a home security survey before you
    leave. The crime prevention representative will suggest
    specific steps for securing your home. You may also hae the
    representative engrave your valuables.
    
    Never leave your house key hidden outside under a doormat,
    in a flower pot, or on the ledge of a door.
    
    Make your house appear "lived in." Use timers for lights and
    radios. Have a neighbor or friend pick up mail, packages,
    and newspapers. You may also want to arrange to keep your
    lawn mowed and watered while you are away.
    
    Leave your trip plans and an emergency telephone number
    with trusted neighbors or friends.
    
    After making sure your home is as secure as possible, relax,
    and enjoy your vacation!
    
    Did Anything Happen When You Called
    Public Safety?
    
    Have you called public safety about something and wondered
    if anything was done? The key to finding out is the "event
    number", When you call public safety ask for the event
    number. This number is used to track the "event" that you
    are calling about. Every call gets an event number, be it a
    report on suspicious activitiy, an illegally parked car,
    domestic violence, or whatever. Later on when you want to
    find out what action was taken or what was the outcome of
    your call you can simply refer to it by its event number. This
    will avoid confusion and allow public safety to respond to
    your inquiry more efficiently.

    Council Watch
    
    At the June 28th Sunnyvale City Council meeting the City
    Council was asked to consider re-evaluating the city's utility
    undergrounding priorities so that the overhead utility wires at
    the Lawrence/101 interchange could be put underground. In
    connection with the Lawrence Expressway HOV project, the
    utility poles have to be moved and all of the 30+ foot tall trees
    along Lawrence, just north of Highway 101, are to be
    removed. There is no plan currently to underground the
    utilities. The speaker making the request pointed out that by
    not removing the trees, the utility lines will be even more of
    an eyesore. It was also pointed out that this "gateway" to
    Sunnyvale is the only gateway identified in the city
    documents as a candidate for improvement that does not
    currently have underground utilities.
    
    Councilmember Stan Kawezynski made a motion, seconded
    by Councilmember Barbara Waldman, to obtain information
    from city staff on what would be invovled to do such a
    project. The motion passed 4 to 2 with the additional support
    of Councilmembers Landon Noll and Jim Roberts. Vice
    Mayor Robin Parker was not present.

    We Can Help - But You Must Call!
    
    If a sanitary sewer lateral does not function, the City should
    be notified at 830-7510 during regular working hours or at
    730-7180 at other times.
    
    1, The sewer crew will then check to make certain that the
    sanitary sewer main is operating in the street. If it is
    operating, the householder will be notified that the
    problem lies in the sanitary sewer lateral and that a
    plumber should be called.
    
    2. It is the responsibility of the landowner or householder to
    maintain the section of sanitary sewer lateral in private
    property. It is also his/her responsibility to remove any
    plugs from the section of sewer lateral between the
    property line and the sewer main caused by paper, toys,
    other solids, etc.
    
    3. Between the property line and the sanitary sewer main, the
    City will make repairs to sanitary sewer laterals that have
    been broken, settled to such an extent as to be
    nonfunctional or plugged by City tree roots entering the
    lateral.
    
    Our Performance Indicator is "Respond to sewer emergencies
    within 20 minutes of receiving the call 90% of the time."
