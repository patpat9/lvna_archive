Below are some general safety suggestions to help make your home as safe as possible while you are away: Before leaving, secure your home by installing reliable locks on all doors and windows, and use them! Did Anything Happen When You Called Public Safety? Have you called public safety about something and wondered if anything was done? The key to finding out is the "event number" When you call public safety ask for the event number. This number is used to track the "event" that you are calling about. Every call gets an event number, be it a report on suspicious activitiy, an illegally parked car, domestic violence, or whatever. Later on when you want to find out What action was taken or what was the outcome of your call you can simply refer to it by its event number. This will avoid confusion and allow public safety to respond to your inquiry more efficiently. Council Watch At the June 28th Sunnyvale City Council meeting the City Council was asked to consider re-evaluating the city's utility undergrounding priorities so that the overhead utility wires at the Lawrence/ 101 interchange could be put underground. In connection with the Lawrence Expressway HOV project, the utility poles have to be moved and all Of the 30+ foot tall trees along Lawrence, just north of Highway 101, are to be removed. There is no plan currently to underground the utilities. The speaker making the request pointed out that by not removing the trees, the utility lines will be even more of an eyesore. It was also pointed out that this "gateway" to Sunnyvale is the only gateway identified in the city documents as a candidate for improvement that does not currently have underground utilities. Councilmember Stan Kawezynski made a motion, seconded by Councilmember Barbara Waldman, to obtain information from city staff on what would be invovled to do such a project.
