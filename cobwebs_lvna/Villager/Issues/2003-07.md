    45 years ago:  In 1958, 
    the first LVRA 
    Picnic was held at 
    Blackberry Farms. 
    At the time, 
    Lakewood Park had not yet been 
    constructed! The LVRA was the 
    predecessor to today’s LVNA. 
     
    45 years ago:  Also in 1958, a fence was 
    established at Havenwood to block 
    access to Calabazas Creek. Access to 
    the creek was a hazard for children. 
     
    40 years ago:  In 1963, it was predicted that 
    homes here would sell for as much as 
    $20,000 by 1968. (And they did!) 
     
    38 years ago: In 1965, the first LVRA “Western 
    Days” event was held in Dick’s Plaza. 
    “Western Days” events were held 
    periodically into the early 1970’s. 
     
    34 years ago:  In 1969, crosswalk markings 
    were painted at key intersections. 
     
    10 years ago:  In 1993, Giovanni’s opened in 
    Dick’s Plaza.  
     
    8 years ago: In 1995, The Villager published a 
    controversial list of the most well kept 
    and most poorly kept homes in 
    Lakewood Village.  
     
    Share your history! 
    Are you a long-time resident of Lakewood 
    Village? Do you have old photographs, real 
    state brochures, newspaper clippings, or old 
    LVRA/LVNA newsletters sitting in your garage? 
    I’ll copy items and return the originals. Call 
    738-5939 or email editor@lvna.net.  

    To the Lakewood Village Community, 
     
    On behalf of the students and staff of 
    Fairwood Elementary School, I thank you 
    for your support during this past school 
    year. In these difficult economic times, 
    we were able to continue to provide our 
    students with a quality and enriched 
    education experience. This would not 
    have been possible without the community 
    support of our Sally Foster fundraiser, 
    which provided money for fieldtrips, the 
    Walkathon and Carnival, which provided 
    funds for teacher classrooms, the Krispy 
    Kreme sales helped to send students to 
    Science Camp, and the Avon sales allowed 
    our Parent Teacher Group to sponsor 
    activities and provided supplies for our 
    students. Thanks to many supportive 
    parents and community members, we 
    continue to have a safe and pleasant 
    school environment. Many of our 
    neighbors watch over our school in the 
    evening hours, and their interest and 
    help is greatly appreciated. I consider 
    many of our neighbors to be guardian

    Dear Friends and Neighbors, 
     
    I bet you didn't know that the LVNA has 
    a hospitality person.  Her name is 
    Margaret and every month she quietly 
    brings to the monthly LVNA meeting a 
    couple of different baked goods for 
    those that attend the meeting.  She 
    places these goodies on the table for

    behalf of the LVNA Board, myself, and 
    all the others that attend the monthly 
    meeting, I say thank you Margaret
    .  We 
    do appreciate you even if we do not say 
    it often.  Those of you that haven't 
    attended a monthly meeting should come 
    and meet Margaret and try her goodies.

    If you are unemployed in Silicon Valley, you are 
    not alone, and you are not without resources. In 
    fact, Sunnyvale residents have some of the best 
    resources available, all practically under one 
    roof. You may already be familiar with “the 
    unemployment office” at 420 South Pastoria, 
    but what you may not know is that there are no 
    less than four significant resources in and 
    around that facility: 
     
    The Employment Development Department 
    help desk and phone bank. This is where you 
    can get questions answered about 
    unemployment insurance benefits and other 
    programs that the EDD has for job-seekers. 
     
    The Connect! Center is a one-stop career 
    center which offers computers, Internet access, 
    labor and training information, resume critique, 
    and more. http://www.novaworks.org/ 
    job_seekers/service_center.html 
     
    NOVA is a federally-funded emplyment and 
    training agency which offers counseling, 
    workshops, and career planning. 
    http://www.novaworks.org 
    ProMatch is a career resource professional 
    association run by members, and it offers 
    workshops and networking opportunities. 
    http://www.promatch.org  
     
    Your first stop shop be the Connect! Center 
    information desk. They can help direct you to all 
    of these resources, depending on your needs. 
