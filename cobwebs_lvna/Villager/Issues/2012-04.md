    Hello Neighbors! 
     
    Last month’s election was a big 
    success. We have, for the first 
    time in a long time, a complete 
    board. I will be staying on for another year as your 
    President and my congratulations to my new board. 
     
    1st Vice President - Wendy Stanley 
    2nd Vice President – Dee Dee Lloyd 
    Secretary – Skip Gottschalk 
    Treasurer – Leah Lane 
     
    All of our board members are long time neighbors and 
    have helped out as volunteers in our different events. 
    This means that LVNA can continue all the wonderful 
    events throughout the year.   
     
    Our March meeting had our NRO Brian Gantt 
    (Neighborhood Resource Officer) talk to us about the 
    crime rate in Sunnyvale.  North Sunnyvale (Lakewood) 
    has no more crime than any other part of Sunnyvale.  
    Burglaries are up and he mentioned to always 
    make sure doors and window are closed and 
    locked.  
     
    Councilmember Jim Griffith also attended our 
    meeting and mentioned that the pool was a 
    done deal. The City has decided that it will take 
    too much money to continue to operate and will 
    be closing the pool this summer. They will have 
    meetings with LVNA and the neighbor’s to    
    discuss what type of new recreation will replace 
    the pool.  
     
    Thanks to all the neighbors and board members 
    who tried very hard to keep our pool open for 
    the children, it just wasn’t in the cards.

    Box Tops for Education  
    By Phyllis Fowler 
    Clip Box Tops ! 
    Have you ever seen those little pink “Box Tops for 
    Education” printed on hundreds of your favorite products? 
    Each of these Box Top coupons are worth 10 cents to our 
    schools. I have found them on Kleenex boxes (the square 
    ones and it’s under the plastic wrap), Pillsbury rolls and 
    biscuits, Ziploc bags and even Avery labels! 
    When I buy anything that has the Box Top, I cut in out and 
    put it in an empty Altoids tin (or anything will do). Even 
    during the summer, I am filling my little box. When my box is 
    full, I take it down to Lakewood, Fairwood or Columbia 
    Middle School and drop them off. 
    You can also go to www.2ways2earn.com and register 
    your Safeway card to earn even more points for your school. 
    At the website are promotions and a list of products that 
    have the Box Tops for Education coupon. 
    Send Box Tops to School   
    You don’t have to be a parent to collect Box Tops for 
    Education. So turn in your Box Tops and our schools will 
    collect and send them to Box Tops for Education. 
    All those little 10¢ Box Tops add up. (Lakewood is up over 
    $400.00 already!)  
    So clip those Box Tops and drop them off at your favorite 
    school. 

    Officer Teddy 
    From March issue of Beyond the Badge 
     
    In February, Lt. Robert Sorci lost his retired canine partner 
    Teddy to cancer. Teddy was the Department's and City's first 
    cross-trained explosive detection canine.  
     
    Teddy and Lt. Sorci set the 
    standard for the current explosive 
    detection canine teams. 
    Whenever the President or 
    another dignitary was in the area, 
    he was a favorite of the Secret 
    Service - not just any canine is 
    used by the Secret Service! In 
    addition to his explosive detection 
    work, he was also successful in 
    locating and capturing numerous 
    dangerous suspects.   
      
    Teddy served the officers and citizens of Sunnyvale for six 
    years. He kept officers and citizens safe during the years 
    after 9/11. Teddy will be missed by all. Our thoughts and 
    prayers are with Lt. Sorci and his family. 
     
    (Editor-I have had the pleasure of seeing Officer Teddy and 
    Lt. Sorci perform at various community events over the years. 
    Teddy was amazing to watch, talk about combining brains, 
    beauty and talent! It was an honor to have him represent and 
    serve Sunnyvale.) 
