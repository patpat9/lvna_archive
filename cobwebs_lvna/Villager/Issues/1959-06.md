    INCORPORATION REPORT
    
    The LVRA has filed incorporation
    
    papers with the California Secretary
    of State and within a month should
    become an incorporated body.
    
    What advantage is to be gained by
    incorporating?Primarily there is the
    liability factor, If an association
    is not incorporated ani a suit for
    damages is brought against it, each
    member of that association is liable
    and can be made to pay. Bat with an
    incorporated bedy,only the assets of
    that corporation are open to claim.
    
    Naturally the association will be
    fully covered by the proper insur-
    ance to pay any claims which might
    be made for damages. Nevertheless, it
    gives a welcome feeling of security
    to know that your savings will never
    be put in jeopardy due to membership
    in the organization.
    
    Another advantage is that the LVRA
    is not just another group of private
    citizens banded together upon mutual
    consent. It is an entity which has
    been recognized by the State of Cal-
    ifornia and can operate as a busin-
    ess if so desired.
    
    Now the LVRA is incorporated as a
    non-profit organization, This means
    that no dividends will be paid to
    the share-holders at the end of the
    year. Other than this one feature,
    which gives us a big break as far as
    taxation is concerned, this corpora~
    tion is free te pursue any course it
    so desires.
    
    So now that we have a good found-
    ation, let's really build the LVRA in
    to a going concern-bring your ideas,
    enthusiasms, and above all, yoursel-
    ves to the meetings. The LVRA needs
    you, and you need the LVRA.
    
    Tom Mogg, Chairman
    Incorporation committee
