    President’s Column 
     
    -Phyllis Fowler 
     
    Greetings!   
    This month we will not be having a 
    meeting.  
    Unfortunately, our copier has bit the 
    dust. The repair bill is more than the printer is worth. 
    Even then, there is no guarantee that the repair will 
    work. Fred and I paid for it to be repaired back in No-
    vember. But when it came to print the January news-
    letter, it failed completely. We bought a new printer on 
    Saturday and as I write this, Fred is getting it set up so 
    I can start printing.  
    Elections 
    However, March 8th is an important meeting, as it is 
    our annual board elections. I will not be serving again 
    as President of the LVNA. It has been an honor, but I 
    do not have the time to do the LVNA justice. Without 
    neighbor’s stepping up and being a part of a very 
    strong association, we will not continue to have our 
    wonderful events throughout the year.  Things like 
    Dumpster Day, the Annual picnic, and the Holiday Pa-
    rade will not happen!  This last year we did not have 
    anyone step forward to take on the chair of the Holiday 
    Parade, so we did not have one. I stepped forward to 
    chair Dumpster Day, because no one else did. 
     
    This association was formed back in 1989.  Lakewood/
    Fairwood has had a reputation (and still to this day) of 
    being on the wrong side of the tracks. The LVNA has 
    worked very hard to establish a relationship with the 
    City and we have changed some of their opinions, but 
    there is still more work.  If this neighborhood is im-
    portant to you, please consider serving on the board or 
    chairing one of the events.  
     
    See you on March 8th !  
     
     
     
     
     
     
     
     
    Newsletter Editor Stepping Down 
     
    I will also be stepping down as newsletter Editor. I have 
    been editing and printing the newsletter for the last 7 
    years. I spend about a week of my “off time” gathering 
    and writing articles for the newsletter. I was able to cut 
    the cost of printing the newsletter from $425.00 a month 
    to less than $250.00. (Sometimes even less)  I have at 
    the same time, increased the newsletter from 4 pages to 
    6 and from black & white to full color!  
     
    The future of the newsletter will depend on whether a 
    new board is elected in March. If you are interested in 
    taking on the newsletter, come to the March meeting. I 
    will be happy to help and assist the new editor to get out 
    their first newsletter. 
     
    You can contact me at sunnyphyl@gmail.com

    Police Blotter 
     
     
    Drug Possession – 700 Blk. San Juan 
    Tuesday January 3 @   2:15pm 
    Officers responded on a report of a suspicious male and 
    female in the area, and that the male was armed with a 
    handgun.  Upon arrival, the female subject was contacted 
    but the male subject fled on foot towards San Miguel 
    School.  The subject was taken into custody as he was 
    running towards the school campus.  The school was on 
    lockdown for about five minutes until officers could confirm 
    no handgun was discarded on the campus.  It was 
    determined the male subject was in possession of a 
    paintball gun.  Both subjects were arrested for being in 
    possession of drugs. 
     
    Fire – 600 Blk. Lakehaven 
    Tuesday January 10 @  10:21pm 
    DPS Fire personnel responded on a report of a vehicle 
    fire.  Upon arrival, it was determined the vehicle was fully 
    engulfed in fire and was impinging on another 
    vehicle.  Crews were able to extinguish the fire before 
    causing further damage to the second vehicle and before 
    the fire spread to a residential fence. 
     
    Possession of Ammunition – 500 Blk. N. Lawrence 
    Sunday January 15 @  39 
    Officers responded to a commercial parking lot of a report 
    of an auto burglary in progress.  Officers arrived and 
    contacted the suspect.  It was determined there was no 
    merit to the auto burglary, but the suspect was a convicted 
    felon in possession of ammunition and drug paraphernalia.   
     
    Possession of Drugs for Sale – 1200 Blk. Wildwood 
    Tuesday January 17 @  9:34 pm 
    An officer conducted a traffic stop on a subject riding a 
    bicycle.  The subject was on probation and it was 
    determined the subject was in possession of drugs for sale 
     
    Public Protest – Washington Park 
    Thursday January 19 @ 4:00 
    DPS officers monitored a planned protest that occurred at 
    Washington Park.  There were about 200 protesters 
    present, and the gathering was peaceful and orderly.  The 
    group dispersed after about an hour. 
     
    Residential Burglary – 1200 Blk. Tucson 
    Tuesday January 24 @ 11:00 am – 8:00 pm 
    An unknown suspect entered the residence through an 
    unlocked window and stole a flat screen TV, laptop, and 
    various electronics. 
