    MAYORAL CANDIDATES FORUM
    You’ve likely heard your fair share of voting-related info this year. However, we’d like to use this month’s 
    “Factoid” to highlight some of the info specifically pertaining to Lakewood Village.
    You should have already received your ballot in the mail. All California voters are receiving a vote-by-mail ballot 
    this year, regardless of their voting preferences from prior elections.
    Nearest drop-box locations, open now (24/7) until 8:00PM on Nov 3rd. Note: Neither Fairwood nor Lakewood 
    schools have them this year -
    •	
    Mission College – Student drop-off : 3000 Mission College Boulevard, Santa Clara, CA 95054 
    •	
    Grocery Outlet Bargain Market : 1180 North Fair Oaks, Sunnyvale, CA 94089
    These are currently the closest to Lakewood Village, but there’s the potential for change, up until the election. 
    Visit eservices.sccgov.org/Rov/ for complete information.
    Mailing your ballot with the prepaid envelope is another great option. Due to the entire state receiving mail-
    in ballots and a huge influx of mailed ballots across the country, sending it in as early as possible has never 
    been more important. Not only can you track your ballot along its journey at wheresmyballot.sos.ca.gov, but 
    early arrival then ensures that, if for some reason the ballot is rejected (the most anticipated cause would be a 
    signature mismatch), you would have enough time to correct the discrepancy, per SB 759.
    If you will be voting in person, the closest voting centers will open for early voting from 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM on 
    October 31st – November 2nd, and again on election day (November 3rd) from 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
    •	
    Levi’s Stadium – Atrium : 4900 Marie P DeBartolo Wy, Santa Clara, CA 95054
    •	
    Fair Oaks Park – Park Building : 540 N Fair Oaks Ave, Sunnyvale, CA 94085
    •	
    Columbia Middle School – Library : 739 Morse Ave, Sunnyvale, CA 94085
    These have the potential to change up until the election. Visit eservices.sccgov.org/Rov/ for complete 
    information.
    If you are eligible to vote but aren’t yet registered, the voter registration deadline is October 19th. Besides this 
    being a presidential election year, there are over two-dozen additional positions and measures included on this 
    ballot. Please consider registering and submitting your ballot so that our leaders and policy changes are truly 
    decided by “the people,” as opposed to only a portion of the eligible voting population. Your voice matters!
    With voting now in full swing, if you weren’t able to join the Sunnyvale Mayoral Candidates Forum last month, or 
    would like to revisit the discussion, the recording has been posted to youtube.com/watch?v=pZdySbtb65k 
    The candidates for this position are: Michael Goldman, Larry Klein and Nancy Smith.

    LVNA MEETING RECAP
    LVNA MEETING ON SEPT 9
    Officers Jason Pistor and Spencer Chen, from the Sunnyvale Department of Public Safety, joined our first-ever 
    digital LVNA general meeting last month to discuss residents’ concerns and answer any questions. This was an 
    amazingly informative hour-long meeting that covered a wide range of issues. Some key takeaways:
    •	
    If you see/hear something you believe to be illegal or dangerous, call 911 immediately. The sooner an 
    officer can arrive on scene, the more likely they are to be successful in resolving the issue.
    •	
    For speeding/donuts: Call 408-736-6224 right away, with a description of the individual, vehicle make/model/
    color, license plate number, direction of travel, and any additional information that may be relevant.
    •	
    If you hear what you believe could be gunshots, call the police immediately so they can investigate. Many 
    people put off calling, second-guessing whether or not they were fireworks. In the case of an incident at 
    Lakewood Park about a week before this meeting (early September), the officers confirmed they were in fact 
    gunshots but, as of writing this, there aren’t any leads.
    •	
    Car break-ins are much more frequent these days. There were changes to the law which significantly reduces 
    the consequences for this crime, leading to a higher frequency of occurrence and more repeat offenders. Your 
    best defense is to not leave anything of value in your car, even if it’s hidden.
    •	
    Multiple streets within Lakewood Village were identified as major thoroughfares for speeders. The officers 
    took note and agreed to place both hidden and visible warning speed sensors to gather data in these areas. 
    Sensors were spotted within days of this meeting. Thanks Officer Pistor and Officer Chen!
    Useful phone numbers: Desk/Office – 408-730-7150 | Speeding/donuts – 408-736-6244 | Non-emergency – 408-
    730-7180 | Emergency – 911
    To discuss any additional issues, both officers have graciously agreed to be open-ears. Feel free to reach out to 
    them through email and include your name and phone number.
    Officer Jason Pistor: jpistor@sunnyvale.ca.gov || Officer Spencer Chen: schen@sunnyvale.ca.gov

    PET OF THE MONTH
    NEW TO ZOOM?
    Our pet is named Snowball. She is a Syrian Hamster. She is albino, 
    which is quite rare, and also mute, so she can’t squeak. Like all 
    hamsters, she also can’t see that well. Did you know that she’s not 
    nocturnal or diurnal? Instead, she’s crepuscular. What that means is 
    that she’s actually most active at dawn and dusk. Cool, right? She also 
    really likes to eat carrots and broccoli and goes wild for celery and 
    sprouts.
     
    During the day, she sleeps in her cage. In the summer, she’s stopped 
    using her igloo and just sleeps in the paper bedding. My little brother, 
    Rishab, feeds her Hamster Pellets in the morning. He loves to play 
    with her. For some reason, she doesn’t drink that much water, but 
    then, I don’t either. It’s really fun to play with her in the evenings when 
    we are all awake. She’s our first ever pet and we all really love her.
    Is your pet ready for the Villager spotlight? Submit your caption and
