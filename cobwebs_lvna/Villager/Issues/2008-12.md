    Youth Basketball League ls Goming!
    Officials and Scorekeepers Wanted
    Referee and scorekeeper positions are available for the
    Sunnyvale Youth Basketball League. This program serves
    first through eighth grade boys and girls. All games are on
    Saturdays, January 17 to March 14, from approximately
    9 a.m. - 5 p.m. at various Sunnyvale school facilities.
    The referee and scorekeeper position requires knowledge
    of basic basketball rules and the skills necessary to play
    basketball. Individuals must have the ability to officiate
    while instructing players in developing skills and under-
    standing the rules of the game. Two paid, mandatory basic
    referee training clinics will be held prior to the start of the
    season. Individuals must possess the ability to work with
    parents, coaches and children, and have the ability to
    develop and maintain a high standard of professional
    conduct and civil responsibility. Applicants must be at
    least 16 years old. Interested applicants should contact the
    league coordinator at (a08) 730-7723 by December 5.
    The Sunnywale Youth Basketball League is a fun league
    designed to encourage participation of all girls and boys
    who want to play basketball. The aim of the league is to
    develop basketball skills while helping children learn to
    respect others, to practice sportsmanship, and to win or
    lose gracefully. No league standings are kept. For more
    information, call (408) 730-7723 or visit
    SYBL.inSunnyvale.com

    Dear Lakewood Village Residents,
    On the evening of Nov 19, our teenage daughter was
    crossing the street at Lakehaven and Meadowlake. She
    was hit by a car turning right onto Meadowlake - a car
    that did not bother to stop at the stop sign - as far too
    many cars do lately. The driver did not even roll down
    her window, but simply mouthed, "Are you ok?" and
    drove off. Fortunately, our daughter was not seriously
    hurt, but she was quite shaken by the incident. This is
    considered a hit and run. We were appalled that the
    driver didn't even get out of the car. We have requested
    that the Sunnyvale Police Department spend more time
    monitoring this four-way stop. We are making an appeal
    to all the residents in Lakewood Village to please slow
    down and obey the stop signs, not only because it's the
    law, but because the next time, your carelessness could
    cause serious harm to someone.
