    Neighborhood President's New Year's Message
    Dear Neighbors,
    Happy New Year!
    As we step into 2025, I want to take a moment to reflect on the incredible sense of community
    we’ve built over the past year. 2024 brought us together in ways that showed just how strong we
    are when we unite with a common purpose. Whether through neighborhood events, helping each
    other during times of need, or simply sharing moments of joy, we’ve made our community a
    wonderful place to call home.
    One of the highlights of 2024 was the return of our beloved Christmas Parade. It was truly a
    spectacular event, and it was heartwarming to see so many of you come together to make it such a
    success. A special thank you to Rena Flovin for her hard work and dedication in bringing the
    parade to life.
    We also revived our annual picnic and introduced a new tradition—cleaning up the Calabazas
    Creek twice a year in collaboration with the Santa Clara Valley Water District. None of these
    events could happen without the dedicated efforts of our neighbors. I encourage each of you to
    consider volunteering and contributing to the continued betterment of our community.
    Looking ahead to 2025, we have much to anticipate. We’re excited to continue improving our
    neighborhood, focusing on sidewalk repairs, tree trimming, and enhancing street lighting. These
    initiatives will make our community even safer, more welcoming, and visually appealing.
    I’d like to extend a special thanks to Mayor Larry Klein, City Manager Tim Kirby, and all the City
    Council members for their ongoing support of the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association.
    Their dedication and collaboration have been invaluable, and we are deeply grateful for their
    efforts in helping our neighborhood thrive.
    Let’s make 2025 a year of connection, kindness, and positive change. I am so honored to serve as
    your neighborhood president and to be part of such a caring and committed community.
    Wishing you all a year filled with health, happiness, and prosperity!
    Warmly,
    Rick Flovin
    President, Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association

    Safe Routes to School - Lakewood Elementary Update
    LVNA VILLAGER - JANUARY 2025
    PAGE 5
    The City of Sunnyvale has a $1.7M state earmark to implement several Safe Routes to School (SRTS)
    improvements identified at the intersection of Lakechime Drive and Meadowlake Drive near Lakewood
    Elementary School. 
    Here is the update from Tim Kirby, Sunnyvale City Manager, from fall 2024:
    The improvements include curb radius reductions, curb extensions to reduce crossing distances, ADA curb
    ramps, high visibility crosswalks, and pedestrian safety signs and striping. In addition, the City will be
    implementing green stormwater infrastructure to better facilitate stormwater runoff in the area. Streetlights
    will be strategically relocated to enhance lighting conditions and improve visibility for travelers near this
    intersection. These improvements are designed to improve the overall safety and connectivity for students
    when walking or biking to school. The next steps are to complete the design and request bids for
    construction. The project has to be completed no later than June 30. 2027, but the City hopes to complete it
    sooner.
    More details can be found in the City’s Active Transportation Plan (ATP). The section of the ATP that covers
    the improvements around Lakewood School can be viewed at https://tinyurl.com/LakewoodSRTS
    LVNA Board Elections
    Would you be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends, and the next
    generation who’s currently growing up right outside your door or even in your own backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice
    President, Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in April and a new board will begin.
    The board meets once per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to holding quarterly
    neighborhood general meetings and a few seasonal events each year. The level of commitment is up to
    each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is a one-year term (April 2025 - March 2026). The only
    requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the neighborhood, and are able to serve the full 12
    month term. Elections are April 9 at 7:00pm in the Lakewood Park Building. For more information, please
    email board@lvnasv.org 
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new neighbors? 
    If you have not already joined the LVNA group on Nextdoor, we encourage you to do so. Nextdoor allows
    separate settings for group posts, so it's an excellent way to get curated Lakewood Village-related content
    while filtering out the rest. Board members also use this group to post reminders about upcoming events.
    Note that only Lakewood Village residents are permitted to join our group. To learn more or to join, visit
    www.nextdoor.com/g/alya6yxap
