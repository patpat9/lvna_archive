    Neighborhood Watch
    
    On August 30, 1994 Sunnyvale Public Safety Officers
    responded to a report that at approximately 2:20AM. a 12
    year old female was awakened in her bed by a suspect who
    was holding her down by the shoulders. The victim screamed
    and the suspect fled out an open window. The victim’s
    screams awakened a parent who came to the bedroom. tended
    to the victim, then called 911.
    
    The suspect is described as an 18-30 year old black male.
    5°6” - 5°7", 190 - 210 Lbs, with short black hair,
    
    It is believed that the suspect entered through the same open
    and unlocked window that he fled through. This assault
    occurred on Manzano Way in Sunnyvale. If anyone has
    information about this assault they are asked to contact
    Detective Don Bacon or Detective Mike Marcalescu at 730-
    7120 Monday through Fiday 8-5. or the desk officer at 730-
    
    7110 at any other time.
    Police Services Press Release August 31, 1994

    A Good Neighbor!
    
    Law enforcement officers can’t be everywhere at once, but
    you and your neighbors can. You're the ones who really know
    what’s going on in the neighborhood.
    
    Put that neighborhood know-how to work. It’s simple. just
    use your eyes and ears - and then your telephone. If you spot
    something suspicious, call the police or sheriff immediately.
    
    Don’t try to stop a criminal yourself - it can be dangerous.
    
    Neighbors working together in cooperation with law
    enforcement make one of the best crime fighting tcams
    around.

    English Classes at Lakewood School
    
    Two different English classes are meeting at Lakewood
    School this fall.
    
    Beginning English classes are at the school on Monday and
    Wednesday from 9:15 to 11:15 a.m. Register in the school
    office, books for this class cost about $15. Last year we had 3
    volunteer teachers and 15-20 student attending each day. This
    semester we want 2-3 more volunteer teachers (10 hours of
    free training provided in September) and 10 more students.
    
    Adult high school teachers have a beginning class in the
    multi-purpose room and intermediate class in room #13
    Starting Sept. 12. These classes are from 6:30 to 9:30 p.m. on
    Mondays and Wednesdays. Register at the Ist class. Books
    cost about $30. During the summer. these classes had 50-60
    students, and during the spring semester there were 75-90
    people in class, these classes are popular,
    
    We appreciate the adult high school department for bringing
    these classes to our neighborhood.
    By: Pat Boyle 744-1149
