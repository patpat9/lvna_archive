    DRIVE
    LVNA wants your California Redemption Value (CRV) 
    recyclables! We’re introducing a new fundraising activity for 
    our neighborhood, and want your help in collecting beverage 
    containers. 100% of the funds will be used for future LVNA 
    neighborhood events and expenses.
    Did you know, the average person in the U.S. consumed about 
    78 gallons of bottled water and soft drinks in 2016? That’s over 
    700 eligible beverage containers! This doesn’t even include 
    alcoholic beverages, such as beer, or sports/energy drinks.

    Suggested Tips
    •	
    Unsure which containers are eligible for CRV redemption? Check the label on the recyclables. Eligible 
    recyclables say one of the following: California Redemption Value, CA Redemption Value, California Cash 
    Refund, CA CRV, or CA Cash Refund.
    •	
    Do you have friends/family outside of the neighborhood? Why not offer to take some of their recyclables 
    off of their hands?
    •	
    If you’re feeling extra ambitious, pre-sorting by material and placing in separate bags is super helpful 
    for our volunteers. When turning them in, aluminum cans, plastic bottles, and glass bottles must all be 
    separated, so this saves an extra step for them.
    •	
    Collected containers may be temporarily stored at our volunteers’ houses. For containers without lids, a 
    quick rinse would be greatly appreciated.
    
    RECIPE CORNER
    Banana Chocolate Chip Cookies
    02     LVNA VILLAGER | JULY 2020
    1.	 Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
    2.	 In a bowl, measure out all of the dry ingredients (flour, 
    baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and 
    cloves) minus the sugar. Whisk together and set aside. 
    3.	 Cream together butter and sugar in a stand mixer, until 
    fluffy (approx.1-2 minutes).
    4.	 Combine egg into butter/sugar mixture. Add mashed 
    banana into mixture until fully combined.
    5.	 Add bowl of dry ingredients into banana mixture and mix 
    just until fully combined.
    6.	 Fold in desired amount of chocolate chips.
    7.	 Line two baking trays with parchment paper. Use a 
    medium-sized cookie scoop and dollop dough onto 
    baking trays. Make sure to leave some room in-between 
    dough balls because they will expand a little bit in the 
    oven.
    8.	 Bake cookies for 13-15 minutes, or until done. Let them 
    cool and enjoy!
    1/2 CUP unsalted butter, room temp 
    2/3 CUP white sugar
    1 egg, room temp
    1 CUP mashed banana, ~3 bananas
    2 CUPS flour, all-purpose
    2 TSP baking powder
    1/2 TSP baking soda
    1/4 TSP salt
    1 TSP ground cinnamon
    1/2 TSP ground nutmeg
    1/2 TSP ground cloves
    chocolate chips, optional
