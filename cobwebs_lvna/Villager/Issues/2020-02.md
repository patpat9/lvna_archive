    MESSAGE FROM 
    THE PRESIDENT
    As we welcome 2020, I want to reflect on what the 
    new board has accomplished, since being elected 
    in July of 2019. We have hosted dumpster day, 
    NNO (National Night Out), a picnic, a Halloween 
    party, a Christmas lighting contest, plus we got 
    the Villager online. Thanks so very much to the 
    Ray family, who has done a fantastic job, as well as 
    all of the other volunteers who have helped with 
    the other events.
    Areas we need to improve on are the monthly 
    meeting participation and neighborhood 
    communication. Not everyone has internet or is aware of the LVNASV.org website. Our 
    goal is to get a paper version, or other forms of communication, to be more inclusive of 
    the whole neighborhood.
    This month, we will have a speaker from the 2020 census, along with food & drinks. In 
    March, we will have someone speaking about the new library, which has finally been 
    approved to move forward, a volunteer appreciation night, with food & drinks, plus we 
    will be voting for the board. Wishing it an even more successful year, going forward.
    Meanwhile, I would like to thank everyone who has helped with getting LVNA functioning 
    again.
    Bev Woidyla
    President LVNA

    LVNA MEETING RECAP
    LVNA MEETING ON JAN 8
    Teresa Zarrin, Associate Planner for the City of Sunnyvale, spoke about the recent updates to ADUs (Accessory 
    Dwelling Units). There is now a streamlined permit process for most ADUs, drastically reducing the cost and 
    headaches previously associated with ADU development. For more information, please check out her presentation 
    slides at lvnasv.org/artifacts/20200108_ADU.pdf.
    From Santa Clara County Registrar of Voters, Sheng Luu gave an overview of Voter’s Choice. Voting in Santa Clara 
    County is now more convenient than ever:
    •	
    Same day registration and voting
    •	
    11 voting days (including 2 weekends)
    •	
    All registered voters will automatically receive Vote-By-Mail ballots
    •	
    Voters can vote or drop off ballots at ANY voting center in Santa Clara County
    •	
    As always, Vote-By-Mail ballots can be dropped off at any post office or USPS mailbox, with the postage-paid 
    return envelope
    For more information, visit www.sccvote.org/VotersChoice
