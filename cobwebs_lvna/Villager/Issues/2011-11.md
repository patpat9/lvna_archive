    Energy Savings Assistance Program 
      
    By Phyllis Fowler (continued from page 1) 
     
    I recently had the audit done on our house. Paul, who is with 
    ―Quality Conservation Services‖ looked for places where air 
    and heat could be escaping our house. A cracked window, a 
    doggie door, even a light panel are all places where energy 
    can escape, wasting both energy and money. 
     
    Paul told me, ―In the winter time, if you put your hand up next 
    to a light switch, sometimes you can feel the cold air actually 
    coming in.‖ In 2008 alone, 62,000 customers benefited from 
    this program. The program will even replace your old light 
    bulbs with energy saving SFL bulbs. A simple thing like replac-
    ing old light bulbs with CFLs could cut your energy bill 75 
    cents a month per bulb! Weather stripping your doors and win-
    dow is also a good idea. This will prevent cool air escaping in 
    the summertime and cold air coming in during the winter. 
     
    Paul replaced several of my light bulbs with the CLFs and I 
    was surprised at how much brighter my 
    kitchen was! I had an old halogen torche 
    light in the TV room and Paul replace it 
    with a three way torche that uses 3 CFLs. 
    I didn’t realize how much brighter the TV 
    room would be at night. If I had, I would 
    have had him replace the one in my den 
    and the one in my sunroom! 
     
    He also replaced one of the lights fixtures in our backyard. It 
    was one on the big halogen light bulbs (rather ugly) that was 
    installed when we built our sunroom. Now we have a black 
    carriage style light fixture that will come on at night and turn off 
    during the day. 
     
    I also got a lot of information about how to save energy (and 
    money!) around my house. Turn off lights you are not using. (If 
    I can just get Fred to do that!)

    When you are shopping for appliances, look for the ―Energy 
    Star‖ sticker. You will find them on almost every kind of 
    appliances. If you do, you will save money on electricity 
    and many washing machines and dishwashers use less 
    water as well. Look at the ―Energy Savings‖ 
    stickers on the machines and they will tell 
    you how much you can save. As you 
    compare the features and prices, also 
    compare the energy savings! The difference 
    between what you can save in energy cost 
    and water use may make a more expensive 
    machine cheaper in the long run. 
     
    Use cold water to wash your clothes whenever possible. I 
    now use a laundry detergent that is made for cold water 
    washing and my clothes are coming out clean. I now only 
    use hot or warm water when I am washing whites only. 
     
    Use the energy-saver cycle on your dishwasher or let your 
    dishes air dry by turning off the appliance before the dry 
    cycle begins. I did it and by the time my dishes were cool, 
    they were dry. 
     
    Did you know you should change the filter in your heater 
    and air conditioner monthly? If you keep your air 
    conditioner thermostat no lower than 78 degrees in the 
    summertime and your heater no higher than 68 decrees in 
    the wing.

    7:00pm and learn more tips and about the programs at the

    SCS is an independent, nonprofit emergency assistance 
    agency that works to prevent homelessness and hunger for 
    low-income Sunnyvale families and seniors facing temporary 
    crises like a layoff or medical need. The agency provides 
    financial aid, food, and other support that prevents larger 
    problems requiring more expensive solutions. SCS served 
    approximately 7,000 people last year.

    Volunteer by Donating to the Lakewood Village Neighbors Association (LVNA)  
     
    By a Lakewood Village Resident 
    Hooray, hooray, hooray! Finally we all have a way to volunteer without taking up our time. 
     
    Last month, I went to the meeting and saw that our LVNA was short on cash. That got me to thinking; there are approximately 
    1400 homes in Lakewood Village. If we all were to donate just $1.00 each our LVNA would not have a problem affording many 
    of the things we would like to do, such as paying a team to clean graffiti, take care of litter, helping senior citizens, etc. 
     
    When you think about it, we give tips to a lot of service people; why not give a tip to all the volunteers that give their time and 
    energy to serve us. 
     
    As you can see this month some of us are fortunate enough to donate more than a dollar, so if one person gives five dollars 
    they pay for four families that couldn’t. If someone gives twenty that compensates for nineteen more. 
     
    I have lived in Lakewood Village since 1969 and have never volunteered my time or energy, so it really makes me feel great to 
    help financially. Here’s looking forward to a lot of others to join me in making a stronger LAKEWOOD VILLAGE

    Bowl with the Sharks! 
     
    The San Jose Sharks are a team of tough, big, scrappy and 
    talented hockey players…but they are also deeply 
    committed to their community. No wonder then, that their 
    entire organization and Foundation are too.

    more than 6,000 children each month! The clinic however

    inspiration. Enter the San Jose Sharks, who are investing 
    up to $100,000 to ―re-imagine‖ the Clinic.

    of players, team colors throughout

    mascot everywhere. More than just

    healthy tips for kids and parents:

    drink water not soda, get your 
    exercise and immunizations…all positive messages to 
    match the positive look and feel.

    holding the inaugural ―Sharks & Strikes‖ charity bowling

    (Editor- Recently, I have noticed an increase in thefts of 
    GPS systems from cars throughout Sunnyvale. If you 
    have the type that is portable, make sure it is not visible. 
    Lock it in your glove box or take it with you. Do not leave 
    your car unlocked! It only attracts thieves and makes it 
    easier for them to take things out of your vehicle.  
     
    I also wanted to bring to your attention 2 thefts of 
    packages left on resident’s front porches. If you are 
    expecting a package, specify that the package is not to 
    be left on your front porch, request that the package 
    require a signature or the package be left at the main
