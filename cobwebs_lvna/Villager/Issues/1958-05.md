## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **Executive Board Message:** Permanent officers elected; call for neighborhood-wide membership and unity.
    - **Directory:** LVRA officers, unit representatives, and committee chairs (Band, Babysitting, Civil Defense, Social, Writers & Artists, Legal, Athletics).
    - **Early Accomplishments:** Securing Lakewood Park acreage, winning residential zoning against a commercial strip mall, and launching youth summer recreation.
- **Page 2:**
    - **Notes at Random:** 'Kleen Sweep' street cleaning schedule, off-street parking rules, and Branden Construction Little League uniform allotment ($180).
    - **Shopping Center Progress:** Announcement of Shell service station and planned Lucky or Broadway supermarket.
    - **School Tax Election:** Breakdown of proposed 70¢ assessment rate increase ($1.50 to $2.20 per $100) before the May 20 election.
    - **Lawn Care & Landscaping:** Upcoming talk by professional landscape artist Wayne Shoemaker at Fairwood School.
    - **Civic Reminders:** Traffic safety on one-way streets and Veteran's property tax exemption filing deadline.
