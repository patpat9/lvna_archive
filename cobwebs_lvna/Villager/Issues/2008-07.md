    SUNNWALE, Calif. - ChoiceCollect - Curbside Collection
    That Fits You, Sunnyvale's new residential garbage and
    recycling system, will be implemented in phases beginning
    July 1. The initial phase will include on-call curbside
    collection.
    With ChoiceCollect's on-cal I cu rbside collection, Su nnyvale
    residents will be able to schedule two no-charge curbside
    collections of extra trash, bulky household items and elec-
    tronic waste such as TVs and monitors, each year. Choice-
    Collect on-call pickups replace the curbside collections
    formerly held Citywide during spring and fall, and now pro-
    vides residents with the flexibility of choosing when they
    want to get rid of extra household items.
    At each on-call collection, residents may set out up to two
    cubic yards of extra trash and non-hazardous household
    material (equivalent to a space six feet long, three feet
    high and three feet wide), plus two bulky items, such as
    appliances or furniture.
    This service is available to all garbage customers in single-
    family, duplex, triplex or and mobile homes for no charge,
    iwice yearly, by calling the Utility Billing Division, Customer
    Service at (408) 730-7400, TDD (408) 730-7501.
