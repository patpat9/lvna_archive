    Gangs? In Lakewood Village?
    
    What is a gang? What does it mean to be a “gang member”?
    Does the problem exist in Lakewood Village? How can we
    tell? What are the signs? Are they “real gangs” or just
    “wannabes”? What’s the difference?
    
    These questions and many more will be answered by Public
    Safety. October 20th, right here in Lakewood Village and
    everyone is invited to attend! LVNA has scheduled a
    presentation, with Gang Awareness as the topic, for the next
    general assembly meeting. Come, see if there is something we
    can do to prevent our youth (all of them) from becoming
    involved with gangs. No children in your family? It doesn’t
    matter, gang activities affect us all and we all should be
    informed, Teens are especially encouraged to attend.

    Sunnyvale City Council Request
    
    The Sunnyvale City Council is seeking qualified applicants to
    apply for seats on the Parks and Recreation Commission.
    
    The Parks and Recreation Commission advises the City
    Council on matters concerning the development, operation
    and use of City parks and recreation programs, policies and
    procedures. It also reviews budget issues and regulations
    affecting parks and recreation programs and makes
    recommendations to the City Council. The Commission also
    advises the Council on City funding of outside groups and
    athletic activities.
    
    The City is interested in creating a diverse pool of applicants
    who are representative of the Sunnyvale community. Persons
    interested in serving should call Volunteer Services at (408)
    730-7533 for an application packet and specific eligibility
    requirements.
    
    To serve on the Parks and Recreation Commission, you must
    be a registered voter currently residing in the City of
    Sunnyvale, you cannot be employed by the City of Sunnyvale

    Tips for a Safe Holloween
    
    Try to Trick-or-Treat when it is still light outside.
    Wear a costume that makes it easy for you to walk, see and be
    seen.
    
    If you must go out at night, make certain that your costume is
    light in color,
    
    Carry a flashlight so you can see and be seen easily.
    
    Use retro-reflective tape on your costume so people driving
    Cars can see you.
    
    A face mask will keep you from seeing well. Take off your
    mask before you cross a street.
    
    Why not use make-up instead of a mask’?
    
    Have a parent, older brother or sister go Trick-or-Treating
    with you.
    
    If someone older cannot go with you, Trick-or-Treat with a
    group of children.
    
    Plan your Trick-or-Treat ahead of time. Pick streets that are
    well-lighted.
    
    Tell your family on which streets you will be Trick-or-
    Treating.
    
    Cross only at corners. Never cross the street between parked
    cars or in the middle of the block.
    
    If there is no sidewalk, walk facing traffic.
    
    Wait until you get home to sort, check and eat your treats.

    By-Laws
    
    What are By-Laws and why do we need them? By-Laws are
    guidelines. We use them to clearly spell out (or as clearly as
    possible), everything from our purpose, to rules of conduct.
    This document gives consistency to an organization. For
    example, the role of each officer is defined, you know what is
    expected of you. Some may view it as “accountability”, and
    some as “guidance”.
    
    A request was presented by a Lakewood resident that a copy
    of the By-Laws be delivered to every home in Lakewood
    Village. After thorough analysis of the Neighborhood
    Association’s current financial status, it has been determined
    this is not cost effective; however if you wish to obtain a copy,
    we are asking that you please do so by mailing a legal sized
    SASE along with $0.75 (to cover printing cost) to:
    
    Lakewood Village Neighborhood Assoc.
    PO Box 9249-1690
    San Jose, CA 95157
    If enough interest is expressed, a public reading of the By-
    Laws will be held, time/date TBD. For info, call 245-8382.
