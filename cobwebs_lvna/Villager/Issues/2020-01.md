    Can you believe it’s already here!? It’s officially 2020 
    and that time of year when many of us have either 
    already abandoned a New Year’s Resolution or at 
    least set ourselves up for a seemingly daunting task 
    ahead. Studies have shown that over 80% of people are 
    unsuccessful with their resolutions, over half of which 
    fail within the first two weeks of the year. But, with a 
    new year comes an opportunity to start off fresh and try 
    something new.
    One of the biggest culprits for peoples’ resolutions 
    fizzling out is that they’re often doomed from the start. 
    When choosing a resolution, a proven technique for 
    success is to ensure it’s SMART.

    Your goal should be specific. In other words, clearly defined so there’s no 
    room for ambiguity. It should be easily measurable so you know when you’ve 
    reached a particular milestone and/or when you’ve succeeded all together. 
    Dreaming big and reaching for the stars is great, but if your end goal is so far 
    away it may seem unattainable. Make sure it’s within reach and that a stretch-
    goal has intermediate milestones to help keep you on track.
    Your goal should be relevant to YOU. Yes, many of us would love to be CEO of our own multi-
    billion dollar company, have a “perfect” BMI, and be the PTA parent that all other parents admire, 
    but your goal should match your own lifestyle, ambitions, and schedule. Among all of this, your 
    goal must be time-bound. One year is too long for most of us to process and actually promotes 
    procrastination...which eventually causes the attainable part to break down. Confine your goal to 
    smaller timeframes, aligning milestones with either specific durations of time (e.g., 6 weeks) or 
    with key events in your life (e.g., one month before the wedding). Following a process like this will 
    help you in defining your goals and putting them on the path towards success.
