## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **Executive Board & Leadership Transition:** Frank Walters assumes LVRA Chairmanship following Irving Mandell's resignation; role of Tract Representatives.
    - **Civic Quality of Life:** Abandoned cars and nuisance parking along tract streets; driving safety to protect neighborhood children.
    - **Shopping Center Reveal:** Update on supermarket tenant - Dick's Super Market secured for Lakewood Shopping Plaza in lieu of Lucky or Broadway.
- **Page 2:**
    - **Community Newsroom & Advertising:** Newspaper staff recruitment; classified ad policies to cover printing costs.
    - **Service Station Freeze:** Sunnyvale Planning Commission pauses gas station permits pending commercial study, holding the planned Lawrence Station Rd station.
    - **Social Committee Events:** First annual LVRA Rummage Sale at 874 Lakewood Drive; preview of the upcoming inaugural Lakewood Village Summer Picnic.
    - **School Election Victory:** Passage of the elementary school assessment measure to expand local classrooms.
    - **Lakewood Athletics:** Lakewood Village 'slow pitch' softball team league opener against Silicon Valley pioneer firm Ampex Audio at Washington Park.
