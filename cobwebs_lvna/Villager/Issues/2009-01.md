    Happy New Yearl Thank you to all of you who made 2008
    a very successful year for LVNA and our neighborhood.
    We had a great Neighborhood Picnic, Dumpster Day, and
    of course, a fantastic Holiday Parade.
    Thank you to all the volunteers who helped make the Holi-
    day Parade the most well attended event that we have
    had. We had a great group of people that showed up
    early to set up and who stayed to serve hot drinks and
    snacks. Thank you to all of the participants we had in the
    parade! A special thanks to those of you who assisted in
    the planning.
    I am very encouraged by the people who have made
    ambitious efforts to help the people in this community. I
    know of several examples where a family had a need, and
    when it was mentioned, there was a person willing to help.
    lf each of us can make the effort to do that for a family this
    year, imagine the possibilities.
    One way that you can help is by joining Lakewood Village
    Neighborhood Association as the President, Vice
    President, or Secretary. Those are the positions that we
    will need to fill for the great work LVNA does each year to
    eontinue. There will be a vote at the February meeting, so
    please invite as many neighbors as you can to attend this
    meeting. You can run for any position on the board of
    LVNA, but those three positions are the ones that will have
    vacancies.
    Finally, we have our winners for the LVNA Lighting
    Contest! The neighborhood was split into 4 quadrants,
    and a winner was chosen from each. Our winners are
    1281 Prescott, 750 Lakemuir,1214 Socorro, and 666
    Lakefair. Congratulations! lf you would like to see pictures
    of any of the winners, please email me.
    Many Thanks to Volunteers for Christmas Parade
    I want to thank all of the groups that were in our Christ-
    mas parade. Your time and generosity were greatly appre-
    ciated. I also want to thank all the people who helped
    with the snacks served after the parade, which included
    Art and Sarah Daughtery, Dennis and Lenora O'Neal,
    Felipe Misch, Romeo and Carol Illorde, Christy and Mi-
    chael Majchrowiczo Robin Woidyla, and a special thanks
    to Imelda Misch for helping me contact the participants in
    the parade. I also want to thank Judy Fernandez for tak-
    ing pictures of the children with Santa.
