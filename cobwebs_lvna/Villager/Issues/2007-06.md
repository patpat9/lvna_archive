    President’s Column 
    -Michael Majchrowicz 
    I want to thank all those who came to the May LVNA 
    meeting. There were some new faces and good 
    conversations on neighborhood issues, as well as good 
    questions for our guest speaker, Adriana Windham, with 
    Project Sentinel, who spoke on fair housing laws.   
    Recommendations on increasing neighborhood safety 
    included adding lights to some of the darker parts of the 
    bike trail running through Lakewood Park. I encourage 
    more of you to come to the meetings, and to help your 
    neighborhood by keeping an eye on the areas where our 
    children play. We want this to be a neighborhood where 
    the kids can run over to the park, and we don’t have to 
    worry so much about their safety. 
    One of the most positive points of the May meeting for 
    me was the opportunity to meet other neighbors, some 
    who have lived in the neighborhood for years and others 
    who have been in Lakewood Village only a short time. 
    Your participation and input are important! 
    The next neighborhood event is Bingo Night. This is a 
    great chance to have fun and win some good prizes. 
    There will be gift certificates for the winners from AMC 
    20 Movie Theater, Starbucks, In-N-Out Burger, Ben & 
    Jerry’s Ice Cream, and Jamba Juice. Bingo Night will be 
    June 13 at 7 p.m. at the Lakewood Park Building. 
    Snacks will be for sale. Join your neighbors for some fun 
    on Wednesday, June 13! 
    If you have suggestions on ways to improve our 
    neighborhood, or topics you would like to see included 
    in the Villager, please call me. My goal is a 
    neighborhood in which there is a constant dialogue and 
    where people know and respect each other. 
    Dumpster Day is coming up on June 30. Dumping in 
    the 20 dumpsters that will be distributed around 
    Lakewood Village can begin that morning. Please 
    respect neighbors who are monitoring the dumpsters. 
    See page 4 for what can and cannot be dumped. If you 
    have hazardous waste materials, you will need to take 
    them to the SMaRT Station (see below) on June 16, July 
    21, or any other third weekend of the month. TVs and 
    other items containing hazardous materials cannot be put 
    into the dumpsters on Dumpster Day. 
    Useful items that you no longer want can go to the 
    Dumpster Day Sale, where you can also get some good 
    bargains. Proceeds from the Parking Lot Sale support 
    LVNA activities, so buying there is one way to support 
    your neighborhood. ■ 
    Remember: NO Hazwaste in the Dumpsters! 
    Take items listed in the No section on page 4 to the 
    SMaRT Station (see page 4). Check Computer 
    Recycling Center (370 West Caribbean Drive), 
    www.crc.org, for a way to donate home electronics. ■

        June in Your Yard and Garden 
    ☼ Weed. Do not let the weeds form seedheads. Be 
    sure to knock down tall dry grasses because they 
    create a serious fire hazard. 
    ☼ Add young, seedhead-free weeds to the compost 
    pile. Remember to keep the pile as damp as a wrungout sponge for faster composting. Turn the pile 
    occasionally. 
    ☼ Watch out for snails and earwigs, both of which 
    devour seedlings. 
    ☼ Use a strong spray from the hose to wash away 
    aphids. 
    ☼ Wrap fruit tree trunks with a sticky tape to control 
    ants. Wrap low so that birds, particularly 
    hummingbirds, will not be attracted. Fruit will be 
    ripening soon and the ants will go after the fruit. 
    Water plants as needed.  
    ☼ Insulate the garden soil from hot, dry weather by 
    applying a two-inch-thick mulch. 
    ☼ Fertilize plants if you see signs of pale, lackluster 
    growth. Yellow leaves with green veins often 
    indicate an iron deficiency, which can be corrected 
    with chelated iron. 
    ☼ Fertilize the lawn. Fertilize all flowering plants 
    about to bloom. Fertilize roses. 
    ☼ Pinch off dead flowers, particularly off lilacs and 
    other late-spring flowering shrubs. Make sure you get 
    the entire flower head when dead-heading annuals—
    including the swollen ovaries where seeds form. This 
    will encourage new flowers as the plant rushes to 
    reproduce itself. 
    ☼ Transplant summer-flowering plants into the 
    bedding plot. Hot weather is hard on newly 
    transplanted plants. Shade them for a few days with 
     
     
    the temporary shelter of an umbrella or wooden shake. 
    Stake tall sprawling flowers such as delphiniums and 
    dahlias. 
    ☼ Continue to plant the vegetable garden. Thin 
    vegetable seedlings that were sown too close together 
    by snipping them with scissors. Harvest vegetables as 
    they ripen, picking them early in the day. 
    ☼ Be very attentive to your watering system to make 
    sure that it is in good working order. Soak all newly 
    transplanted plants deeply. 
    These tips are from the book Northern California Gardening: 
    A Month-by-Month Guide, by Katherine Grace Endicott, 
    published by Chronicle Books, San Francisco, copyright 1996, 
    and are used with the publisher’s permission. 
    And Don’t Plant These 
    Hottentot fig (iceplant with yellow flowers),  
    Vinca major (periwinkle), 
    licorice plant,  
    scarlet wisteria,  
    broom,  
    pampasgrass,  
    cotoneaster. 
    California Invasive Plant Council information notes 
    that these plants are among those that have escaped 
    from gardens and are displacing native species and 
    changing natural processes in the San Francisco Bay 
    area. The Web site www.cal-ipc.org offers 
    alternatives to the plants listed above and gives details 
    about the problems these “pest plants” create when 
    they go wild. Some Lakewood Village residents 
    know, for example, that rats favor ivy growth. ■

    Dumpster Day Is Saturday, June 30, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.          
     
    Dumpster Day Rules 
    YES!: 
    •Dumping on Saturday June 30, 2007. 
    •Trash 
    •Furniture 
    •Wood 
    •Fence wood 
    •Appliances (except refrigerators or air conditioning 
    units) 
    •Car parts and car body pieces without ANY fluids 
    •Small amounts of concrete (no entire driveways!) 
    •Small amounts of construction debris (no commercial 
    dumping!) 
    •Tires (ONLY in the tire dumpster, which will be 
    located between the two gas stations at the Lakewood 
    Village Shopping Center). 
    •Dried paint 
     
    Help Lakewood Village keep Dumpster Day by 
    respecting the volunteer dumpster monitors and the 
    dumping rules! 
     
    → → → → → → → → → → → → → 
    Take items in the No list at right to the SMaRT 
    Station, 164 Carl Road on June 16, July 21, or any 
    other third Saturday of the month, between 8 a.m. 
    and 1 p.m.. To get to the SMaRT Station, go north on 
    Lawrence Expressway (becomes Caribbean Drive) to 
    Borregas Avenue and turn right. Follow the signs for 
    the Household Hazardous Waste Drop-off Event. 
    Take the hazwaste in a sealed container that doesn’t 
    leak or bags or buckets that you don’t want to keep. 
    Take no more than 5 gallons or 50 pounds per trip to 
    the station. 
     
     
    NO, NO, NO: 
    •Dumping before Saturday June 30, 2007. 
    •Computer monitors (see No Hazwaste on page 1) 
    •Television sets (see No Hazwaste on page 1) 
    •Refrigerators 
    •Air conditioning units 
    •Car parts with fluids 
    •Big loads of concrete 
    •Commercial construction debris 
    •Motor oil 
    •Adhesives 
    •Pool chemicals 
    •Oil filters 
    •Insecticides 
    •Aerosol cans 
    •Antifreeze 
    •Herbicides 
    •Automotive fluids 
    •Mercury (thermometers) 
    •Batteries 
    •Fire extinguishers 
    •Household & automotive cleaners 
    •Latex and oil-based paints and stains (paint that has 
    been poured onto newspaper and dried is okay to 
    dump) 
    •Flammable or combustible liquids 
    •Compressed gas cylinders 
    •Syringes (syringes may be placed in an approved 
    sharps container or 2 liter bottle with cap so the 
    contents are visible).
