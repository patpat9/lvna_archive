The success of Neighborhood Clean - Up Weekend will go a long in making Lakewood Village an even more desirable neighborhood The appearance of the neighborhood can be thought of as the sum of the appearances of our individual homes, This is an important concept to keep in mind when making changes to the outside appearance of your home. Before you make a change ask your self one very important question. What would the neighborhood look like if everyone was to do the same thing? Ifthe answer is not a positive one please re- think what you were about to do. Here are some examples. You want to use garage for a workshop, so you put that extra car in the street. What would the neighborhood look like if every car was parked in lhe street'? You want to keep your garbage can or recycling bins in thc driveway because its easier for you. What would it look like if everybody did the same thing? You want a chain link fence in the front yard because its easy maintenance for you. What would the neighborhood look like if we all had chain link fences? You want to push home a shopping cart from the market and leave it out in the front yard until they come by and take it. What if everyone had a Shopping cart in their front yard? None of these things contribute to an attractive neighborhood. So please, when making changes consider the contribution your home makes to the total appearance of the neighborhood Matthew Castrigno - L.V.N .A.     Public Safety Corner Neighborhood Watch Month Bob Issacs . Neighborhood Resource Officer 730-7145 More than I .9 million crimes are reported in California every year and many remain unreported. State law enforcement officials attribute some of the increase in reported crimes to greater citizen awareness. Voluntary crime prevention efforts such as Neighborhood Watch have proven effective in encouraging greater citizen participation. The purpose of California Neighborhood Watch Month is to recognize and encourage even greater participation in this program. August has been selected as the Neighborhood Watch month in Sunnyvale. Neighborhood Watch meetings offer information on home security and property identification. They help neighbors cooperate with one another. Neighborhood Watch promotes good security practices, observant and caring neighbors and cooperation between citizens and law enforcement. Citizens work with local law enforcement agencies to: • Report suspicious activities • Assist in property identification • Conduct home security surveys • Use home security measures If you are interested in preventing crime in your neighborhood, contact the Department of Public Safety Community Services Bureau at 730-7140. Parents...are your children home alone? KidCall is a new toll-free telephone line 1-800-KGO-7549 for children throughout the Bay Area who are home by themselves after school. Specially screened and trained adult volunteers. supported by the Lucile Packard Children's Hospital pediatric nurses. provide comfort, information and emotional support by phone to children from 3 PM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday. During these after-school hours, KidCaII volunteers can: help with homework, answer questions, read stories, play games, tell jokes, ease loneliness and fears. This service is not a crisis line. Callers remain anonymous, except for first names and zip codes. KidCall is meant to supplement and strengthen family networks in the community. Circe de Santa Clara College. Before fence was removed Anti-Graffiti Update We've had a lot of success combating graffiti in the Village but a new form of graffiti has popped up. You've all seen it. It is Garage/Yard Sale signs scrawled on everything from pieces of cardboard to computer print outs. Though they serve a useful purpose, once the sale is over, they look as bad as graffiti. Please, when your sale is over, try to remember where you posted your signs, then go around and remove all of them (and the tape). You'll get a warm feeling knowing that you acted responsibly, your neighbors will thank you for making our Village look better and you can count your money without any guilt feelings. Remember, if you don't remove your signs, we know who you are and where to find you.
