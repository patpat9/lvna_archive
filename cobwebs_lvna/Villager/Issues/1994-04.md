    LVNA President Proposes Arming
    Crime Spotters with Cellular
    Phones
    
    At the March 21st City Council meeting, LVNA
    President, Matthew Castrigno asked the City to
    provide the LVNA cellular phones so that the LVNA
    can organize a volunteer crime spotting patrol. The
    patrols would focus on problem areas but could
    encompass the entire neighborhood. The phones
    would be programmed to only be capable of calling
    911 and Public Safety directly. Castrigno has
    discussed the idea with Public Safety before as a
    general crime fighting tactic but the recent surge in
    gunfire prompted him to take the concept directly to
    the City Council to help get it off the ground. The
    LVNA Board and City Officials will be meeting this
    month to discuss the proposal and the recent incidents
    involving gunfire. If anyone thinks they may want to
    volunteer for the crime spotting patrol call Matthew
    Castrigno at 734-5442.'¢
    
    Letter from the President
    
    Hello Lakewood Villagers. My name is Matthew Castrigno.
    I am honored to be able to serve as the 94/95 President of the
    LVNA. Some of you may recall me as the acting President in
    the Fall of 91 when the organization was just starting. Well I
    am back and ready to serve. I want to thank all the people
    who have worked very hard to make the organization what it is
    today. It is truly impressive what all of you have done in the
    past 2 1/2 years.
    
    The outgoing 93/94 board has made an excellent hand off to
    the incoming board. My thanks to them for helping to make
    the transition a smooth one. We hope to pick up and carry
    through with the many projects that were started by the 93/94
    board. The incoming board is off to a great financial start of
    $3600 in the bank and plan to make the best possible use of
    that money.
    
    I am very excited about the rest of the 94/95 LVNA Board;
    Debbie Nixon, as the Ist Vice President, Nick Wright 2nd
    Vice President, Carmen Paredes as Secretary and Angie De La
    Cruz as Treasurer. As Individuals they all bring their own
    special skills to the organization and collectively we will make
    a strong Board with a wide breadth of abilities. 94/95 will be
    a great year in which the organization will continue to grow
    stronger, building on all of the great work that has already
    been done. I look forward to working with all of you in the
    coming year.

    Fairwood Park Update
    
    The Supervisor of Parks has informed the LVNA that
    the City expects to secure the permit for the
    improvements to the Hetch Hetchy ri ght-of-way by the
    end of the month. Once the permit is secured the
    entire Fairwood Park Project will go out for bid. The
    bids will include improvements along the bike path
    from Blazingwood to Fairwood as a separate line item.
    Should the winning bid be over budget, the
    improvements along the bike path from Blazingwood
    to Fairwood will be dropped from the project. The
    Fairwood Park Project includes the renovation of the
    school grounds as well as new facilities including
    restrooms, tennis courts and a volleyball area. @
    
    Airport Noise
    
    Are the jets from San Jose International sometimes so
    loud they sound like they are flying into your living
    room? The jets are required to fly noise abatement
    patterns - but sometimes don't. If the noise is
    particularly loud from a jet flying by it is important to
    call and record a complaint when it happens So that
    non-compliant pilots and airlines are held accountable.
    In addition there is a curfew between 11:30 PM and
    6:30 am when you should not hear any jets. To leave
    a recorded complaint call 998-0707. During business
    hours you may be able to get a live person, Larry
    Galindo, at 277-4111. Mr. Galindo is the noise
    control person for the City of San Jose. There is a
    very effective group in San Jose dedicated to reducing
    noise from the San Jose Airport. For more
    information call Matthew Castrigno at 734-5442.8

    Lawrence Expressway Project
    Update
    
    Matthew Castrigno - 734-5442
    
    The City of Sunnyvale has hired a consulting firm to
    determine the feasibility of the proposals made by
    Matthew Castrigno to the City Council to connect
    Wildwood Avenue directly to Lawrence Expressway
    with a complete intersection. That study has shown
    that one of the proposals would provide an intersection
    that would not impact the Lawrence Expressway
    project or the Caltrans overpass project and would
    provide traffic relief to our neighborhood both now
    and in the future. In a recent discussion with the new
    LVNA President Matthew Castrigno, Louis Montini,
    Assistant Director at the Santa Clara County
    Transportation Agency, indicated that the County is
    evaluating the study and will comment on it. The
    comments were not available at press time. Mr.
    Montini also indicated that the County has now
    decided to develop a landscaping plan for the project.
    This will allow for landscaping to be added easily in
    the future. #
    
    Kindergarten Registration
    
    Sunnyvale School District has announced that
    Kindergarten registration began Monday, April 4.
    Parents who have children born on or before
    December 2, 1989 may pre-register their child to start
    school next September.
    
    You will need to have proof of the following
    immunizations: Poliomyelitis, measles, diphtheria,
    tetanus, whooping cough, mumps, tuberculosis, and
    rubella. You will also need verification of date of birth
    and proof of residence. @

    Parking Hints
    
    When vehicles are parked properly everyone in the
    community benefits. As a friendly reminder, review
    the following parking hints as stated by the
    Department of Public Safety :
    
    @ Vehicles parked on the street must be licensed and
    operable, and cannot be left in one location for more
    than 120 hours (5 days).
    
    @ Inoperable vehicles parked on private property
    cannot be relocated to a garage or a carport.
    
    @ All vehicles must be parked on a permanent paved
    surface such as asphalt, Portland Cement or other
    approved stabilized permanent surface. The entire
    vehicle must be parked on a permanent paved surface,
    not just the tires.
    
    @ Vehicles should be parked so they do not block or
    partially block the sidewalk or a neighbor's driveway.
    
    m@ Boats must be licensed and stored on a trailer which
    is operable and registered. Boats must also be
    covered.
    
    If you wish to make an anonymous complaint
    concerning vehicles, please call the Department of
    Public Safety at 730-7110. To anonymously report
    abandoned vehicles call the Abandoned Vehicle
    Hotline 730-7706. ‘¢
