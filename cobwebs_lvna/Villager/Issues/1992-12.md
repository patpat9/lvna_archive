    Profile on A.B.A.T.E.—A Participant in the
    L.V.N.A. Holiday Parade
    by Robert Wickersham
    
    Ten years ago, a motorcycle organization was formed. They
    called themselves American Bikers Against Totalitarian Ethics,
    or A.B,A.T.E. Over the years, the name has changed to Ameri-
    can Brotherhood Aimed Towards Education to better reflect the
    organization's goals.
    
    In that time, A.B.A.T.E. has grown to 43 chapters, with a to-
    tal of 7000 members and growing. A.B.A.T.E. now has a Politi-
    cal Action Committee and a full-time lobbyist in Sacramento
    who were responsible for such state government actions as open-
    ing commuter lanes to motorcycles and approving the Purple
    Heart license plate.
    
    In addition, A.B.A.T-.E. co-sponsored the "Love Ride"
    which raised thousands of dollars for the fight against muscular
    dystrophy. A.B.A.T.E. sponsors a number of activities every
    year, including annual state runs, poker runs, family picnics,
    campouts, rodeos, charity runs for organizations such as the Ke-
    vin Collins Foundation, and toy runs for kids in need.
    
    The Local #32 Chapter of the Santa Clara Valley was
    formed in October of last year. With a dedicated committee con-
    sisting of President Hans Proskauer, Vice President Cliff Dodd,
    Treasurer Lonny Doi, and many others, Local #32 has a total of
    218 members and continues to grow stronger. As a member of
    Local #32, I would like to thank the L.V.N.A. for inviting us to
    participate in their upcoming Holiday Parade. |_|

    Parks & Recreation
    
    The Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Department offers many ser-
    vices and classes at the Sunnyvale Community Center at 550 E.
    Remington Drive. But they also offer several programs for
    young people right here in Lakewood, For general information
    on any Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Program, call 730-7530.
    
    Lakewood Daycare Fundays
    Gives licensed Sunnyvale family daycare providers an
    opportunity to network with each other and have their
    participants play, create arts & crafts, and join in many
    activities. Space is limited to 25 children. Fundays is of-
    fered Fridays starting in January at the Lakewood Park
    building. The fee is $15 for 6 or fewer children, $30 for
    7 or more. Call 730-7521 for registration or information.
    
    Kids Club
    Drop in to Lakewood Park for this free afterschool pro-
    gram for 6-12 year olds. Club members will enjoy arts
    & crafts, games, and snacks from 3:15-5:15 P.M. on
    Mondays and Wednesdays. Kids Club begins on January
    4th. For more information call 730-7523.
    
    Art-Rageous
    An outrageous arts & crafts class for 6-12 year olds held
    Thursdays from 3:15-5:15 P.M. at the Lakewood Park

    valentines, and have all kinds of fun! The fee is $15 for
    6 sessions and includes all snacks and supplies for your
    child. Permission slips will be sent homewith your child

    gin January 7th. For more information call 730-7521.
    
    Super Special Events
