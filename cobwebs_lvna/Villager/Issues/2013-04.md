    Home Improvement Grants 
     
    The City of Sunnyvale offers grants and loans to low 
    income Sunnyvale residents to help maintain and improve 
    their homes. 
    The City of Sunnyvale has several home 
    improvement programs to choose from: 
     
    Housing Rehabilitation Loans for Home 
    Owners 
    Deferred loans of up to $60,000 ($15,000 for 
    mobile homes) to fund deferred maintenance 
    and repairs of plumbing, roofing, electrical, 
    and/or heating systems, and/or windows, flooring and wall 
    repairs or replacement. 
     
    Home Access Grants for Home Owners and 
    Renters 
    Grants of up to $6,500 for seniors and/or disabled 
    low income homeowners or renters to retrofit the 
    home to eliminate physical barriers which inhibit the use of 
    the home and/or improve accessibility, such as: grab bars, 
    hand rails, grab bars, and ramps. 
     
    Exterior Paint Loans and Grants fro Home 
    Owners 
    Loans of up to $4,000 to pay for labor and 
    materials for exterior painting or grants up to 
    $1,000 for paint and supplies for exterior 
    painting. 
     
    Energy Efficiency Loans and Grants for 
    Home Owners 
    Deferred loans of up to $25,000 or a matching 
    grant of up to $5,000 to increase the energy 
    efficiency of low income households, including 
    insulation, weather stripping, air sealing, and 
    the replacement of inefficient and aging 
    appliances, heating systems, windows, and other types or 
    weatherization improvements. 
     
    Emergency Grants for Home Owners 
    Grants up to $2,500 to fund urgent health and safety 
    repairs, such as: burst pipes, water heater repairs, heating 
    repairs and sewer line repairs.

    The "10 Most Unwanted" List! 
     
    Tips from Neighborhood Preservation on How to Keep 
    Your Property Attractive. 
     
    1 - Trash and recycling containers must be 
    screened from public view or stored in the side 
    yard behind the fence. 
     
    2 - Outside storage of junk, debris or materials 
    must not be visible from the street. 
     
    3 - Fences visible from the street must be maintained in 
    good condition and not sagging or leaning. 
     
    4 - Graffiti must be removed immediately. 
     
    5 - Major auto repair is allowed only 
    within an enclosed structure and may not 
    be done for profit. 
     
    6 - Parking of vehicles, boats and trailers 
    is not allowed on the landscaped portion 
    or the yard. 
     
    7 - Inoperable, wrecked or dismantled vehicles may not 
    be visible from the street. 
     
    8 - Vegetation in the park strip or on private 
    property may not impair visibility of the street 
    or sidewalk and must not exceed two feet in 
    height in the park strip. 
     
    9 - Weeds must be removed to avoid health 
    and fire hazards. 
     
    10 - Home businesses require a business license and 
    must meet specific requirements.   

    The City also receives anonymous calls 
    reporting violations and they will follow 
    them up. The fines can range from 
    $100.00 to $500.00!  
     
    Neighborhood Preservation then does an investigation that 
    lasts about tree days.  The owner of the property will first    
    receive a courtesy notice and then two warnings to fix the 
    problems before a citation is issued.  Between January and 
    February, there were 16 complaints and 45 active cases 
    against homes in the Lakewood/Fairwood area.  The City 
    does everything they can to help you out before they cite you.   
    Garage Conversions: you must have a permit 
    and a separate carport built.  If you have lived in 
    Lakewood/Fairwood during the 70’s and 80’s you 
    were grandfather in.  One side note        regard-
    ing the garage, there is no sleeping in it    al-
    lowed! Even with a permit most insurance com-
    panies will not cover a conversion, so check your 
    policy first.  
      
    Construction Hours:  
    Repairs  done by a homeowner are allowed: 
    Monday – Friday: 7am to 7pm  
    Saturday -8am to 7pm and  
    Sunday   - 9am to 6pm.   
    If you hire a professional, the hours are:           Mon-
    day-Friday -7am – 6pm  
     
       Saturday 8am to 5pm 
    And no work on Sunday or National holiday’s.  
     
    Front Yard Fence: can be no higher than 4 feet. The back-
    yard fence can be up to 6 feet; unless you get a permit, then it 
    can go to 8 feet.  
     New Driveway: You don’t need a permit 
    for it, but it can only be 50% of the front 
    yard.  You can get a more detailed list 
    from the City of Sunnyvale’s web site.  If 
    you have any questions you can also con-
    tact Neighborhood Preservation at (408) 
    730-7610.  My advice is to check things 
    out before stating your remodel of repairs 
    so that you will not have to take things 
    down which in turn will cost you more 
    money.
