    Being a parent is a very special job. You are your child’s first
    and most important teacher. Your example teaches your child
    how to listen and talk with others. You can make a big
    difference in how well your child develops communication
    skills. It is important to help your child learn and practice
    these skills at home. This can be an overwhelming task,
    especially if your child has communication difficulties. Your
    child may have problems listening and following directions
    (receptive language). Your child’s speech may not be clear
    (articulation). Or, your child may use shorter sentences than
    other children the same age (expressive language).
    
    The Sunnyvale School District provides free speech-language
    evaluations and therapy for preschool children (ages 3 to 5
    years). If you have concerns about your child’s development
    of communication skills, please call the Sunnyvale Preschool
    Center (522-8287) at Columbia School
