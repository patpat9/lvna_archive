Your example teaches your child how to listen and talk with others. You can make a big difference in how well your child develops communication skills. It is important to help your child learn and practice these skills at home. This can be an overwhelming task, especially if your child has communication difficulties. Your child may have problems listening and following directions (receptive language). Your child's speech may not be clear (articulation). Or, your child may use shorter sentences than other children the same age (expressive language). The Sunnyvale School District provides free speech-language evaluations and therapy for preschool children (ages 3 to 5 years).
