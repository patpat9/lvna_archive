Neighborhood Participation by Bill 8 Mary Magnie, 744-1234 We moved to Lakewood Village from East San Jose, We had heard all the bad stories about this area, but decided any place would be better than where we were. Well, it was better, but not by much. Drug dealing, graffiti, abandoned cars, run down houses, and a "who cares" attitude greeted us. We were very glad when concerned people such as our- selves started up the 'Lakewood Village Neighborhood Associa- tion" in October of 1991. We thought "WOW, this is great!" Our area now has a chance to be known as a better place to live, com- pared to what it was known for in the past. We went to as many meetings as we could. We saw lots Of people interested in improving our neighborhood and plenty of others wanting to get involved in the beginning. But now, aftera year and a half, the attendance has dramatically declined. What started out as a massive community effort has dwin- dled down to a handful of people straining to keep the L.V.N.A. alive. Sure, everybody likes the "free" BBQs, all the dumpsters that were put out for clean-up, and the holiday parade, but most people seem to think this happens all by itself. Well wake up people, it doesn't. In the last year and a half this neighborhood has made tremendous strides in appearance and self esteem, Don't let it go to waste. We need everyone to GET INVOLVED. The handful of people that are involved are TIRED of the work. We need more volunteers to pass out flyers and more participation in neighbor- hood activities. Any help would be greatly appreciated.     Announcements Thank you to everyone for their generous outpouring of dona- tions for the family who had their home destroyed in a fire. Everything was much needed and much appreciated. The Lakewood Village Brownie and Girl Scout troops are al- most done with their cookie sales. They are sorry if they have missed you so far. There are still a few boxes left in all seven fla- vors. cookies are $3 a box. call Nelda King at 734-3772. There have been reports in the neighborhood of sparks and fires where dead branches come in contact with P.G.& E. power lines. Please keep trees trimmed of dead branches where they touch power lines. For more information, contact P.G.& E. Are you interested in starting a co-ed softball league out here in Lakewood? The league could be for adults only, or for families. Contact Steve Dorrough at 734-4557 or Greg Hall at 745-7420 to let them know if you might be interested. A local not-for-profit agency is collecting used and broken computers which they repair and donate to neighborhood schools. If you or your company is discarding IBM-compatible or Apple computers, call 734-5030 to make a tax-deductible con- tribution instead. Volunteers are also needed to spend an evening testing, repairing, and rebuilding the donated computers. There have been several acts of vandalism in and around Lake- wood Park Center in the past month. If you are driving by the park, please keep an eye out for any suspicious activity. Report any potential vandalism to the desk officer at the Public Safety Department and 730-7110. Let's keep our park safe and usable. Home Security Tips from Officer Jeremy Watney • Lock your doors and windows • Have solid core doors and deadbolts on all doors into the house, including the door from the garage • Install an alarm • Keep house lit, with timed lights and motion detectors • Keep bushes short, so burglars can't hide behind them • Make house numbers visible on the house and curb • Keep your garage door closed all the time • Don't flaunt your possessions • Don't depend on your dog • Call 730-7148 for a free home security check • Know your neighbors; report suspicious activities L.V.N.A.
