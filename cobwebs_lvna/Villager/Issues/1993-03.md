    Officer Watney Speaks On Home Safety
    
    If you are among the hundreds of Lakewood residents who
    were unable to attend the February meeting for whatever reason,
    you may be interested to hear about the presentation by represen-
    tatives of the Public Safety Department.
    
    Our former Neighborhood Resource Officer, Tom Hoppin,
    introduced his replacement, Jeremy Watney. Before saying his
    farewells, Officer Hoppin congratulated Lakewood on having
    the most active neighborhood association in Sunnyvale.
    
    Officer Watney then informed us that in spite of having one
    of the lowest burglary rates in the city, Lakewood has had some
    burglaries recently. He offered some specific tips for protecting
    your home against burglaries. Those tips are printed on the back

    Neighborhood Participation
    by Bill & Mary Magnie, 744-1234
    
    We moved to Lakewood Village from East San Jose. We
    had heard all the bad stories about this area, but decided any
    place would be better than where we were. Well, it was better,
    
    but not by much. Drug dealing, graffiti, abandoned cars, run
    down houses, and a "who cares" attitude greeted us.
    
    We were very glad when concerned people such as our-
    selves started up the "Lakewood Village Neighborhood Associa-
    tion" in October of 1991. We thought "WOW, this is great!" Our
    area now has a chance to be known as a better place to live, com-
    pared to what it was known for in the past.
    
    We went to as many meetings as we could. We saw lots of
    people interested in improving our neighborhood and plenty of
    others wanting to get involved in the beginning. But now, after a
    year and a half, the attendance has dramatically declined.
    
    What started out as a massive community effort has dwin-
    dled down to a handful of people straining to keep the L.V.N.A.
    alive, Sure, everybody likes the "free" BBQs, all the dumpsters
    that were put out for clean-up, and the holiday parade, but most
    people seem to think this happens all by itself. Well wake up
    people, it doesn't. In the last year and a half this neighborhood
    has made tremendous strides in appearance and self esteem.
    Don't let it go to waste.
    
    We need everyone to GET INVOLVED. The handful of
    people that are involved are TIRED of the work. We need more
    volunteers to pass out flyers and more participation in neighbor-
    hood activities. Any help would be greatly appreciated. |
    
    Letter from the President
    
    In my last message as president, I would like
    to express my thanks to everyone who has
    helped me to build the L.V.N.A. organization
    into the successful association it is! With
    the new board, I would ask for your support
    for the continued success of the organization.
    
    In the past year, we have gained recognition
    by city, county, and state officials. We must
    continue these good relationships. Attendance
    at monthly meetings, participation in events,
    and the newsletter are also very important to
    our success. Let's keep up the good work!
    
    Jessica Lee was nominated for president, and
    she has accepted. Jessica is an excellent
    candidate and brings with her good skills. I
    hope that all who have indicated they would
    support her do so. If you do not know Jessica,
    I hope you will get to know her and also
    support her.
    
    There are other board positions still open and
    I hope to see some candidates at the March
    elections!
    
    Vicki Piazza, L.V.N.A. President

    Announcements
    
    Thank you to everyone for their generous outpouring of dona-
    tions for the family who had their home destroyed in a fire.
    Everything was much needed and much appreciated.
    
    The Lakewood Village Brownie and Girl Scout troops are al-
    most done with their cookie sales. They are sorry if they have
    missed you so far. There are still a few boxes left in all seven fla-
    vors. Cookies are $3 a box. Call Nelda King at 734-3772.
    
    There have been reports in the neighborhood of sparks and
    fires where dead branches come in contact with P.G.& E. power
    lines. Please keep trees trimmed of dead branches where they
    touch power lines. For more information, contact P.G.& E.
    
    Are you interested in starting a co-ed softball league out here in
    Lakewood? The league could be for adults only, or for families.
    Contact Steve Dorrough at 734-4557 or Greg Hall at 745-7420
    to let them know if you might be interested.
    
    A local not-for-profit agency is collecting used and broken
    computers which they repair and donate to neighborhood
    schools. If you or your company is discarding IBM-compatible
    or Apple computers, call 734-5030 to make a tax-deductible con-
    tribution instead. Volunteers are also needed to spend an evening
    testing, repairing, and rebuilding the donated computers.
    
    There have been several acts of vandalism in and around Lake-
    wood Park Center in the past month. If you are driving by the
    park, please keep an eye out for any suspicious activity. Report
    any potential vandalism to the desk officer at the Public Safety
    Department and 730-7110. Let's keep our park safe and usable.
    
    Home Security Tips _ from Officer Jeremy Watney
    
    .
    
    Lock your doors and windows
    
    Have solid core doors and deadbolts on all doors into
    the house, including the door from the garage
    
    Install an alarm
    
    Keep house lit, with timed lights and motion detectors
    
    Keep bushes short, so burglars can't hide behind them
    
    Make house numbers visible on the house and curb
    
    Keep your garage door closed all the time
    
    Don't flaunt your possessions
    
    Don't depend on your dog
    
    Call 730-7148 for a free home security check
    Know your neighbors; report suspicious activities

    Newsletter Contributions
    
    All information in the Villager is contributed by residents of
    Lakewood. If you have an article, announcement, or request for
    information which you would like published, contact newsletter
    editor Linda Cheung at 734-1661.
    
    The deadline for most months' newsletters is the first day of
    the month. Distribution is on the weekend before the third Thurs-
    day of the month. We will attempt to to print all submissions as
    space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any
    questions or concerns about the newsletter.
    
    Parks & Recreation
    
    The Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Department offers many ser-
    vices and classes at the Sunnyvale Community Center at 550 E.
    Remington Drive. But they also offer several programs for
    young people right here in Lakewood. For general information
    on any Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Program, call 730-7530.
    
    Toddler Time
    
    A cooperative preschool program for 3—5 year olds, em-
    phasizing socialization and play. Parents must sign up
    to assist the program specialist for a minimum of one
    class per registered child. Thursdays from 9:30-11:30
    A.M., April 29 through June 10. $25 per child,$5 family
    discount. Children must be 3 years of age and toilet
    trained. For information and registration, call 730-7515.
    
    Kids Club
    Free afterschool program at Lakewood Park Center for
    6-12 year olds. Club members will enjoy arts & crafts,
    games, and snacks from 3:15-5:15 P.M. on Mondays and
    Wednesdays. For more information call 730-7523.
    
    ArtRageous
    An outrageous arts & crafts class for 6-12 year olds held
    Thursdays from 3:15-5:15 P.M. at the Lakewood Park
    building. The fee is $15 for 6 sessions and includes all
    snacks and supplies for your child. The next session be-
    gins April 8th. For more information call 730-7521.
    
    Super Special Events
    These hour-and-a-half-events are held every three weeks
    at Lakewood School. Future programs will include Bun-
    ny Bonanza, April Fool's Month, and Wacky Olympics.
    (Children in ArtRageous participate as well.)
