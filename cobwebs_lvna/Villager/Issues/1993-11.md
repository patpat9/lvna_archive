    Holiday Parade and Lighting Contest
    
    Ready — Set — Lights!!!
    
    It's that time again. The L.V.N.A.'s third annual Holiday
    Lighting Contest is almost here. This year, the judging will take
    place on Wednesday, December 15, at 6:00 P.M. sharp.
    
    In previous years, the contest has proven to be a big success,
    and this year the committee would like to see even more involve-
    ment. Like other neighborhoods who receive media attention,

    So pull out those lights and be creative. December 15 will
    be here before you know it. Call 747-0452 with any questions.

    The time is fast approaching. The second annual L.V.N.A.
    Holiday Parade will be held on Saturday, December 11. If you
    are the oldest or youngest resident in Lakewood Village, call Pat
    Maloney at 734-3594 and you will be included in the parade fun.

    expected, and if in time, baby and mom will be in the parade.

    have a day of fun with the L.V.N.A.! Happy Holidays!! |_|

    Welcome New Elected Officials
    
    The L.V.N.A. wishes to send its sincere congratulations to
    our newly elected officials: Sunnyvale City Councilmembers
    Robin Parker, Jim Roberts, and Landon Noll, and School Board
    members Peggy Quillinan, Bill McDowell, and Linda Kilian.
    
    While we are looking forward to working with these new
    members, we also want to thank the officials who have served
    our city and district in the past: Mayor Pat Castillo, Larry Stone,
    Alfonso Guevara, and Jeanne Gonzales.
    
    Regrettably, only about 25% of the registered voters in
    Lakewood voted on November 2. This is compared to a 65%
    turnout in 1992. If Lakewood Village wants to influence City
    government and other elected officials to consider our interests,
    we need to maintain a higher turnout at the polls. | |
    
    Lakewood Preschool Playgroup
    by Debbie Bakker, 734-3688
    
    Are you a parent of preschool children? Do you know about
    the Lakewood Park playgroup? Every Tuesday morning, for a
    year and a half now, parents and preschoolers have been getting
    together to provide a positive play environment for the kids and
    a support network for the parents. We create arts & crafts, con-
    sume delicious snacks, and play to our hearts' content.
    
    We have had many wonderful experiences getting to know
    the neighborhood children from newborn on up to 5-year-olds,
    but for me personally, making friends with other parents of pre-
    schoolers has been especially important and gratifying.
    
    We went from 8 children in 1991 to 38 this summer. But
    now the playgroup needs some new life. September seemed to
    catch everyone off guard, and participation has dwindled.
    
    The Lakewood Park playgroup needs some enthusiastic par-
    ents to give it a kickstart! The City gives the playgroup free use
    of the building when it is rainy or cold, and we have toys inside.
    The playgroup is whatever Lakewood parents want to make of it.
    Please call me to discuss how you can get involved and keep this
    experience going for our kids. |
