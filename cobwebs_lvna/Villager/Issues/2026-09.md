    City Sidewalk Repairs Underway: Sunnyvale concrete and tree crews have begun grinding and replacing
    damaged sidewalks across Lakewood Village to improve neighborhood walkability and accessibility.
    Village Vistas — Lakewood Elementary Hollyhocks: A heartfelt thank you to our neighborhood gardeners
    for adding color and charm along school pathways with blooming hollyhocks.
    Volunteer Needed — LVNA Board Secretary: We are seeking a community-minded neighbor to join the
    LVNA board as Secretary to record meeting minutes and assist with official correspondence.
    Thank You, Dumpster Day Heroes: More than two dozen volunteer monitors and drivers came together to
    keep our streets clean, partnering with Goodwill and helping BikEx rescue 6 bicycles from landfills!
    Lakewood Elementary School: Noon Aides Help Needed!
    Big excitement at Lakewood Park! The newly renovated playground is officially open and ready for action.
    Neighborhood kids and families are already having a blast exploring the colorful new climbing structures,
    swings, and slides.
    Right next door, the finishing touches are underway on our brand-new Lakewood Branch Library and
    Learning Center. The City of Sunnyvale is completing the final construction and interior setup, with the
    grand opening celebration scheduled for later this fall. Stay tuned to https://sunnyvale.ca.gov and
    upcoming LVNA announcements for the official opening date and library hours!
