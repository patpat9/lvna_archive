    - LAKEWOOD PARK RENOVATION -
    There is an update on the City of Sunnyvale’s plans to renovate Lakewood Park! For anyone who is not aware, the City received
    grant funding from the County of Santa Clara in September 2020 to renovate the park. The funding will support building an
    all-inclusive playground and other improvements. This will be the first major renovation to Lakewood Park since it was built in
    1964. In Fall 2022, the City awarded the design contract to Verde Design and the design phase began.
    On January 12, the City is holding the first Community Meeting from 6-7pm. The meeting will be held virtually. The meeting is a
    chance to learn more about the project, provide your input, and get questions answered.

    - LVNA Needs Help -
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new neighbors? Would
    you be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends, and the next generation
    who’s currently growing up right outside your door or even in your own backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President,
    Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in March and a new board will begin. The board
    meets once per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to neighborhood general meetings and a few
    seasonal events each year. The level of commitment is up to each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is
    a one-year term (March 2023 - March 2024). The only requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the
    neighborhood, and are able to serve the full 12 month term.
    WANTED - VILLAGER EDITOR
    Want to help, but not interested in a board position? We are looking for a new Villager Editor, a position open
    to anyone who is interested (no age requirement). The role consists of at least proof-reading the content and
    compiling it into a single document. Previous editors have also helped write content, design a new layout, take
    photos, and send the Villagers out to the subscribers each month.
    Want to partner with a family member or friend? Great! Are you a high school student interested in writing or
    media design and trying to increase your community service hours or build a portfolio? This is your chance!
    Any of these roles are a great opportunity to help actively drive change for our community and make a positive
    impact on our neighborhood. If interested, please email the board at board@lvnasv.org and let them know
    you’re on board (yes, pun fully intended).

    - Help Outdoor Cats in our Community with TNR-
    Santa Clara County has hundreds of thousands of free
    roaming cats. Our local animal shelter for Sunnyvale,
    Humane Society of Silicon Valley (HSSV), offers a FREE
    trap-neuter-return (TNR) program for feral and
    community cats.
    In a TNR program, feral cats are trapped, fixed and
    vaccinated, and returned to the exact spot where they
    were trapped. TNR is the only proven way to stop
    breeding and population growth. If you are feeding
    outdoor community cats, please make sure the cats are
    spayed/neutered and each cat has a left ear clipped. If
    you need to borrow humane cat traps, submit a request
    on bayareacats.org/tnr or call Sunnyvale Animal
    Control.
    Go here to get started on trapping: bayareacats.org/hssv.
    No appointments are needed for Sunnyvale cats. Once
    you’ve trapped the cat, bring it to HSSV intake between
    9:00am and 6:00pm (7 days a week). If you are unable
    to transport, call Sunnyvale Animal Control
    (408-730-7178 and press 0) and they can transport
    the cat for you Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday
    before 3pm.
    Contact Bay Area Cats if you are uncomfortable holding
    the cat overnight by texting 408-384-8694.
    Feral cats do not need food 24/7 - please feed
    responsibly by monitored feeding, twice per day at a
    max of 30 minutes. Feeding cats on a schedule allows
    monitoring for injuries, sickness, and helps identify new
    unfixed cats or kittens.
    Help keep our feral cat populations healthy and safe! If
    you have questions, file a TNR request for guidance at
    bayareacats.org/tnr. If you need help with
    spaying/neutering your outdoor cat, please email
    bayareatnr@gmail.com.
