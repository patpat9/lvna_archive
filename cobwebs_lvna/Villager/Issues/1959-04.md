    MIGREEN THUMB (Softwood Cuttings)
    | By S. Deloy
    
    | b.. is one of the best months for taking
    and rooting softwood cuttings. Softwood is
    the stems of plants that remain green and
    soft the year around such as geraniums, or
    carmations, or the green wood now forming
    on many deciduous shrubs such as roses,
    viburnum, chrysanthemums, etc. The best
    cuttings are taken from unblooming side
    shoots, from 3 to 6" down from the tip,
    with a sharp knife though much longer
    cuttings are sometimes taken to produce
    larger plants in a shorter period of time.
    At least one leaf should be left near the
    tip of the cutting, and the cut end should
    be placed in a rooting medium such as
    builder's sand, in a warm, shaded spot,
    and kept moist at all times. Roots should
    form on most plants from 2-8 weeks, at
    which time transplanting is in order.
    Coffee cans, baby food cans, milk car-
    9s, etc., make good containers fot these
    ings while they are forming roots.
    Buin, marigolds and many houseplants
    may be rooted by placing the cut ends in
    a jar of water.
