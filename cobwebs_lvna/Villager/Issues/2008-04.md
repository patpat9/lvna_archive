    Last meeting we celebrated all our volunteers. The
    events that we have in our neighborhood, such as the
    parade, the annual picnic, and Dumpster Day, would
    not be possible without the help of our many volun-
    teers. This newsletter that you receive would also not
    be possible without the people who walk the
    neighborhoods and pass it out each month. We are
    grateful for the contributions of all of you who are
    helping. Also, the advertisers are the ones who make
    it possible for us to afford this newsletter and greatly
    appreciate your patronage.
    Coming up on June 28 is our annual Dumpster Day.
    Many ofyou have monitored dumpsters in past
    years, and perhaps others want to help out for the
    first time. If you are available Saturday, June 28 and
    would like to help or have questions, please email
    me at president@lvna.net, or call me at 408-886-
    8037. We will have more details in the May Vil-
    lager.

    7:00ilm, we will be joined by Dominic Caserta, who
    is running for the State Assembly, District #22. This
    district includes Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, San Jose,
    Cupertino, and most of Mountain View.
    Dominic Caserta is seen as one of the front runners
    for the June 3rd Democratic primary. Whether you
    are a Republican or Democrat, this gives us an
    opportunity to participate in the political scene that
    directly impacts our city, and to ask questions. The
    more we see candidates' positions and are able to
    evaluate them, the more informed voters we will be.
    Our speaker for LVNA's May meeting will be Joe
    Esteves, the mayor of Milpitas, who is running for
    Santa Clara County Supervisor.
