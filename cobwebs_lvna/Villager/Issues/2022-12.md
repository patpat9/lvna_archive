    - Recap of the November Meeting -
    The November LVNA General Meeting took place at 7pm on Nov 9 in the park building at Lakewood Park. For
    this meeting, our Treasurer, Linh Ly, ran a project of creating Vision Boards. Vision Boards are displays of
    images and words that represent what individuals want our neighborhood to be.
    Common themes included a sense of community, knowing your neighbors (especially the people on your
    block), a place for kids to hang out after school, diversity, supporting your neighbors, and building good
    memories.

    - LVNA Needs Help -
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new neighbors? Would
    you be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends, and the next generation
    who’s currently growing up right outside your door or even in your own backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President,
    Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in March and a new board will begin. The board
    meets once per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to neighborhood general meetings and a few
    seasonal events each year. The level of commitment is up to each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is
    a one-year term (March 2023 - March 2024). The only requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the
    neighborhood, and are able to serve the full 12 month term.
    WANTED - VILLAGER EDITOR
    Want to help, but not interested in a board position? We are looking for a new Villager Editor, a position open
    to anyone who is interested (no age requirement). The role consists of at least proof-reading the content and
    compiling it into a single document. Previous editors have also helped write content, design a new layout, take
    photos, and send the Villagers out to the subscribers each month.
    Want to partner with a family member or friend? Great! Are you a high school student interested in writing or
    media design and trying to increase your community service hours or build a portfolio? This is your chance!
    Any of these roles are a great opportunity to help actively drive change for our community and make a positive
    impact on our neighborhood. If interested, please email the board at board@lvnasv.org and let them know
    you’re on board (yes, pun fully intended).

    - Sunnyvale Village Center Plan -
    The City of Sunnyvale has unveiled a master plan to create seven Village Centers in Sunnyvale. One of these
    Village Centers (Village Center 6) will be where the current Lakewood Shopping Center is located. A Village
    Center is intended as a pedestrian-oriented place with a mix of commercial and residential uses. The intent is
    to appeal to people of diverse incomes, lifestyles, and ages, and it should be easily accessed by pedestrian and
    bicycle trails or regular transit.
    To solicit resident feedback, the City ran an online survey in May 2022 and did a virtual workshop in June
    2022. A summary of the solicited feedback can be found online here:
    https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/3663/637951186321730000 Some
    important takeaways:
    -
    Page 2 shows the amount of feedback received by Village Center. Of the 502 survey respondents, only
    18% of them provided feedback on our Village Center 6. We need more input from our community on
    this development!
    -
    Page 3 lists the questions that were asked in the survey
    -
    Page 10 lists the themes from the results about our Village Center 6. Themes that emerged included a
    strong interest in a better grocery store; more restaurants; and easy access for bikes and pedestrians
    -
    Page 87 (as written in the pdf) / Page 101 (of the whole pdf) is the beginning of the detailed survey
    response regarding Village Center 6. Note only 54 people responded to this section - out of a
    neighborhood with more than 1000 houses. We need more input from our community!
    The City has also done an evaluation of the current site conditions. For those details, see Page 76 of this
    document: https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/3216/637879683037530000
    The City is investing substantial time and energy in this project, and LVNA wants to make sure that our
    neighborhood input is included. LVNA will be looking to bring someone from the City to a neighborhood
    meeting in 2023 to talk more about the City’s plan. We would also welcome volunteers from the community
    to help make sure that our voices are heard and incorporated into the plans. Please reach out to
    board@lvnasv.org if you are interested in helping - and keep an eye on future Villagers and on NextDoor for
    more details about a neighborhood meeting.
    For more information, see
    https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov/business-and-development/projects-in-sunnyvale/long-range-planning-initiative
    s/village-center-master-plan
