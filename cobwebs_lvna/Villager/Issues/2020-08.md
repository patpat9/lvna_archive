    Due to the success of this initiative, we plan to continue collecting recyclables to help raise money for LVNA 
    events (once we’re able to bring those back).
    How You Can Help
    You and your family can start by saving eligible recyclables. Once you’re ready for someone to collect them, 
    schedule a pick-up by emailing us at recycle@lvnasv.org and one of our volunteers will coordinate a time to 
    swing by and collect them. That’s it!
    Suggested Tips
    •	
    Please make sure all items are clean, empty, and CRV eligible. You should see one of the following 
    printed on the label: California Redemption Value, CA Redemption Value, California Cash Refund, CA 
    CRV, or CA Cash Refund
    •	
    Before containers can be turned in at the redemption center, all items must be properly sorted. While 
    pre-sorting isn’t required, our volunteers greatly appreciate the extra help.
    •	
    Glass separation by color: Green, Brown, and Clear
    •	
    Plastic separation by color: Green, White, and Clear
    •	
    Aluminum cans – no additional sorting required
    
    RECIPE CORNER
    Caramelized Onions
    02     LVNA VILLAGER | AUGUST 2020
    1.	 In a large skillet over medium heat, melt butter.
    2.	 Add sliced onions into skillet. Reduce the heat to medium 
    low and stir the onions every 3-5 minutes, allowing them to 
    brown a little on the bottom in-between each stir.
    3.	 If onions start sticking to the pan, add 2-3 tablespoons of 
    water to deglaze the pan while scraping all the flavor off 
    the bottom of the pan. 
    4.	 Repeat steps 2 & 3 until the onions have cooked for at 
    least 45 minutes to an hour (or until the onions reached 
    your desired color).
    5.	 Enjoy!
    Caramelized onions are a great addition to many dishes like 
    sandwiches and casseroles. An upcoming Villager recipe may 
    even include caramelized onions!
    2-3 large onions, sliced (yellow onions were used here)
    2 tbsp butter, salted or unsalted or oil
