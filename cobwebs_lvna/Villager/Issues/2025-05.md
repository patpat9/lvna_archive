    2025 LVNA Board Election Results
    The votes are in, and we’re thrilled to share the results of the 2025 LVNA Board Elections! Our community has
    spoken, and we’re ready to welcome fresh faces to keep Lakewood Village thriving.
    A big thank you to Tony Bugna, our outgoing 2nd Vice President, for his fantastic contributions to the board.
    Tony’s not stepping away entirely—he’ll continue to strengthen our neighborhood by forging ties with local
    businesses. We’re grateful for his ongoing support!
    Say hello to Erica Cramer, our new 1st Vice President! Erica’s ready to jump in and help steer our community
    toward even brighter days.
    We’re also excited to welcome Evelyn Profeta, a Lakewood Village resident since the early 1980s, as our new
    social media lead. Evelyn’s passion for bringing neighbors together is contagious, and she’s eager to connect
    with you online. Got ideas for our community? Join the conversation on our LVNA Facebook group:
    https://www.facebook.com/groups/lvnasv.
    Want to make a difference? We’re now accepting applications for the open 2nd Vice President position. If
    you’re passionate about our neighborhood, email board@lvnasv.org to apply. Let’s keep the momentum going!
    Nathan Chan
    Memorial Day Scavenger Hunt
    Let’s celebrate Memorial Day with a fun Scavenger Hunt throughout Lakewood Village! From May 1 to May 31,
    homes in our community across the Lakewood and Fairwood side will display an American flag near sidewalks,
    each one easily visible for you to spot. The winner will be the person who finds the most flags, so get ready to
    explore!
    How to Play:
    Locate as many flags around the neighborhood as you can.
    Do NOT take the flags! Leave them for others to find.
    All residents (including prior winners) are eligible to win.
    Email board@lvnasv.org with the address (e.g., 123 Main St) of
    each flag. Send your email after you’ve found as many flags
    as you can!
    A map with hints and the winner announcement will be
    published on Nextdoor, Facebook and our website. Find the
    links at https://linktr.ee/lvnasv.
    Entries are due by May 31, 2025, at 11:59 p.m.
    The first 5 people to submit pictures of 8 or more flags win
    a $20 gift card!
    Good luck, neighbors! Happy hunting, and let’s honor Memorial
    Day together!

    Calabazas Trail Shines Thanks to Sunnyvale Parks Team
    We extend our heartfelt thanks to Supervisor Jonathan Howard and the City of Sunnyvale Parks
    Team for their commitment to keeping the Calabazas Trail, bordering our neighborhood, clean and
    beautiful. We’ve noticed the prompt removal of dumped items, reflecting your team’s diligence. Each
    time we’ve emailed jhoward@sunnyvale.ca.gov with photos and specific location details, your quick
    response and action have been impressive. The team also ensures the newly placed trash bins along
    the trail are regularly emptied. As the weather warms up, strolling the trail is a delight, thanks to your
    team’s outstanding efforts.
    Spot Dumped Items on Calabazas Trail? – Report them to Jonathan Howard at
    jhoward@sunnyvale.ca.gov and help keep our trail pristine!
    SSD Board of Education Vacancy Filled by Evelyn Profeta
    We’re excited to forward the news that the Sunnyvale School District (SSD) Board of Education has
    filled its recent vacancy with a familiar face—Evelyn Profeta! Evelyn is no stranger to leadership in
    our neighborhood. She also joined the LVNA Board as the social media manager, where she’s already
    working to connect neighbors through platforms like our LVNA Facebook group
    (https://www.facebook.com/groups/lvnasv). Her experience on the SSD Board will further amplify her
    impact, as she advocates for our local schools and students.
    With a deep commitment to fostering collaboration and growth, Evelyn is eager to contribute to SSD’s
    mission of providing a high-quality education for all. Have ideas for our schools or community? Reach
    out to Evelyn via the LVNA Facebook group and let your voice be heard!

    Dumpster Day is Coming – Volunteers Needed!
    Mark your calendars—Dumpster Day is set for Saturday, July 19! This is your chance to clear out
    clutter and help keep Lakewood Village tidy, all while connecting with neighbors. We’ll be setting up
    at multiple locations across the neighborhood (details to come) for a day of community cleanup.
    We need volunteers to make this event a success! Whether you can help with setup, manage drop-
    offs, or assist with cleanup, your support will go a long way. No experience needed—just bring your
    enthusiasm for our neighborhood. Ready to pitch in? Contact our Treasurer, Linh Ly, at
    treasurer@lvnasv.org to sign up or learn more. Let’s make Dumpster Day a win for Lakewood Village!
    Goodwill Truck Drive at Fairwood School
    Time for some spring cleaning! Join us for a Goodwill Truck Drive on Saturday, May 31, from 9 a.m. to
    1 p.m. at Fairwood School. It’s the perfect chance to declutter your home and donate gently used
    items to a great cause. Drop off clothes, household goods, and more—your donations will support
    Goodwill’s mission to improve the quality of life for individuals and families. Clear out your space
    and make a difference in one go!
    Calabazas Creek Clean Up- Volunteers Needed!
    Get ready to roll up your sleeves and make a difference at the Calabazas Creek Cleanup, hosted by
    LVNA! On Saturday, May 17, from 9 a.m. to noon, join your neighbors as part of National River
    Cleanup Day 2025. This fun, hands-on event is all about sprucing up the creek and keeping our local
    waterways thriving. Backed by Valley Water, this cleanup promises a morning of community spirit and
    eco-action. For questions, email volunteer@valleywater.org or call (408) 630-2333.
    The association is calling for volunteers—no experience needed, just a willingness to pitch in! Cleanup
    supplies will be provided onsite, as well as trash pickup service to haul away collected trash. Want to
    join the crew? Sign up here: https://tinyurl.com/calabazas-cleanup-2025. Let’s make Calabazas
    Creek shine!

    We’re looking for volunteers to step up and make a difference.

    If organizing and securing resources is your strength, join our Procurement team.

    Contact board@lvnasv.org today to volunteer. Lakewood Village is OUR neighborhood—let’s keep it thriving
    together!
    Issues in the Village
    Help Fix Lakewood Village! Report Issues Today
    The LVNA board is working to compile a list of the most neglected areas in Lakewood Village, and we need
    your help! Our goal is to identify the Top 10 Worst Spots in the neighborhood and share them with the City of
    Sunnyvale to push for much-needed repairs and improvements.
    What Issues Should You Report?
    Look around Lakewood Village and let us know about areas that need attention, including but not limited to:
    Sidewalks that are cracked or unsafe
    Potholes that need filling
    Overgrown trees in need of trimming
    Abandoned vehicles that are taking up space
    Neglected properties that impact our community’s appearance
    How to Submit a Report
    The more reports we get, the stronger our case! You can submit issues in two easy ways:
    1.Email us with specific details (exact addresses, descriptions, and photos if possible) at board@lvnasv.org.
    2.Report directly to the City via Access Sunnyvale (https://sunnyvale.dynamics365portals.us). This officially
    enters your complaint into the system. If you do this, let us know so we can help follow up!
    Together, we can make Lakewood Village a safer, cleaner, and better-maintained community. Thank you for
    taking the time to report issues and advocate for our neighborhood!
