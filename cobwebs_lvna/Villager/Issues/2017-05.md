    Tasman Corridor Project by Zedory
    ln April, the LVNA board cancelled it's regularly
    scheduled monthly meeting, and instead attended the
    Valley Transit Authority's (WA) Complete Streets Study.
    Members of the LVNA board attended, as well as many
    residents of Lakewood Village, and surrounding neigh-
    borhoods.
    The purpose of the meeting was to provide infor-
    mation, and conduct a community survey on the current
    usage, and future plans for improvements to the Tas-
    man Drive corridor. lmprovements proposed include in-
    creasing pedestrian, bicycle and motor vehicle safety
    along the corridor, with consideration for the expected
    growth and development of the area over the next 20
    years.
    Read more about the project online at
    http://mvlv.vta. org/projects-and-programs/complete-
    streets/tasman-study.
