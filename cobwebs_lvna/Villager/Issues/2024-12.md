    Villager
    VOL. 67 EDITION 12  •  DECEMBER 2024
    Can you believe it’s already that time of year again? The Lakewood Village holiday lighting competition is back!
    Please email board@lvnasv.org to enter your house or to nominate a neighbor's house in the competition; so, get
    those decorations ready! Lights and decorations must be up by 6:00pm on Saturday, December 14, for
    consideration by the judging panel.
    We have an all-star set of judges this year. There will be four winners total (one winner per quadrant). The
    quadrants are divided into two on the Lakewood side, split into North and South (split along Lakehaven) and two
    on the Fairwood side, split into North and South (split along Sandia). 
    Each winning home will receive a $25 gift card and coveted bragging rights for the best Lakewood Village light
    displays of 2024. To ensure fairness, last year’s winners will be ineligible for this year’s prizes, but are highly
    encouraged to continue raising the bar on the Lakewood Village’s festiveness (and last year’s winners are still
    eligible for honorable mentions).
