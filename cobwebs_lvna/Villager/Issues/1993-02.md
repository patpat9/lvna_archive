    Announcements
    
    Recently there has been a reappearance of graffiti here in Lake-
    wood. Residents are encouraged to paint over graffiti as soon as
    it appears. Also, report any leads on suspects to Public Safety at
    730-7100 or the Neighborhood Resource Officer at 730-7146.
    
    Nominations for the L.V.N.A. board will be held at the Febru-
    ary meeting. The offices of president, vice president, secretary,
    and treasurer are all opening.
    
    The Lakewood Village Brownie and Girl Scout troops are finish-
    ing their cookie sales . Saturday, February 20, is your last
    chance to buy these famous cookies. The girls will be going
    door-to-door in small groups. Cookies are $3 a box.
    
    A local not-for-profit agency is collecting used and broken
    computers which they repair and donate to neighborhood
    schools. If you or your company is discarding IBM-compatible
    or Apple computers, call 734-5030 to make a tax-deductible con-
    tribution instead. Volunteers are also needed to spend an evening
    testing, repairing, and rebuilding the donated computers.
    
    In spite of massive budget cuts across the city, the plans for im-
    proving Fairwood Park survived the Sunnyvale City Council's
    budget meeting without a scratch.
    
    It's time to come out and help clean up the lot at Blazingwood
    and Sandia. Call Vicki at 730-8274 or Charline at 732-6504 to
    volunteer.
    
    City of Sunnyvale S.N.A.P. Program
    
    Once again, the Villager asks, "Is Lakewood prepared for
    the next major disaster?" Sadly, the City of Sunnyvale feels that
    we could be much better prepared.
    
    Of the 1600 homes in Lakewood, only about 300 are cur-
    rently involved in Sunnyvale Neighborhoods Actively Prepare
    (S.N.A.P.). Residents who have networked as part of a S.N.A.P.
    group are much better prepared for a disaster. They know who to
    turn to for help, and they have a network to support their needs
    for the first three days after a major disaster.
    
    The Lakewood area is greatly in need of more volunteers to
    be S.N.A.P. captains and committee members. The Department
    of Public Safety will help you set up an organizational meeting
    of 40-50 neighbors, and will gladly send a representative to the
    meeting to explain the S.N.A.P. program and give a presentation
    on emergency preparedness.
    
    To get involved, call 730-7190. | |

    Reminders
    
    The L.V.N.A. wants to remind everyone that a little effort goes a
    long way toward keeping the neighborhood looking its best.
    
    Thank goodness for all the rain we've been having this win-
    ter. But as spring approaches, let's get out our lawnmowers and
    make sure our neighborhood continues to look great.
    
    Don't forget the city strips when mowing. Perhaps you can
    offer to help someone without a mower—your teenager can earn
    some extra spending money mowing lawns.
    
    Put away trash cans after pick up and clean up debris. You'd
    be surprised how nice the neighborhood can look with a little ef-
    fort.
    
    Let's TAKE PRIDE in our community and our neighbor-
    hood. Let's teach our young through our own actions, so every-
    one can have a neighborhood to be proud of. |
    
    Newsletter Contributions
    
    All information in the Villager is contributed by residents of
    Lakewood. If you have an article, announcement, or request for
    information which you would like published, contact newsletter
    editor Linda Cheung at 734-1661.
    
    The deadline for most months' newsletters is the first day of
    the month. Distribution is on the weekend before the third Thurs-
    day of the month. We will attempt to to print all submissions as
    space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any
    questions or concerns about the newsletter.
    
    Parks & Recreation
    
    The Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Department offers many ser-
    vices and classes at the Sunnyvale Community Center at 550 E.
    Remington Drive. But they also offer several programs for
    young people right here in Lakewood. For general information
    on any Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Program, call 730-7530.
    
    Toddler Time
    A new preschool program for 3-5 year olds. The pro-
    gram will emphasize socialization and play by introduc-
    ing children to arts and crafts, music movement, sing-
    alongs, story-telling, indoor and outdoor play, and much
    more. The program is set up on a cooperative basis.
    Parents must sign up to assist the program specialist
    for a minimum of one class per registered child.
    
    Toddler Time will be held on Thursdays, March 4—April
    15 from 9:30-11:30 A.M. at the Lakewood Park Center.
    $25 per child, $20 for each additional child in the same
    family. Children must be 3 years of age and toilet
    trained. For information and registration, call 730-7515.
