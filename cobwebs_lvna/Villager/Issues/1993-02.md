Program Once again, the Villager asks, "Is Lakewood prepared for the next major disaster?" Sadly, the City of Sunnyvale feels that we could be much better prepared. Of the 1600 homes in Lakewood, only about 300 are cur- rently involved in Sunnyvale Neighborhoods Actively Prepare (S.N.A.P,). Residents who have networked as part of a S.N.A.P. group are much better prepared for a disaster. They know Who to turn to for help, and they have a network to support their needs for the first three days after a major disaster. The Lakewood area is greatly in need of more volunteers to be S.N.A.P. captains and committee members. The Department of Public Safety will help you set up an organizational meeting of 40—50 neighbors, and will gladly send a representative to the meeting to explain the S.N.A.P. program and give a presentation on emergency preparedness. Parks & Recreation The Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Department offers many ser- vices and classes at the Sunnyvale Community Center at 550 E. Remington Drive. But they also offer several programs for young people right here in Lakewood. For general information on any Sunnyvale Parks & Recreation Program, call 730-7530. Toddler Time A new preschool program for 3—5 year olds. The pro- gram will emphasize socialization and play by introduc- ing children to arts and crafts, music movement, sing- alongs, story-telling, indoor and outdoor play, and much more. The program is set up on a cooperative basis. Parents must sign up to assist the program specialist for a minimum of one class per registered child. Toddler Time will be held on Thursdays, March 4—April 15 from 9:30—11:30 AM. at the Lakewood Park Center. $25 per child, $20 for each additional child in the same family. Children must be 3 years of age and toilet trained. For information and registration, call 730-7515.
