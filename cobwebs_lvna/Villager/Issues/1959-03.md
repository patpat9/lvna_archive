## In This Issue

- **Page 1:**
    - **City Officials Visit LVRA:** Sunnyvale civic leaders Spiers and Smith speak at the monthly membership meeting on municipal infrastructure.
- **Pages 2-4:**
    - **The Green Thumb:** Wayne Shoemaker shares spring lawn restoration, weed control, and fertilization techniques.
    - **Open Letter to Lakewood Residents:** Candid assessment of Sunnyvale municipal administration and neighborhood tax representation.
    - **Local Merchant:** C&H Paint Center on El Camino Real.
- **Pages 5-8:**
    - **Local Commerce & Services:** Mac's Hardware tools and Shopsmith sales; sales representative opportunities.
    - **LVRA Officers Directory:** Bill Burgess (Chairman), Ted Glasnow (Vice Chairman), Bev Howerton (Secretary); classified ads and babysitting network.
