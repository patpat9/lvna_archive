    semi-finalist in an energy efficiency 
    competition for a prize of $5 million. The City 
    is proud to currently be in 16th place out of 
    50! To help Sunnyvale bring home $5 million 
    and also reduce your utility bills, here are some tips to 
    help save energy in your home: 
     
    Replace any older light bulbs with energy efficient 
    CFL or LED bulbs. 
     
    Find and fix any water leaks - saving water saves 
    energy.

    Beware & Watch Your Mailboxes 
     
    By Phyllis Fowler 
     
    I saw this alert from Dorothy Castro Lake-
    wood’s Nextdoor,com: 
     
    “We live on Lakewood Drive between the 
    church & the end by Hiddenlake. My husband 
    was in our garage; he opened the garage 
    door & saw a strange blond, well dressed 
    woman in our neighbor across the street yard, partly   
    hidden behind a bush. She was startled when the garage 
    door opened. She took off walking fast, crossed the 
    street turned right onto Hiddenlake & disappeared. My 
    husband went over to check the neighbor’s yard since 
    they were not home. He found that behind the bush was 
    their mailbox. Lucky the mailman had not run yet.  
     
    So PLEASE BEWARE & WATCH YOUR MAILBOX 
    ALONG WITH YOUR NEIGHBORS. This was between 
    10 & 11 am.” 
     
    This is one of the ways your identity can be stolen. 
    Thieves take your bank statement, credit card statements 
    or any other mail that may have information about you or 
    your household.  
     
    How can I better protect my in-coming mail? 
    Install an effective locking mailbox and USE THE LOCK. 
    Or else install the mail slots so that your mail goes into 
    your garage. You can be purchase these at Lowe’s, 
    Home Depot or OSH. 
     
     
    Watch your mailbox for deliveries   
    (when possible have them delivered to your 
    work or when you will be home or if you are 
    ordering from Amazon have it sent to the 
    Amazon Locker. The nearest is the 7-11 on 
    Fair Oaks). 
     
    Pick up your mail as soon as possible 
    from your personal mailbox. Never let it 
    overflow. 
     
    If you are away, have a neighbor pick up and 
    safely store each day’s mail deliveries or have the US 
    Post Office hold your mail for you. 
     
    Consider electronic banking and direct deposits 
    to minimize the amount of financial data coming into 
    and going out from your mail box. 
     
    Run a credit check regularly. 
     
    Rent a PO Box from US Post Office or a private      
    company (UPS store, Postal Annex etc.) 
     
    Always review your credit card and bank state-
    ments closely and promptly. I recently balanced a 
    bank statement and there was a check my boss had 
    written in 2014. The check had been paid twice, once 
    in 2014, and again last month! This is the second 
    time it has happened in 3 months.  
     
    No one is going to protect your identity better than 
    you!  

    Weapons Violation – 900 Blk. E. Duane 
    Monday March 7th @:1:36am 
    Officers responded to a nightclub on a reported domestic 
    dispute.  Officers contacted the one of the involved parties and 
    determined he had a loaded handgun in his vehicle.  The 
    suspect was arrested for being a felon in possession of a 
    loaded firearm, in addition to other charges, and was booked 
    into County Jail. 
     
    Aggravated Assault – 700 Blk. S. Fair Oaks 
    Sunday March 20th @ 1:21am 
    Officers responded on a report of a physical fight at a liquor 
    store.  All involved parties were gone on officer arrival.  A 
    couple of hours later, the victim called 911 for a medical 
    emergency and it was determined he had been stabbed 
    multiple times during the altercation at the liquor store.  The 
    victim was transported to the hospital for emergency surgery 
    and was expected to survive. 
     
    Aggravated Assault – 300 Blk. Hiddenlake 
    Saturday March 12th @ 9:33pm 
    Officers responded on a domestic dispute in which the suspect 
    pointed a rifle at his mother and step-father and threatened 
    them.  The mother and step-father fled the house and were 
    uninjured.  Officers arrived and located the suspect in the 
    vicinity of the house.  The suspect was taken into custody and 
    booked into County Jail.  The rifle was recovered in the 
    residence.  
     
    Auto Accident – Morse over Central Expressway 
    Thursday March 10th @ 11:26pm 
    DPS personnel responded on a report of a collision on Central 
    Expressway.  Upon arrival it was determined the vehicle was 
    traveling on Morse over Central Expressway, lost control, and 
    drove through the guardrails.  The vehicle landed on its roof 
    on Central Expressway.  The vehicle did not strike any other 
    vehicles and the driver self-extricated from the vehicle with 
    only minor injuries. 
     
    Arson – 1085 Tasman 
    Monday March 25th @ 4:38pm 
    DPS personnel responded on a report of a fence fire that was 
    endangering structures.  The fire was extinguished before it 
    spread to any residences and the investigation identified two 
    juvenile suspects who were arrested.  
     
    Explosion – 1100 Blk. Sunnyvale – Saratoga 
    Wednesday March 23rd @ 6:18pm 
    DPS received numerous calls regarding an explosion in the 
    area.  Upon arrival it was determined to be from an 
    underground PG&E electrical vault.  Nobody was injured in the 
    explosion but approximately 10,000 residents lost power.
