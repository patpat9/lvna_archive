    Thursday December 11 @ 10:36 am

    the area. lt was determined that the PG&E vault under the
    sidewalk flooded a created three explosions which sent
    flames shooting out of the vault. Nobody was injured in the
    incident and PG&E responded to stabilize the situation.

    Saturday December 13 @ 5:25 am

    was physically assaulted by four unknown subjects.
    During the assault, the suspects demanded property from
    the victim, but were unsuccessful in taking anything.
