    Earth Day Celebrations
    Join the Fun at Sunnyvale’s Earth Day Festival 2025!
    Get ready for Sunnyvale’s Earth Day Festival 2025 on Saturday, April 5, from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at the
    Sunnyvale Civic Center, 456 W. Olive Ave. This lively event brings sustainability to life with cool activities:
    think eco-friendly crafts, guided tree tours, and community booths packed with green tips. Enjoy live music,
    grab a bite from local food trucks, and park your bike for free at the valet. It’s all part of Sunnyvale’s push for
    a cleaner future through its Climate Action Playbook. 
    For more info, check out https://sunnyvaleclimateaction.org/earth-day-festival-2025. Come join the fun and
    make a difference!
    Calabazas Creek Clean Up
    Volunteers Needed for Calabazas Creek Cleanup!
    Get ready to roll up your sleeves and make a difference at the Calabazas Creek Cleanup, hosted by LVNA!
    On Saturday, May 17, from 9 a.m. to noon, join your neighbors as part of National River Cleanup Day 2025.
    This fun, hands-on event is all about sprucing up the creek and keeping our local waterways thriving. Backed
    by Valley Water, this cleanup promises a morning of community spirit and eco-action. For questions, email
    volunteer@valleywater.org or call (408) 630-2333.
    The association is calling for volunteers—no experience needed, just a willingness to pitch in! Cleanup
    supplies will be provided onsite, as well as trash pickup service to haul away collected trash. Want to join the
    crew? Sign up here: https://tinyurl.com/calabazas-cleanup-2025. Let’s make Calabazas Creek shine!
    Donate to LVNA
    Donate to LVNA: Your Support Keeps Our Events Alive!
    Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association (LVNA) is now a 501(c)(3)
    non-profit organization. This milestone means your donations are tax-
    deductible, and they’re more important than ever!
    Community favorites like the Summer Picnic, block parties, Dumpster
    Day, the Holiday Parade and Lights Contest wouldn’t be possible
    without the support of generous neighbors like you. These cherished
    traditions depend on community donations to continue. Help us keep
    them going and create new opportunities for Lakewood Village!
    How to donate
    Donate online: Send your donation to treasurer@lvnasv.org via
    Zelle, or simply scan the QR code with your banking app.
    Mail a check: Make checks payable to "Lakewood Village
    Neighborhood Association" and mail them to: C/O Linh Ly, 1102
    Pecos Way, Sunnyvale, CA 94089
     Every dollar counts toward building a stronger, more vibrant

    We Need Volunteers
    We Need Volunteers – Lakewood Village Needs YOU!
    Our neighborhood thrives because of people like you, and right now, we need your help to keep it strong!
    We’re looking for volunteers to step up and make a difference.
    Are you skilled with websites or social media? We need your expertise to keep our community informed. 
    Do you enjoy photography? Help us capture the heart of our events.
    If organizing and securing resources is your strength, join our Procurement team. 
    And if you’re a parent of Lakewood School students, be our liaison to build a stronger bridge between our
    community and education.
    We also urgently need neighbors to host block parties—these events are the backbone of our community spirit!
    Don’t wait for someone else to step up; now is your chance to make a meaningful impact. Whether you have an
    hour a week or just want to pitch in for an event, there’s a role for you. 
    Contact board@lvnasv.org today to volunteer. Lakewood Village is OUR neighborhood—let’s keep it thriving
    together!
    Issues in the Village
    Help Fix Lakewood Village! Report Issues Today
    The LVNA board is working to compile a list of the most neglected areas in Lakewood Village, and we need
    your help! Our goal is to identify the Top 10 Worst Spots in the neighborhood and share them with the City of
    Sunnyvale to push for much-needed repairs and improvements.
    What Issues Should You Report?
    Look around Lakewood Village and let us know about areas that need attention, including but not limited to:
    Sidewalks that are cracked or unsafe
    Potholes that need filling
    Overgrown trees in need of trimming
    Abandoned vehicles that are taking up space
    Neglected properties that impact our community’s appearance
    How to Submit a Report
    The more reports we get, the stronger our case! You can submit issues in two easy ways:
    Email us with specific details (exact addresses, descriptions, and photos if possible) at board@lvnasv.org.
    1.
    Report directly to the City via Access Sunnyvale (https://sunnyvale.dynamics365portals.us). This officially
    enters your complaint into the system. If you do this, let us know so we can help follow up!
    2.
    Together, we can make Lakewood Village a safer, cleaner, and better-maintained community. Thank you for
    taking the time to report issues and advocate for our neighborhood!

    LVNA Board Elections
    Are you passionate (or even just interested in) our neighborhood? Do you enjoy meeting new
    neighbors? Would you be willing to help build a tight-knit (Lakewood) Village for yourself, your friends,
    and the next generation who’s currently growing up right outside your door or even in your own
    backyard?
    Please join the LVNA board! There are five board positions: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice
    President, Treasurer, and Secretary. The current board’s positions end in April and a new board will
    begin. The board meets once per month to discuss ongoing activities, in addition to holding quarterly
    neighborhood general meetings and a few seasonal events each year. The level of commitment is up
    to each individual - these are all volunteer roles. It is a one-year term (April 2025 - March 2026). The
    only requirements are that you are 18+ years old, live in the neighborhood, and are able to serve the
    full 12 month term.
    Elections are April 9 at 7:00pm in the Lakewood Park Building. For more information, please email
    board@lvnasv.org.
    Extra Dumping Weekends
    Sunnyvale residents can dispose of extra garbage for free during two special weekends in April:
    Saturday, April 5 & Sunday, April 6
    Saturday, April 12 & Sunday, April 13
    Head to the Sunnyvale SMaRT Station® at 301 Carl Rd. between 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. Bring
    proof of residency, as this is for Sunnyvale residents only. All loads must be covered (or pay $15 for a
    tarp), with vehicles limited to one-ton capacity and trailers no larger than 5 feet by 10 feet. Don’t miss
    out!
