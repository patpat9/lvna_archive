## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **Traffic Safety Drive Launched:** Petition drive demanding State and City traffic signals and crosswalk improvements on Lawrence Station Road and Bayshore Highway.
    - **Sunnyvale Foreign Car Club:** Car enthusiast club meetings; Mac's Hardware promotions.
    - **Civil Defense Survival Training:** Basic family disaster preparedness courses organized in San Jose and Sunnyvale.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Membership Application:** Association drive to enroll all 1,600 tract households.
    - **School Safety Patrols:** Letters urging safety patrols at major intersections.
    - **Lakewood Park Model Homes:** Conventional and GI financing for remaining subdivision lots.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Religious Instructions:** Schedules for Catholic, Baptist, and Methodist Sunday schools.
    - **LVRA Officers Directory:** Executive board and unit coordinators.
