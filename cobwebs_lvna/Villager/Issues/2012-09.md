    You can also pick up printed maps at City Hall, the Library, 
    Columbia Neighborhood, Senior and Community Centers 
    on September 21 after 10 a.m.  
     
    Pre-plan your route according to items on your list to save 
    fuel, reduce your carbon footprint and make 
    your shopping adventure successful!  
     
    Here are a few suggestions from some garage 
    sale aficionados:  
     
    • Have your list of items and route planned.  
    • Take blankets, rope, bags, boxes and 
    newspaper to wrap or tie down and protect 
    your new treasures in transport.  
    • Take plenty of water in a reusable water bottle 
    to stay hydrated and pack some snacks to keep you 
    energized while you shop.  
    • Carry plenty of small bills and change.  
    • If you see something you like, pick it up right away – or 
    someone else will! 
    • Group your purchases; you can often get discounts when 
    buying more items.  
    • If there’s something you like but think it’s too expensive, 
    leave your contact information with 
    the seller. If it’s still available after the 
    sale, they may take your offered price 
    anyway.  
     
     
     Savvy Sellers Garage Sale Tips  
    Cleaning up clutter and clearing out 
    cupboards, drawers and closets is 
    the most time-consuming task when 
    preparing for a garage sale. It’s 
    helpful to use two boxes or bags: 
    one for sale or donation items, the 
    other for trash. A good rule of thumb 
    is, if you haven’t used or needed it in a year, you can 
    probably live without it.  
    Here are some tips on making your sale a great success, 
    putting some money in your pocket and helping the 
    environment by keeping usable items out of the landfill:  
    • Make sure all items are clean and in good repair.  
    • Display small items at eye level, not the ground.  
    • Keep pets away from shoppers.  
    • Display items should have price tags on top for 
    easy viewing.  
    • Group items by category such as clothes, 
    kitchenware, books and toys to make shopping 
    easier.  
    • Put books in boxes where titles can be easily 
    read.  
    • Have a “Free” box of things that may be missing 
    parts, or need repair.  
    • Have plenty of change and $1 bills.  
    • Put showcase items out front to attract shoppers.  
    • Provide day care for your young children so you can focus 
    on your sale.  
    • Display a sign stating, “All Sales Final”, to avoid returns.  
    • Consider having baked goods for customers to welcome 
    them to your sale!  
     
    Seller Safeguards:  
    • Don’t allow shoppers to use your bathroom, direct them to 
    the closest public facilities.  
    • Don’t leave your money unattended! 
    • Eliminate any tripping or falling hazards to shoppers. 

    Downtown Construction Updates 
     
    Apple has moved into the new 156,000 
    square-foot Town Center Office building at 
    the corner of Mathilda and McKinley 
    avenues. Apple expects to have about 400 
    employees there. The increase in daytime 
    population will boost business for our 
    downtown restaurants and shops. 
     
    Downtown development projects continue. There are 
    several active projects as well as upcoming projects. This 
    includes activity from private property owners to street 
    improvement projects by the City of Sunnyvale. Two of the 
    current projects are Solstice and Carmel Lofts. If you get a 
    chance to visit the Farmer’s Market on Saturday’s, you 
    can check out the progress! The new buildings where the 
    Town and Country shopping area used to be are going up 
    fast! 
     
    Solstice 
     
    BRE Properties, Inc. continues 
    construction on a mixed use project with 
    280 residential units and approximately 
    34,000 square feet of ground floor retail. 
    The project area is at West Washington 
    Ave and Frances Ave. Underground utility work will 
    continue on streets surrounding the project. This may 
    require temporary lane and street closures throughout the 
    process. 
    If you have any questions, please contact: Robert Dauth, 
    Project Manager for Douglas Ross Construction, Inc.925-
    989-5708 
    robertd@rossconstruction.com 
     
    Carmel Lofts 
     
    Carmel Lofts is a mixed use development that will be 
    adjacent to the Plaza del Sol off of Frances Avenue and 
    Olson Way. It will consist of two buildings comprised of up 
    to four stories of residential with 133 apartment units and 
    8,000 square feet of ground floor retail space.
