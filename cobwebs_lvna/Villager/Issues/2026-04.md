    Hi neighbor, do you love the community spirit of Lakewood Village? Have you ever thought about getting
    involved but worried about the time commitment? We are looking for passionate neighbors to fill crucial, yet
    flexible, volunteer roles on the LVNA Board. You don't need a huge chunk of time—just a willingness to help
    our community. The commitment is light, but the fun and positive impact are massive!
    What does it involve?
    A quick monthly board meeting to keep things running smoothly.
    Helping organize a handful of beloved neighborhood events (like our summer picnic and holiday parade).
    That’s it! You have the flexibility to contribute your skills to a one-year term (April 2026 – March 2027) in one
    of these five volunteer roles: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President, Treasurer, or Secretary.
    Special Guest & Project Updates
    Beyond the elections, this is a meeting you won't want to miss! We are thrilled to welcome Eileen Le, who will
    discuss several exciting projects under consideration for Sunnyvale. We will also receive vital progress
    updates on the new Library and the Lakewood Park revamp. Come get the inside scoop on how our corner
    of the city is evolving.
    Eileen will also review Sunnyvale City Council priority projects so that residents can give their feedback:
    Retail, Sidewalk maintenance, Funding for Nonprofits, Age Friendly, Accessibility and Teen Staff Advisory
    Committees, Revitalizing Safe Routes to School Program, Public Sector Workforce Housing Feasibility Study,
    Non-residential sign codes and compliance with the First Amendment, Creation of a Food Co-op, State-
    sponsored Market, and Streamlining the Permit Process.
    Join Us!
    The only requirements to run for the board are that you must be 18 years or older, live in our neighborhood,
    and be able to serve the full 12 months.
    When: Wednesday, April 8, 2026 at 7:00 PM
    Where: Lakewood Park Building
    Questions? Email us at board@lvnasv.org
    We look forward to welcoming your energy and community spirit!

    Village Vistas: Blooms & Birds
    A Sign of Spring in the Village
    If you’ve taken a walk through the neighborhood
    lately, you’ve likely been stopped in your tracks by a
    burst of giant, goblet-shaped flowers. That stunning
    display belongs to the Saucer Magnolia. Often
    called "tulip trees" by neighbors, these local favorites
    are one of the earliest signs that spring has arrived in
    Lakewood.
    Cool Factor: Magnolias are ancient! They evolved
    before bees even existed, so they are biologically
    designed to be pollinated by beetles.
    Neighbor Spotlight: Mark’s Backyard Feeder
    It’s not just the plants waking up; our local bird population is busy
    too! Our neighbor Mark shared some wonderful "action shots" from
    his bird feeder last month.
    Dark-eyed Junco: Easily spotted by its crisp black "hood" and white
    belly.
    Cool Factor: They are known as "Snowbirds" because their arrival
    often signals the start of colder weather (or in our case, the peak
    of our "California Winter").
    Mourning Dove: A gentle presence in the Village, known for its soft
    cooing and plump feathers.
    Cool Factor: These birds can fly up to 55 miles per hour! They
    may look sleepy on a fence, but they are incredibly fast fliers.
    White-crowned Sparrow: Showing off its distinctive black-and-white
    striped head.
    Cool Factor: Young sparrows learn their songs from the
    "neighborhood" they grow up in, meaning our Lakewood sparrows
    may have a local "dialect" different from birds in other areas!
    Editor’s Note: Please keep in mind your newsletter editor is an
    enthusiast, not an expert! If I’ve misidentified a feathered friend or a
    backyard bloom, please reach out and let me know. I’m always happy
    to learn more about our local ecosystem from our resident experts.
    Spotted something interesting? Send your photos to
    editor@lvnasv.org to be featured!

    The LVNA board is working to compile a list of the most neglected areas in Lakewood Village, and we need
    your help! Our goal is to identify the Top 10 Worst Spots in the neighborhood and share them with the City of
    Sunnyvale to push for much-needed repairs and improvements.
    What Issues Should You Report?
    Look around Lakewood Village and let us know about areas that need attention, including but not limited to:
    Sidewalks that are cracked or unsafe
    Potholes that need filling
    Overgrown trees in need of trimming
    Abandoned vehicles that are taking up space
    Neglected properties that impact our community’s appearance
    How to Submit a Report
    The more reports we get, the stronger our case! You can submit issues in two easy ways:
    1. Email us with specific details (exact addresses, descriptions, and photos if possible) at board@lvnasv.org.
    2. Report directly to the City via Access Sunnyvale (https://sunnyvale.dynamics365portals.us). This officially
    enters your complaint into the system. If you do this, let us know so we can help follow up!
    3. Attend a City Hall meeting on a Tuesday to voice your concerns in person—this can make an even bigger
    impact and catch their attention. If you choose this option, also file a claim on Access Sunnyvale to
    strengthen your case. More information about the meeting: https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov/your-
    government/governance/city-council/council-meeting-information
    Together, we can make Lakewood Village a safer, cleaner, and better-maintained community. Thank you for
    taking the time to report issues and advocate for our neighborhood!
