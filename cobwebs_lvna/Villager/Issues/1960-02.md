## In This Issue

- **Pages 1-3:**
    - **1960 Board Nominations:** Call for candidates to lead the association and advocate for park amenities.
    - **Mac's Hardware:** Garden mowers and tools.
    - **Contractor Negotiations:** Review of discussions with subdivision builders regarding tract maintenance.
- **Pages 4-6:**
    - **Boy Scout Troop 425 Celebrates Golden Anniversary:** Founding history of Lakewood Village's Boy Scout troop.
    - **Troop 425 Wilderness Outing:** Scouts recount their first overnight wilderness campout.
    - **Local Services:** Jack Putnam tax service on Sandia Avenue; Goodwill clothing drive awards.
- **Pages 7-8:**
    - **Religious Directory:** Schedules for Lakewood Baptist Church and neighborhood Catholic classes.
    - **Officers Directory:** Roster and contact phone numbers.
