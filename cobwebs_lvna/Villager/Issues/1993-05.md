    Letter from the President
    
    The May meeting of the L.V.N.A. will be a celebration of

    Neighborhood Watch
    
    One Resident's Perspective
    
    My message to you all as fellow Lakewood residents is that
    we must continue in our endeavors to keep our neighborhood
    drug free and crime free, Drugs and crime in our neighborhood
    affect us economically, socially, and politically. They ruin lives,
    and they don't discriminate in who they hurt or destroy in their
    path. The trickle effect of these 'monsters' has enormous impacts
    on all of us.
    
    We need to be more responsive in immediately reporting
    any suspicious activities to Public Safety (See phone numbers on
    back page). Call anonymously if you'd like; it's not necessary to
    give your name.
    
    Don't be intimidated by people in your neighborhood who
    have been acting uncaringly and carelessly for long periods of
    time. We as a community must not close our eyes and get used
    to undesirable situations because we don't think it matters, or we
    feel that the police won't respond in an adequate manner.
    
    As a resident of Lakewood and Sunnyvale, I strongly urge
    you not to hesitate in reporting any activities in your neighbor-
    hood which may ultimately affect the well-being of our commu-
    nity as a whole, Don't be afraid. The next victim may be you, or
    one of your loved ones.
    
    Let's continue to grow and make Lakewood one of the nic-
    est places to live and raise a family. You can bring about a
    change. You do matter. Your voice counts. Please help us all!
    
    “To communicate and educate" continues to be
    the goal of the L.V.N.A. This year we will
    
    focus our efforts on strengthening our sense
    of community and expanding youth involvement
    in our neighborhood.
    
    You can help by joining our talent bank. Help
    out in your own way. Organize a project, bring
    a friend or a refreshment to the monthly
    meeting, walk an issue of the newsletter,
    bring in a paid advertisement, draw an event
    flyer, etc. We need your help to continue to
    make great things happen in our community.
    
    Last year, over 100 active volunteers
    discovered that they can make a difference.
    Their commitment helped to make Lakewood
    Villiage a distinguished neighborhood not only
    for all of us, but also for the City of
    Sunnyvale. We will recognize these valued
    volunteers at our May meeting. I invite you to
    come meet them and show your appreciation.
    
    Please also join me in welcoming volunteers
    Sara Chun, Debbie Hall, and Lea Schafer. Sara
    has volunteered to coordinate Summer Fun Day,
    Debbie will initiate the Welcome Wagon, and
    
    Lea is coordinating our Graffiti Team.
    
    Jessica Lee, L.V.N.A. President

    Announcements
    
    Don't forget to take advantage of spring clean-up month in the
    City of Sunnyvale. On every regular garbage pick-up day in
    May, you can leave out most large and unusual garbage and Spe-
    cialty Solid Waste will remove it.
    
    Get ready for the second annual Dumpster Day on June 5.
    Dumpsters will be placed at about 16 locations around Lake-
    wood Village for use by residents (no contractors, please). The
    locations will primarily be the same as last year. Use the dump-
    sters for all garbage except paint, oil, hazardous materials, and
    auto parts, including batteries. Also, make sure branches are
    small enough to fit easily in the dumpsters. Please do not overfill
    the dumpsters, and do not leave garbage on the outside.
    
    The L.V.N.A. is still compiling a community talent bank.
    Please contact the board if you have any talent you can share.
    We currently need talent in the following areas: photographers,
    Lakewood historian, refreshments, welcome wagon, newsletter
    walkers, graffiti paint-out, youth activities, community garden,
    teen mural, holiday parade, holiday lighting contest, entertain-
    ment, and translation to Vietnamese, Spanish, Filipino, etc.
    
    The Sunnyvale School Board is looking for a parent from the
    north side of Sunnyvale to serve on the School Attendance Re-
    view Board. This board meets once a month. For more informa-
    tion or to volunteer, contact Peggy Quillinan at 736-7332.
    
    There have been reports in the neighborhood of sparks and
    fires where dead branches come in contact with P.G.& E. power
    lines. Please keep trees trimmed of dead branches where they
    touch power lines. For more information, contact P.G.& E.
    
    Newsletter Contributions
    
    All information in the Villager is contributed by residents of
    Lakewood. If you have an article, announcement, or request for
    information which you would like published, contact newsletter
    editor Linda Cheung at 734-1661.
    
    The deadline for most months! newsletters is the first day of
    the month. Distribution is on the weekend before the third Thurs-
    day of the month. We will attempt to to print all submissions as
    space permits. Also feel free to call the editor if you have any
    questions or concerns about the newsletter.
