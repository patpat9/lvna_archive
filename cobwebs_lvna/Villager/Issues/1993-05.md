Neighborhood Watch One Resident's Perspective My message to you all as fellow Lakewood residents is that we must continue in our endeavors to keep our neighborhood drug free and crime free. Drugs and crime in our neighborhood affect us economically, socially, and politically. They ruin lives, and they don't discriminate in who they hurt or destroy in their path, The trickle effect of these 'monsters' has enormous impacts on all of us. We need to be more responsive in immediately reporting any suspicious activities to Public Safety (See phone numbers on back page), Call anonymously if you'd like; it's not necessary to give your name. Don't be intimidated by people in your neighborhood who have been acting uncaringly and carelessly for long periods Of time. We as a community must not close our eyes and get used to undesirable situations because we don't think it matters, or we feel that the police won't respond in an adequate manner. As a resident of Lakewood and Sunnyvale, I strongly urge you not to hesitate in reporting any activities in your neighbor- hood which may ultimately affect the well-being of our commu- nity as a whole. Don't be afraid. The next victim may be you, or one of your loved ones. Let's continue to grow and make Lakewood one of the nic- est places to live and raise a family. You can bring about a change.     Announcements Don't forget to take advantage of spring clean-up month in the City of Sunnyvale. On every regular garbage pick-up day in May, you can leave out most large and unusual garbage and Spe- cialty Solid Waste will remove it. Get ready for the second annual Dumpster Day on June 5. Dumpsters will be placed at about 16 locations around Lake- wood Village for use by residents (no contractors, please). The locations will primarily be the same as last year. Use the dump- sters for all garbage except paint, oil, hazardous materials, and auto parts, including batteries. Also, make sure branches are small enough to fit easily in the dumpsters. Please do not overfill the dumpsters, and do not leave garbage on the outside. The L.V.N.A. is still compiling a community talent bank. Please contact the board if you have any talent you can share.
