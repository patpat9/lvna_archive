    Fililpino-Americans Donate Bus Shelter
    
    The Filipino-American Association of Lakewood Village,
    Inc. is donating a bus shelter to the City of Sunnyvale. The bus
    shelter, now under construction on Lakehaven near Lawrence, is
    a community project by the Filipino-American Association. The
    project was conceived and approved by the Filipino-American
    Association in 1984, but did not secure the approval of the City
    until recently.
    
    The bus shelter has become a reality under the leadership of
    past president Felix Arbolante, one of the founders of the Filipi-
    no-American Association of Lakewood Village, Inc. The project
    is also indebted to the City of Sunnyvale officials, especially
    Mayor Pat Castillo, Vice Mayor Robin Parker, Councilman Stan
    Kawezynski, and other city officials like Joe Avila of the city en-
    Bineers office.
    
    The Filipino-American Association of Lakewood Village,
    Ine. is conunilted to community service and the improvement of
    the quality of life in Lakewood Village, and it will continue to
    strive to attain its community goals.
    
    The date for the official dedication and tumover of the bus
