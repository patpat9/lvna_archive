# The Villager: Historical Policies, Guidelines & Advertising

## About the Publication

*The Villager* has been published and delivered door-to-door to Lakewood Village residents since May 1958. Founded originally by the **Lakewood Village Residents Association (LVRA)** and continued by the **Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association (LVNA)**, it is one of the longest continually running neighborhood publications in Santa Clara County.

### Volunteer Distribution Network

The newsletter was circulated free of charge every month to over **1,650 households**, local Lakewood Village merchants, and Sunnyvale City Hall officials. The entire distribution was carried out by volunteer neighborhood "walkers", each responsible for delivering printed copies to one or two neighborhood streets on the weekend prior to the monthly general meeting.

## Editorial Principles & Policies

*The LVNA Villager* operated under five foundational principles:

1. **Purpose:** The purpose of *The Villager* was twofold: (a) to inform the residents of Lakewood Village of news, civic developments, and community events of relevance to the neighborhood, and (b) to provide a constructive forum for residents to express their views in written form.
2. **Fairness:** *The Villager* did not restrain publication of the views of individuals or groups solely on the basis of disagreement with those views.
3. **Respect:** *The Villager* did not publish material that attacked an individual or group.
4. **Propriety:** *The Villager* did not publish material containing language commonly viewed as vulgar or offensive, maintaining family-appropriate standards for all households.
5. **Editorial Prerogative:** Final decisions regarding what material was published and how it was edited were at the sole discretion and judgment of the volunteer Editor.

## Contributing to *The Villager* (Historical Guidelines)

Historically, *The Villager* relied on submissions from neighbors, local merchants, school representatives, and Sunnyvale city officials to keep the newsletter dynamic and reflective of the entire neighborhood.

### Welcomed Community Submissions

- **Letters to the Editor & Board:** Resident commentary on traffic calming, street lighting, park maintenance, code compliance, and Sunnyvale City Council actions.
- **Neighborhood History & Memoirs:** Personal recollections and vintage photographs of Lakewood Village during its 1950s orchard-era construction and suburban boom.
- **Event Recaps & Photographs:** Reports from the annual Lakewood Park Summer Picnic, Holiday Parade, Christmas Lighting Contest, and neighborhood Dumpster Days.
- **Youth & Club News:** Announcements from Lakewood and Fairwood Elementary schools, Columbia Middle School, Lakewood Little League, and local Boy Scout and Girl Scout troops.

### Historical Submission Standards

- **Concise Length:** Because physical print space was strictly limited by advertising revenue (typically 6 to 14 pages), articles were kept focused and concise.
- **Submission Deadlines:** Articles were submitted roughly 10 days before the monthly distribution weekend to allow for typesetting, layout, proofreading, and offset printing.
- **Non-Partisan Policy:** Standard association guidelines prohibited partisan political endorsements while welcoming objective, equal-length candidate statements prior to Sunnyvale municipal elections.

## Historical Advertising Guidelines & Rates

Commercial advertising was the financial backbone of *The Villager*, covering 100% of printing and paper costs so that the publication could remain completely free to all 1,650+ neighborhood homes.

### Print Advertising Rate Card (2000s)

| Ad Format | Dimensions | Monthly Rate |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Column Inch** | 3.5" wide × 1.0" high | $12 / month |
| **Business Card** | 3.5" wide × 2.0" high | $24 / month |
| **Double Business Card** | 3.5" wide × 4.0" high | $48 / month |
| **Half Column** | 3.5" wide × 5.0" high | $60 / month |
| **Half Page** | 7.5" wide × 5.0" high | $125 / month |
| **Full Page** | 7.5" wide × 10.0" high | $200 / month |
| **Full Page Insert** | 8.5" × 11.0" (1,700 copies provided by advertiser) | $100 / month |

### Advertising Policies

- **Prepayment Discount:** A 10% discount was offered for advertisers who prepaid for three or more consecutive months.
- **Format:** Advertisers supplied scanner-ready paper copy or electronic digital formats.
- **Ad Placement:** Placement within the newsletter was at the sole editorial discretion of *The Villager* staff.
- **Web Archive Non-Commercial Policy:** Unlike the historical print edition, this online digital archive is strictly non-commercial and does not accept or display paid advertising.

## Archival Preservation & Missing Issues

Between 2002 and 2008, LVNA webmaster Brian Smithson led a major community effort to locate, scan, and preserve original copies of *The Villager* and *LVRA Villager* from neighborhood attics, filing cabinets, and past board members. That collection forms the backbone of the digital archive presented on this website today.
