# About LVNA.NET & Digital Archive Colophon

!!! note "Archive Colophon & Webmaster History"
    This page preserves the original background, purpose, acknowledgments, and technical disclaimers for **LVNA.NET**, which served as the official digital home of the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association from 2002 to 2008, created and maintained on a volunteer basis by resident webmaster Brian Smithson and preserved in the [Cobwebs: LVNA](https://www.grot.com/Cobwebs/LVNA/index.html) archive.
    
    The LVNA continues actively serving the neighborhood today. For current association leadership, neighborhood activities, and modern issues of *The Villager*, visit the official website: **[https://lvnasv.org](https://lvnasv.org)**.

[🗺️ **Civic Directory & Community Reference Guide**](../../Resources/resources.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

### Audience & Organization

When originally launched in 2002, LVNA.NET was organized around three resident audiences:

* **Prospective Residents:** Those considering moving to Lakewood Village could explore the [Historical Community Guide](../../LV/lv.md) to learn about parks, schools, commerce, and transit corridors, alongside historical [Home Sale Prices](../../LV/lv.md#historical-housing-and-real-estate-snapshot-2002-2008).
* **New Residents:** Neighbors new to the area could review the [Historical Community Guide](../../LV/lv.md), read [The Villager](../../Villager/villager.md) in the [Complete Issues Archive](../../Villager/Issues/index.md), learn about [The LVNA Governance](../../LVNA/lvna.md) and [Activities & Events](../../Activities/activities.md), and access the [Community Directory & Maps](../../Resources/resources.md).
* **Longtime Residents:** Established neighbors could explore the 50-year [Neighborhood History](../../LV/History/history.md), examine [Cartography & Environmental Maps](../../LV/lv.md#environmental-and-geologic-setting), and view the [Local History Bibliography](../../LV/History/history.md#local-history-bibliography-and-references).
