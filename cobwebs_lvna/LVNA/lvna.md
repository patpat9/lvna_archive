## Organizational Structure and Historic Roster

The Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association operates as an entirely volunteer civic body representing the interests of residents in northern Sunnyvale, California. 

During the period captured in this archive, the association was led by five elected executive officers and several appointed volunteer coordinators. Elections were held annually by secret ballot at general membership meetings in March.

## Meeting Framework

Historically, the LVNA conducted three distinct types of meetings to manage projects, review city policies, and engage tract residents:

### 1. General Meetings
- **Audience:** Open to all Lakewood Village homeowners, tenants, and invited guests.
- **Schedule:** Held on the second Wednesday of each month at 7:00 PM.
- **Locations:** Rotated between the Lakewood Park Building, Lakewood Elementary Multi-Purpose Room, and Fairwood Elementary Multi-Purpose Room.
- **Agenda:** Featured presentations from Sunnyvale Public Safety officers, local school district trustees, City Council members, and community updates, followed by open public question-and-answer periods.

### 2. Board Meetings
- **Audience:** Elected executive officers and key appointed coordinators.
- **Schedule:** Held monthly at least fourteen days prior to each general meeting.
- **Agenda:** Reviewing association budgets, approving event permits with the City of Sunnyvale, setting upcoming general meeting agendas, and managing newsletter production schedules.

### 3. Special Meetings
- **Purpose:** Convened when urgent neighborhood issues arose requiring immediate collective action, such as proposed municipal zoning amendments, airport flight path noise disputes, or school attendance boundary realignments.
- **Notice:** Required seven days advance written notice delivered to all tract households.

## Volunteer Operations and Delivery Network

The LVNA was sustained entirely through the volunteer efforts of neighbors living on every street:

- **Self-Sustaining Funding:** The association collected no mandatory dues. Operating budgets, event costs, and the commercial printing of *The Villager* were funded 100% through advertising purchased by local businesses.
- **The Volunteer Walker Network:** Each month, over thirty volunteer delivery walkers distributed bundles of *The Villager* to individual doorsteps across all 1,600+ homes, ensuring full neighborhood communication without postal costs.
- **Community Event Committees:** Volunteer teams organized and managed the annual Lakewood Park Summer Picnic, Holiday Parade traffic and safety, Holiday Lighting judging panels, and citywide Dumpster Days cleanups.

    <div id="article-i-name" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE I. NAME</strong></div>

    The name of the organization shall be **Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association** ("LVNA").

    ---

    <div id="article-ii-purpose" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE II. PURPOSE</strong></div>

    The purpose of the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association is to:

    1. Enhance the quality of life through education programs, such as the newsletter, SNAP, etc.
    2. Promote a sense of safety and peace of mind through positive neighborhood awareness programs.
    3. Maintain and increase the value of our investment by projecting to others our sense of purpose, peace, and prosperity.
    4. Maintain and promote partnerships with other city and county government, local businesses, and other neighborhood programs.
    5. Foster community unity.

    ---

    <div id="article-iii-membership" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE III. MEMBERSHIP</strong></div>

    Membership in the Lakewood Village Neighborhood Association shall be open to all residents and/or owners of homes on lots zoned R0, R1, and R2 (single-family and duplex-type homes) within the boundaries of Highway 101 to the South, Calabazas Creek to the East, Tasman Drive to the North, and Fair Oaks Avenue to the West.

    ---

    <div id="article-iv-voting" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE IV. VOTING</strong></div>

    All residents in the area defined in Article III above age 18 years and older shall have one (1) vote to cast for each office subject to election or such other matter that may from time to time be considered for vote of the Membership.

    ---

    <div id="article-v-funding" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE V. FUNDING</strong></div>

    Funding shall be derived from sources other than dues or Membership fees.

    ---

    <div id="article-vi-meetings" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE VI. MEETINGS</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Public Meetings</strong></div>
    Open public meetings will be held generally on the 2nd Wednesday of each month unless otherwise detailed in the newsletter. The purpose of this meeting will be to conduct LVNA's business, voting, and such other matters as may be presented to the assembly.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Board Meetings</strong></div>
    Board Meetings shall be held regularly as scheduled and announced to the general Membership.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: Special Meetings</strong></div>
    A Special Meeting of the Membership may be called by any Member eligible to vote as deemed necessary. Notification and purpose of the Special Meeting shall require seven (7) days advance written notice to all LVNA Members.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 4: Agenda</strong></div>
    The President shall prepare the Agenda for General Meetings of the Membership. Any Member of LVNA may make a motion to add an item to the Board or General Agendas at those respective Meetings for consideration at the next like Meeting. Adoption of that motion requires a second and majority vote.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 5: Quorum</strong></div>
    Unless otherwise specified in these bylaws, a Quorum for any General or Special Meeting of LVNA shall be the number of Members in attendance. Decisions of LVNA shall be made by a majority vote of those present. A majority of the Board shall also be required to be present at these Meetings.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 6: Participation</strong></div>
    Any General, Special, Board, or Committee Meeting is open to any person and all who wish to be heard. However, only those eligible for Membership are entitled to vote. All actions or recommendations of the General or Special Meetings shall be communicated to all LVNA Members.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 7: Attendance Records</strong></div>
    A sign-up sheet will be provided at every Board, General, and Special Meeting and these records shall be kept and maintained by the Secretary of LVNA.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 8: Procedures</strong></div>
    The LVNA shall follow Robert's Rules of Order (Revised) in all areas not covered by the bylaws.

    ---

    <div id="article-vii-board-of-directors" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE VII. BOARD OF DIRECTORS</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Composition of the Board</strong></div>
    The Board of Directors, hereinafter referred to as the Board, shall be composed of five (5) members: President, 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Duties of the Board</strong></div>
    1. **Management:** The affairs of the LVNA shall be managed by the Board. The Board shall be accountable to the Membership; shall seek the views of those affected by any proposed policies or actions before adopting any recommendations on behalf of LVNA; and shall strictly comply with these bylaws.
    2. **Vacancies:** The Board may fill any vacancy on the Board or Committee by a majority vote of the Board. This must be ratified by the Membership at the next General Meeting. The appointee shall serve the remainder of the unexpired term.
    3. In cases where a Board or Committee Member has missed three (3) Meetings, the Board is required to consider replacement of that Member. Members to be replaced shall be notified in writing by the Board of Directors.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: Board Meetings</strong></div>
    The Board shall meet at least fourteen (14) days prior to any General or Special Meeting or at any other time the President or Board designates. These Meetings shall be open sessions; however, only Board Members may vote. A Quorum for Board Meetings shall be three (3) Members, and decisions shall be made by majority vote.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 4: Terms of Office</strong></div>
    1. All Members of the Board of Directors shall be elected for a one (1) year term.
    2. The term for all elected Board Members shall officially begin on the second Wednesday of March of the year in which they are elected and continue through until the second Wednesday of the next year.
    3. The term of any one elected Board Member shall be limited to three (3) consecutive terms in any Office.

    ---

    <div id="article-viii-duties-of-the-officers" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE VIII. DUTIES OF THE OFFICERS</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: President</strong></div>
    1. The President shall be an Ambassador for the LVNA.
    2. The President shall prepare the Agenda and preside at all Meetings of the Board and Membership.
    3. The President shall chair the Budget Committee, which will review, maintain, and forecast future expenditures and income for the Association and report back to the Board Members their findings and/or proposals for the year.
    4. The President shall sign all reports, documents, and correspondence that may require his/her signature. If the President is unavailable, the 1st Vice President, 2nd Vice President, Secretary, or Treasurer can sign for the President, in the order listed.
    5. The President shall appoint an Audit Committee of at least three (3) Members to audit the financial records of the LVNA. The appointments shall be ratified by a majority of the Members.
    6. The President shall vote only to break a tie vote of the Board and Membership.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: 1st Vice President</strong></div>
    1. The 1st Vice President shall be an Ambassador for LVNA.
    2. In the absence of the President or the vacancy of the office of President, the 1st Vice President shall assume the role of the President and serve the remainder of the President's term upon a majority vote of the Board and ratified by the Membership.
    3. The 1st Vice President shall chair the Publicity Committee, which includes overseeing the Newsletter Committee. In addition, the 1st Vice President will be responsible for preparing written or verbal information regarding the Association, such as press releases and flyers.
    4. The 1st Vice President shall preside over all Meetings that the President cannot attend.
    5. The 1st Vice President shall be responsible for keeping attendance records of the Board Members.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: 2nd Vice President</strong></div>
    1. The 2nd Vice President shall be an Ambassador for LVNA.
    2. In the absence of both the President and 1st Vice President, the 2nd Vice President shall assume the role of President.
    3. The 2nd Vice President shall serve as Parliamentarian.
    4. The 2nd Vice President shall chair the Grievance Committee.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 4: Secretary</strong></div>
    1. The Secretary shall be an Ambassador for LVNA.
    2. The Secretary will be responsible for posting Meeting dates for the year and/or any schedule modifications.
    3. The Secretary shall take minutes at every Board, General, and Special Meeting.
    4. The Secretary shall maintain these minutes and related documentation, including attendance sign-in sheets, in chronological order.
    5. The Secretary shall provide the Board with previous minutes for adoption at the beginning of each Meeting.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 5: Treasurer</strong></div>
    1. The Treasurer shall be an Ambassador for LVNA.
    2. The Treasurer shall keep true and accurate records of all financial transactions on behalf of the LVNA.
    3. The Treasurer shall deliver an oral report of the financial status of the Association at each regular Board, General, and Special Meeting.
    4. The Treasurer shall prepare and distribute to the Board a written financial report semiannually at the end of March and August.
    5. The Treasurer shall receive, endorse, and deposit all funds into the LVNA account.
    6. The Treasurer shall prepare and cosign with the President all authorized LVNA expenditures.
    7. The Treasurer shall manage bookkeeping for each disbursement of LVNA funds.
    8. The Treasurer shall allow Members to examine the financial records of the LVNA upon written request at any Board Meeting.
    9. The Treasurer shall submit all records and inventory to the authorized Audit Committee upon request.
    10. At the expiration of the term, the Treasurer shall deliver all Association books, accounts, inventory, and property to their successor.

    ---

    <div id="article-ix-committees" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE IX. COMMITTEES</strong></div>

    The following Standing Committees shall be instituted to manage programs and activities of a long-standing nature, serving one (1) year terms:

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Budget Committee</strong></div>
    Chaired by the President, consisting of three (3) additional members appointed by the President and ratified by the Board. Prepares an annual budget outlining income and expenditures by the second Wednesday of March for Board approval on or before April 30.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Audit Committee</strong></div>
    Composed of three (3) members appointed by the President with Board ratification. Audits financial records annually following elections in April, and conducts an audit two weeks prior to outgoing Board members leaving office.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: Publicity Committee</strong></div>
    Chaired by the 1st Vice President, composed of three (3) members including the Newsletter Editor. Manages community outreach, flyers, and press releases.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 4: Social and Recreation Committee</strong></div>
    Appointed by the President with Board approval to coordinate community events (such as the Annual Picnic and Holiday events), determine chairpersons, and recruit event volunteers.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 5: Grievance Committee</strong></div>
    Chaired by the 2nd Vice President and composed of three (3) members to resolve resident complaints regarding association decisions or policies.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 6: Ad Hoc Committees</strong></div>
    Instituted by the President with Board consent for specific defined tasks, disbanding immediately upon completion of their goals.

    ---

    <div id="article-x-elections" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE X. ELECTIONS</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Eligibility</strong></div>
    Only persons eligible for LVNA Membership shall be qualified to hold an elected or appointed position.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Nominations & Election Timeline</strong></div>
    Members of the Board shall be elected for a one (1) year term. Nominations are taken at the February General Meeting. Elections are held by secret ballot at a Special Meeting in March, with ballots counted immediately following the vote in view of attending members.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: Absentee Ballots</strong></div>
    Members unable to attend the election meeting may vote by absentee ballot during the week prior to the election date at designated times and locations.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 4: Majority Vote</strong></div>
    Board Members shall be elected by a combined majority vote of absentee ballots and members present at the election meeting.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 5: Recall of Board Members</strong></div>
    Any holder of an elected office may be removed for conduct detrimental to the LVNA by a Quorum of at least twenty-five (25) Members and a two-thirds (2/3) vote at a General or Special Meeting, with fifteen (15) days prior written notice.

    ---

    <div id="article-xi-grievance-procedure" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE XI. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Filing a Complaint</strong></div>
    A person or group adversely affected by a decision or policy of LVNA may submit a written complaint to any Member of the Grievance Committee.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Review Timeline</strong></div>
    Within seven (7) days of receipt, the Committee arranges an interview with the petitioner and recommends a resolution in writing to the Board within thirty (30) days.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 3: Final Resolution</strong></div>
    The Committee submits a report of recommendations to the complainant, Board, and Membership. If agreement cannot be reached, final resolution is determined by majority vote of the Membership at a General or Special Meeting.

    ---

    <div id="article-xii-adoption-and-amendment-of-bylaws" class="bylaw-article-header"><strong>ARTICLE XII. ADOPTION AND AMENDMENT OF BYLAWS</strong></div>

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 1: Adoption</strong></div>
    Adoption of these bylaws shall require a Quorum of at least twenty-five (25) Members, with a two-thirds (2/3) vote of Members present at a General or Special Meeting.

    <div class="bylaw-section-header"><strong>Section 2: Amendments</strong></div>
    Amendments may be proposed at any General or Special Meeting after Members have received at least seven (7) days advance written notice. Adoption of amendments requires a Quorum of twenty-five (25) Members and a two-thirds (2/3) vote.
