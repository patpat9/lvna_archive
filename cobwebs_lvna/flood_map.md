## Technical Methodology

### 1. Mathematical Model: Raster Algebra
The predicted 100-year flood depth is calculated by cell-by-cell raster subtraction:

$$\text{Water Depth} = R_{\text{BFE}} - R_{\text{DEM}}$$

Where:
- **$R_{\text{DEM}}$**: Bare-Earth ground elevation derived from 1-meter USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) airborne LiDAR (NAVD88 feet).
- **$R_{\text{BFE}}$**: Water surface elevation surface generated by interpolating FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL) Base Flood Elevation contours and static Zone AE/AH ponding elevations.

### 2. Risk Classification Tiers
- **$\le 0\text{ ft}$ (Dry Land / Low Risk)**: Ground elevation exceeds the 100-year flood level ($R_{\text{DEM}} \ge R_{\text{BFE}}$). Candidate for FEMA Letter of Map Amendment (LOMA).
- **$0 \sim 1\text{ ft}$ (Shallow Flooding Risk)**: Low-depth sheet flow and curb overtopping.
- **$1 \sim 3\text{ ft}$ (Moderate Flooding Risk)**: Ground floor and crawlspace inundation hazard.
- **$> 3\text{ ft}$ (Deep Water High Risk)**: High velocity, structural danger, and vehicle floatation hazard.
- **Zone X (Shaded)**: 500-year (0.2% annual chance) floodplain and levee-protected areas.
- **Zone X (Unshaded)**: Areas of minimal flood hazard outside the 500-year floodplain.

### 3. Why Static Snapshot Outperforms Real-Time APIs
- **Zero API Bottlenecks**: Avoids tens of thousands of live HTTP requests to federal REST servers.
- **Instant Client-Side Querying**: Decodes ground elevation, BFE, and depth in $< 0.1\text{ ms}$ from a high-precision binary buffer (`flood_lookup_data.bin`).
- **Zero Hosting Cost**: Hosted statically on GitHub Pages with under 3.5 MB total asset size.
