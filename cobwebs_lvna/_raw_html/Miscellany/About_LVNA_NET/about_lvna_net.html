Who is this site for?

If you are...

  • ...someone who is considering moving to Lakewood Village and would like to know more about the neighborhood, check out the general description and location pages, as well as the pages about transportation, schools, parks, and shopping and entertainment. And, of course, check out the recent home sale prices.

     
  • ...a new resident who wants to learn all about your new neighborhood, look at transportation, schools, parks, and shopping and entertainment, read current and recent issues of The LVNA Villager newsletter, find out about the LVNA and neighborhood activities, and discover resources like the community calendar, phone directory, and links.

     
  • ...an existing resident who wants to know more about your neighborhood, you’ll be interested in the history pages, links,  maps and views, and the reading list. Share this web site address with distant relatives and friends so they can see where you live!

I hope you enjoy the web site and find it useful and informative. If you have any comments, send them to the LVNA.NET webmaster.

Viewing Requirements

Browsers

This web site is designed for use with the following web browsers and versions:

  • Netscape Navigator 4.X or higher
  • Microsoft Internet Explorer 4.X or greater
  • Opera 5.X or greater
  • Any version of Firefox

Size

The site is designed for at least 800x600 pixel displays, but should work (with occasional horizontal scrolling on some pages) on a 640x480 pixel display.

Colors

The site will look best if viewed with at least a 16-bit color palatte, also known as “Hi Color” or 65,336 colors. However, it should be viewable with an 8-bit color palatte or 256 colors.

Platforms

The site should be viewable on Microsoft Windows, Macintosh, Linux, UNIX, etc. No platform-specific coding has been used.

Accessibility

The site has not been designed in accordance with ADA Section 508 guidelines for accessibility.

Searching

This site’s internal search engine is no longer available. Use a search engine like Google and limit your search to the domain “www.lvna.net”.
