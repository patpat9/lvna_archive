Contributing to The LVNA Villager

The LVNA Villager welcomes contributions of content from residents, merchants, City officials, and just about anyone else who has the desire to write something that is relevant to Lakewood Village and its residents. Specific areas of contribution include (but are not limited to):

  • Letters to the LVNA
  • Articles
  • Reports and photographs of Lakewood Village events

Because of space limitations, contributions should be as brief and concise as possible. The Editor reserves the right to reject or edit all contributions. There are other editorial policies that you should review before submitting a contribution. If you are interested in submitting content to The LVNA Villager or have questions about it, please contact the Editor.

Contributing to the LVNA Web site

The LVNA Web site also welcomes contributions of content from its readers. Feedback and suggestions are especially useful, but the Webmaster will also consider direct contribution of content to the site. If you are interested, please contact the Webmaster.
