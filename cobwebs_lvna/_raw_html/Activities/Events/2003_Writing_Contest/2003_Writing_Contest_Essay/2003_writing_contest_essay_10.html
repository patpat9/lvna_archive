Name:

Monica J.

Award:

3rd Prize

Category:

6th-8th

Essay:

If I was in charge of Lakewood Village, hardly anything would change because our village is so great. Almost everyone wants their yards to look pretty. To run the parks I would keep it as it is because people can go there anytime they want and spend as much time as they want there. One thing we can all do is go around and ask neighbors to please pick up trash around their house so that our village stays clean. I like that Fairwood School and Lakewood School play basketball games against each other now. It’s fun and I wish we played more. Lakewood Village is such a great place to live because everyone lives close to each other and we are all like family, helping one another, saying hello everyday, and things like that. I hope that in the future Lakewood Village will stay as nice as it is.
