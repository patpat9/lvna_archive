Through the generous contributions of several Lakewood Village residents, the LVNA and The Villager held a writing contest for Lakewood Village children and young adults. . The contest was open to Lakewood Village residents from Kindergarten to the 12th grade.

The topic to write about for the contest was:

“If I was in charge of Lakewood Village…”

The contest ended on July 11th, 2003, and the winners are listed below. Click on each winning entry to see its essay.
