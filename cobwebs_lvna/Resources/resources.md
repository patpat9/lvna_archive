## Local and Regional Web Links

A curated directory of local civic, educational, informational, and news links serving Lakewood Village residents:

### Government and Civic Resources

- **City of Sunnyvale:** [sunnyvale.ca.gov](https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov) - Official municipal government portal, city council agendas, recreation programs, and public safety.
- **Sunnyvale Municipal Code:** [Sunnyvale Municipal Code](https://www.sunnyvale.ca.gov) - Local ordinances, zoning standards, and neighborhood preservation guidelines.
- **County of Santa Clara:** [sccgov.org](https://www.sccgov.org) - County public health, property tax assessor, and social services.
- **State of California:** [ca.gov](https://www.ca.gov) - State government services, DMV, and California legislative records.
- **Bay Area Adapting to Rising Tides (ART):** [explorer.adaptingtorisingtides.org](https://explorer.adaptingtorisingtides.org/explorer) - Regional sea level rise, coastal flooding, and shoreline vulnerability mapping tool covering San Francisco Bay and the South Bay.

### Schools and Educational Institutions

- **Lakewood Elementary School:** Sunnyvale School District campus located at 750 Lakechime Drive, serving neighborhood students west of Lawrence Expressway.
- **Fairwood Elementary School:** Sunnyvale School District campus located at 1110 Fairwood Avenue, serving neighborhood students east of Lawrence Expressway.
- **Columbia Middle School:** Sunnyvale School District middle school serving 6th through 8th grades.
- **Fremont High School:** Fremont Union High School District campus in Sunnyvale.
- **Colleges & Universities:** Mission College (Santa Clara), Cogswell Polytechnical College, and Santa Clara University.

### Regional Libraries and Cultural Institutions

- **Sunnyvale Public Library:** Located at 665 W. Olive Avenue in the Sunnyvale Civic Center, housing extensive reference materials and the California Collection local history room.
- **Sunnyvale Heritage Park Museum:** Dedicated to preserving the history of Sunnyvale and the agricultural Santa Clara Valley ("Valley of Heart's Delight").

### Local News & Media

- **The Sunnyvale Sun:** Local weekly newspaper covering Sunnyvale municipal affairs and neighborhood events.
- **San Jose Mercury News:** Major metropolitan daily newspaper of Silicon Valley and the South Bay.
- **Metro Silicon Valley:** Weekly alternative arts, culture, and dining journal.
